# The initial Christmas-tree row is referenced by the wrong equation number

Volume 4A, first edition, first printing (2011).
Answer 7.2.1.6–75, first sentence, printed 799.

The answer says that the relevant row is the bottom descendant of the first row in (33). Equation (33), on page 456, depicts the two ways to insert a new leaf in Remy's construction and has no Christmas-tree rows.

The intended reference is (35), on page 457: its first row is `10`. After n-2 applications of the chain growth rule (36), this initial singleton has M_(n-2) descendants, and its bottom descendant is the first row of length n-1. Thus the argument and formula are correct after replacing (33) by (35).

# Deleting a 1 lowers the weight by one

Volume 4A, first edition, first printing (2011), Answer 7.2.1.3–104, Notes, first sentence,
printed page 745, file page 762.

The 1-shadow in this note is the set obtained by deleting one 1 bit from a string. The weight is the number of 1 bits. Consequently the 1-shadow of a family of weight-t strings consists of strings of weight t-1.

The printed sentence correctly starts with strings of weight t, but then says their 1-shadow consists of the first mu_t(N) strings of weight t. The second occurrence must be **t-1**. For example, the first weight-2 string of length three is 011, and its 1-shadow is {01}, of weight 1. The count mu_2(1)=1 is unaffected.

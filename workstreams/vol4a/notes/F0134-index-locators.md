# Two index entries point one page too early

Volume 4A, first edition, first printing (2011), Index and Glossary,
printed pages 845–846.

On page 845, the `de Bruijn, ... cycles` subentry includes pages 625 and
657. These should be **626 and 658**. The occurrences are in answers
7.1.4–44 and 7.1.4–185, respectively. The duplicate `Bruijn, ... cycles`
entry on page 840 already gives the correct locators.

On page 846, `Differential quantification` includes `633–634, 636`.
These should be **634–635, 637**. The differential quantifier occurs in
answers 7.1.4–89(b), 90, 92, 96, and 105(a) on those pages. The entry
under the quantifier symbol on page 845 already gives the correct
locators; the Boolean-difference entry also agrees.

Only these locators need changing. The mathematics at the destinations
is unaffected. The original index and destination pages were inspected;
this is not an OCR page-offset discrepancy. Historical and current 4A
errata and targeted public searches disclosed no matching correction.
This does not establish absolute novelty.

Evidence: `../sources/index-final-source-check.json` and the original
page images listed there.

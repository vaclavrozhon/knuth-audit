The last paragraph of answer 91 is already corrected by the 2 April 2014
erratum. The printed map g(k)/2 is not the asserted bijection: k=011011
has even runs but its image has adjacent ones. The replacement k ->
g(2k), from n-bit strings without adjacent ones to (n+1)-bit strings
without odd runs of ones, fixes both the direction and the length.

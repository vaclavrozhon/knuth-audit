# Volume 4A: perfect schemes and multiset combinations

Main 369–372 before Shadows, exercises 7.2.1.3–49–67 and answers
734–737 through 67 were read together. No new finding; no mathematical
code ran. Current/historical 4A and Fascicle 3 errata were screened.

The inversion-parity obstruction in Theorem P follows from the
root-of-unity binomial evaluation in 49. Pairing the vanishing factors
and the residue factors gives that formula and its multinomial extension
in 50. The converse constructions cited for perfect schemes, multiset
permutations and end-around swaps are accepted as published results.

56 were included, rather than treated as new findings.

Algorithm W's suffix-rotation recursion, j/k invariants, carry and
termination were checked. Its original initialization includes the
sentinel a_n=0. The packed MMIX transformations agree with the same
successor; the known 2022 restriction on its SET initialization was
screened. The piano-chord blocks in 57 join by one finger move; removing
fixed gaps gives 58. The new-maximum term of the generating function in
59 has exponent tn-(t-1)m; at z=-1 it gives the stated parity obstruction.

The 37 bounded selections in the main example split into 14,13,10
according to the number of white balls. Algorithm Q maintains the
unallocated sum and resets a full low prefix correctly; its two fast
cases agree with the displayed successors. The reflected bounded
composition recursion in 61 has two-part joins. In 62, row/column
greedy maxima are the same interval-intersection matrix, while reversed
column intervals give the minima; row recursion generates all tables.
The local four-entry move in 63 preserves both margins. The full
Hamiltonian construction implicit in its proof sketch is excepted.

For 64 the two mutually recursive subcube lists have the stated
endpoints; each join is an allowed star/digit swap or one-bit flip,
and the A list closes into a cycle. The enumeration framework in 65
was examined without rerunning its large counts. The basis recursion

odd total excludes a nontrivial cycle. The proposed stronger Ising

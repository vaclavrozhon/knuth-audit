# V3-C0067 — The Patricia path-length recurrence refers to the wrong equation

Confirmed cross-reference error in volume 3, second edition, first
printing (1998), printed p.506, immediately after equation (16)
(file p.517).

Equation (16) has the unknown h'_n(1) on the left and a binomial sum
of h'_k(1) on the right with coefficients depending on n. This has
the same-index form of equation (5), with a different additive term.
The text instead refers to equation (6), whose left-hand side has
index N+1 while its binomial coefficients have index N.

The distinction is substantive to the method: p.502 explicitly explains
why this index shift changes the recurrence analysis. Replace the
reference to (6) by (5). Exercise 31 and the stated path-length result
are consistent with the latter recurrence.

Original p.506 checked visually and retained. No matching correction
was located in the official layers or limited public searches; see
`sources/digital-searching-errata-review.json`.

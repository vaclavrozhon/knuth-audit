# V3-C0081 — The Lee–Wong reference has the wrong journal volume

Confirmed in volume 3, second edition, first printing (1998), printed
p.566 (file p.577), the citation after the rectangular-region bound.

The printed reference gives Acta Informatica 23 (1977), 23–29. The
paper by D. T. Lee and C. K. Wong is in volume 9, pages 23–29 (1977).
Change the bold volume number from 23 to 9. The authors, year and
page range identify the intended paper correctly.

The [publisher's article record](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF00263763)
confirms the volume, authors and page range. Original p.566 inspected;
no corresponding correction located in the official errata screen.
See `sources/secondary-keys-errata-review.json`.

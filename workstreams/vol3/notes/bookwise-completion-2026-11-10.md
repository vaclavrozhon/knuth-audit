## Limits

Every block note and page-certification exception remains in force.
In particular, external lower bounds, analytic estimates and geometric
or coding theorems are accepted as cited results; no blanket claim is
made to reconstruct their source proofs. Research questions, historical
benchmarks, exhaustive optimum censuses and unavailable priority sources
were not pursued. Compressed algebra and implementation sketches whose
full expansion would exceed the agreed scope have individual limits.
Large tables and diagrams were read and checked where material to the
argument, without building general exhaustive verifiers.

The retained exceptions are local: for example, the cocktail-shaker
distribution, the bounded-memory tape lower-bound sketch, lookahead
buffer minimality, the hashing formulas identified in that block,
and the general deleted-node-cycle argument in 6.5-10(c). They do not
stand for unread sections. Numerical certificates already established
for the shared Appendix A tables were reused after comparing the data.
Old internal `partial` records are historical evidence of a stronger
proof-reconstruction standard, not hidden current page states.

presentation validation is recorded in

# V3-C0073 — Two current errata headings identify the wrong items

Confirmed in the official `all3` errata, PDF p.5, downloaded and
matched to the official source on 10 November 2026. These are errors
in the errata headings, not new errors in the book's programs or
exercise ratings.

The 30 September 2024 change to comments on printed pp.493–494 names
Program P. The program at those pages is Program T, the trie search.
Patricia is Algorithm P on p.500. The heading should say Program T.

The 19 May 2026 change on printed p.510 names exercise 43 and changes
HM40 to HM42. Exercise 34 is on p.510 with rating HM40; exercise 43
is on p.512 and already has rating HM42 in the first printing. The
heading should identify exercise 34, whose answer is also corrected
by the errata entry of the same date on PDF p.7. The current `err3`
file repeats the exercise-number mismatch.

Checked the original errata page and book pp.493, 494, 510 and 512
visually. Earlier errata contain no intervening relocation or rating
change that would explain these locators. No separate repair was
located in the limited public screen. Source record:
`sources/digital-searching-errata-review.json`.

# Volume 3: preliminaries, appendices and index

Reviewed 10 November 2026: the 11 supplied frontmatter images, printed
583–584 and 748–780, and the separate two-page Appendix C added in 2011.
No new finding in this block.

The preface, exercise conventions and contents were read with the three
introductory answers on 584. Fermat's theorem is an external result, not
a request to reconstruct its proof here. The answer-1 code M20 and the
answer-2 pedagogical explanation are consistent with the exercise notes.
The persistent-data-structure prospectus on 583 contains no new algorithm
to validate. Its Chazelle journal-volume correction is already official.
The supplied scan omits frontmatter leaves (including viii and xiv), and
the bottom of x is clipped before the paragraph continued on xi. These
unavailable lines are a source limitation, not a finding about the book.

Appendix A was viewed in the original. The scan of 749 is upside down;
the saved upright image was used. The accepted Volume 1 transcription
and certificates (`workstreams/vol1/notes/appendix-a.md` and
`results/appendix-a-transcription.json` there) are reused. All 34 decimal
strings, including their rounding signs, match after whitespace removal.
Of the 44 octal strings, 41 match automatically; the three OCR differences
(phi, sin(1), log(phi)) were resolved against the image and also match.
The 31 rows of harmonic, Bernoulli and Fibonacci numbers were compared
with the same accepted transcription. No numerical evaluation was rerun.
The fractional harmonic formulas on 751 agree with the already reviewed
Gauss formula, including the signs and the p/q normalization. Natural
domains are understood; the introductory wording does not assign finite
values at poles. The paired 2021 Bernoulli-convention change is known.

Appendix B was read in the five original images. This includes the
negative rising/falling powers, strict versus non-strict Stirling-sum
indices, modulo convention, generating-function moments, continued
fractions, multiset sum and MIX notation. Known corrections to the
Kronecker-delta and exponential references, imaginary-part wording,
conditional notation and section-sign formatting were excluded.

Appendix C was read in `errata/pdf/3-appc.pdf` and compared with the
17 April 2011 corrections: Theorem 5.1.2C, Algorithm 5.2.2R,
Algorithm 5.4.2A, Program 6.2.3A, the duplicate Program 5.2C and the
two added program entries. A fresh official download matches the archive;
see `integration/2026-11-10-bookwise/errata/vol3-appendix-c-manifest.json`.
These later pages 757–758 are not mapped onto the original index pages
with the same numbers.

The whole original index and glossary were read as navigation and
terminology, including the colophon. Apparent issues with AND/bitwise
and, Colin's name, Williams's identity, external path length, and
Sperner's theorem are covered by the published index amendments. An
unsorted sequence of references by itself was not promoted to a finding.
The review does not claim an exhaustive check of every indexed occurrence,
personal name, foreign-script spelling, historical attribution or
production-history statement against external primary sources.

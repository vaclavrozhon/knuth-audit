# Volume 2: appendices and index

Reviewed 10 November 2026: printed pages 726–762, together with the
separate two-page Appendix C added in 2011 and its later corrections.

Appendix A was compared with the already certified tables in Volume 1
(`workstreams/vol1/notes/appendix-a.md` and the saved transcription and
results). All 34 decimal strings match the accepted transcription after
whitespace normalization. The 44 octal entries were compared visually
with that transcription; the source scan of page 727 is upside down,
so it was rotated for reading. Table 3 has the same 31 harmonic,
Bernoulli and Fibonacci rows. The existing interval/exact-rational
certificates are reused; no constant evaluation was rerun. All four
original pages were viewed. The fractional harmonic formulas on 729
agree with the existing Gauss-formula review. A suspected plus sign
before log(2q) was rejected after enlarging the image: it is a minus.
The 2021 paired change of Bernoulli convention is known, not a new error.

Appendix B was read in the original images on 730–734. The coefficient,
factorial, Stirling, number-theoretic, asymptotic and probability
notations agree with their use in the book under their natural domains.
The negative falling-power convention and the strict/non-strict index
conditions in the two Stirling sums were checked in the original.
Known changes to the Kronecker-delta reference, the exponential reference,
the imaginary-part wording, conditional notation and the added rounding
notation were accounted for.

The 2011 Appendix C was read from `errata/pdf/2-appc.pdf` and compared
with its 2011 corrections/additions and the 14 May 2026 correction
272 → 271 for Theorem 4.3.1B. This appendix is absent from the 1997
book: its printed 735–736 must not be mapped onto the original index
pages 735–736. Algorithm/theorem labels were compared with the reviewed
section material. No further discrepancy was identified.

The entire index/glossary was read as navigation and terminology.
Suspicious entries were compared with the main text and the historical
index amendments. Alt's given name, Moses's page range, Nussbaumer's
extra reference, Palmer's duplicate reference, and other recorded
amendments are known corrections. C0263 records two additional local
issues: the gamma-distribution reference and `exponention` spelling.
The damaged upper-left part of the scan of page 761 was inspected;
the Turing, Turing-machines, twindragon and two-squares entries remain
readable. The colophon was included.

Limits: this is not an exhaustive source-level validation of every
personal name, foreign-script spelling, historical attribution or
every occurrence listed in the index. The Wrench desk-calculator
attribution and colophon production history are not independently
reconstructed. Missing external historical sources are allowed review
exceptions. None of these limits conceals unread mathematical text.

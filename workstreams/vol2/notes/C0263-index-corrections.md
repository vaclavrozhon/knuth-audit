# C0263: two index corrections

Confirmed in the third edition, first printing (1997), on 10 November 2026.
Severity: low; navigation and spelling only.

On printed page 741 (file 753), the subentry `Distribution, gamma` points
to pages 253–264. Those pages concern the distribution of floating point
numbers, which has its own immediately preceding subentry. The gamma
distribution is defined on page 133 and discussed on page 134; exercise
3.4.1-16 on page 140 supplies the small-parameter method. Replace the
reference by `133–134, 140`, consistently with the separate `Gamma
distribution` entry on page 745. The original image shows the wrong
reference clearly; this is not an OCR substitution.

On printed page 749 (file 761), `m-ary method of exponention` should read
`m-ary method of exponentiation`. The correct term is used throughout
§4.6.3 and elsewhere in this index. The original image confirms the
missing `ati`.

Screening covered the index amendments in all2-prepre, all2-pre, all2 and
err2, refreshed on 10 November 2026, and targeted public searches for
these exact entries. No matching correction was found. The many known
index corrections (Alt, Moses, Nussbaumer, Palmer, and others) are
excluded. Later physical printings and absolute reporting priority are
unverified. A search snippet from a later paginated book copy also shows
`exponention`; it is corroboration of the spelling, not an independent
error report and not a verified printing identification.

`images/bookwise-p749-2026-11-10.png`; target text on pages 133–134 and
the gamma-distribution entry on page 745.

# V2-C0253: two reference typos in the matrix-vector rank answer

Confirmed editorial errors, low impact. Third edition, first printing
(September 1997), printed page 708, file page 720, answer 4.6.4-50.
The original typesetting was inspected on 10 November 2026.

The proof calls its tensor-rank lower bound "Lemma L". The relevant
result is **Lemma T** on printed page 508: the rank of a tensor is at
least the rank of any matrix obtained by grouping its indices. There
is no Lemma L in this section. Applied to the permutation matrix in
the answer, Lemma T supplies the stated lower bound mn, where m and n
are the matrix's row and column counts. The upper bound and conclusion
are correct.

The same paragraph cites Winograd's 1970 paper as volume **3** of
Communications on Pure and Applied Mathematics, pages 165–179. The
volume is **23**, as correctly cited in answer 38 on printed page 705
and confirmed by the [publisher's article record](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/cpa.3160230204).

The corrections are therefore `Lemma L` to `Lemma T`, and `3 (1970)`
to `23 (1970)`. They repair source navigation; they do not change the
rank argument. Both occur in the same short answer and are grouped in
one editorial card.

Checked all2-prepre, all2-pre, all2, err2 and current err2.textxt against
the fresh official copies archived for this pass. No corresponding
page-708 correction is present (the nearby amendment concerns answer
47). The older second-edition errata and targeted public searches also
yielded no matching report. Later physical printings and absolute
priority remain unverified. No report has been sent.

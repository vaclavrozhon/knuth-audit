# F7-C0003 — Answer 20 calls the slot/type items “options”

Confirmed, low; independently accepted by the coordinator after reading the
original p. 160–161, this note and all three p. 161 primary-TeX errata entries. Source: first digital
Fascicle 7, answer 20, first sentence on p. 161 / file 176. Original
typesetting is visible in `images/file-176.png`; the construction begins
at the bottom of p. 160 / file 175.

The sentence says, “Thus there will be one option for each slot, and one for
each type.” The immediately preceding text has introduced one primary
**item** for each slot and one primary **item** for each model type. The
next parenthesis also calls A an item and gives its multiplicity. In this
MCC encoding, the options are the possible slot/model pairs, each covering
the two corresponding items and the relevant capacity items. Thus the
word “option” in the first sentence should be “item”.

This is a local terminology error, not a failure of the intended model.
For the ten-car, six-model example there are 16 initial slot/type items
and 60 potential slot/model options before constraints or pruning. A slot
generally occurs in several options. In particular, the two valid sequences
FABABACDCE and ECDCABABAF place B and D respectively in zero-based slot 2.
Consequently the encoding needs distinct options (2,B) and (2,D), each
containing the same primary item 2. Both complete witness sequences were
checked against their required multiplicities and all 37 capacity windows;
the check is saved in `results/F7-C0003-evidence.json`.

All three public F7 errata layers were inspected: the primary TeX source,
the short rendered errata, and the full rendered errata, including the
minor-correction list. The current 6 November primary download is bytewise
identical to the shared 31 October source. The existing p. 161 changes are
the 24 October 2026 secondary-item wording in answer 20 and the 5 September
2026 option-content corrections in answers 20 and 21. None changes this
first sentence. Source hashes and scope are in the evidence JSON. No
email or external report has been sent; no public errata were edited.

Proposed minimal correction: replace the first occurrence of “option” on
p. 161 with “item”. The surrounding construction then uses the terminology
consistently. The full mathematical audit of car sequencing continues
separately; this local terminology finding does not mark the whole answer or page checked.

# F7-C0020: Step N5 is not independent of the number of variables

**Status:** confirmed, low severity. **Location:** Fascicle 7, first digital release (2025), printed page 103 / file page 118, complexity paragraph immediately before the Maltsev discussion.

The text says that step N5 takes time independent of n, the number of CSP variables. The preceding implementation instead seeds its dictionary by processing the projections of every tuple of S0, one by one. The number of those tuples can grow with n even when the domain and polymorphism arity are fixed.

Use the Boolean domain, ternary majority as the near-unanimity operation, n>=4, and one unary input constraint v1=0. During N1, each pair of coordinates i<j produces the n-tuple whose only 1s are at i and j. This tuple must be contributed: no other N1 tuple can supply the projection 11 at that pair. Thus the initial stub, and hence S0 for the first constraint, contains at least binomial(n,2) tuples.

For the N5 call with sigma=12 and rho=1, the projected dictionary has only four possible two-bit keys. Nevertheless the prescribed seeding scans at least binomial(n,2) source tuples, discarding duplicate keys as it goes. Its cost is therefore at least quadratic in n, not bounded independently of n. This contradiction does not depend on how full n-tuples are charged. With explicit witnesses, coordinatewise evaluation and contribution tests also introduce n-dependent work.

The fixed-arity polynomial-time conclusion remains valid. The repair is to say that N5 takes polynomial time in n, and account separately for seeding, witness construction and maintaining the compact stub. The bounded projected dictionary alone does not make the whole step constant in n.

The original page image `images/bookwise-file118.png` confirms both the one-by-one seeding and the false independence claim. The official current and cumulative errata snapshots of 12 November 2026 contain no correction for this paragraph. No new implementation or experiment was needed.

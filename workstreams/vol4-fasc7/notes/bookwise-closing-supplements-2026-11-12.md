The generating-function supplement to answer 306 has now been reviewed,
closing the forward reference from X0026. Here C(z) is the Catalan generating
function, and L(z), R(z) are the sums of the level and interval-count totals
over forests. Separating the first tree gives the printed recurrence for L;
separating the last tree gives the recurrence for R. Their solutions give
L_m = 4^m - (2m+1) C_m and R_m = (4^m - (m+1) C_m)/2, as stated.
The gap is level minus the remembered support level. B[theta] counts visits
to level theta before its first backward step, and G[theta] counts visits
with gap at least theta. The first-tree decomposition gives the displayed
recurrences and G[theta]/B[theta] = 1/(2-C). The bounded-height Catalan
recurrence, with C[-1]=0, gives the polynomial ratios and their expression
using D=C-1=z C^2. Substitution yields
G[theta] = D^(theta+1)/(z(1-D^(theta+1))). In particular G[1]=z C'(z),
so the mean number of zero-gap visits is m+1 among the 2m+1 visits. The
Mellin-transform asymptotic cited to forthcoming work is accepted as an
external assertion; no new analytic proof or numerical experiment is claimed.

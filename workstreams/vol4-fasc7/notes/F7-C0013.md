# F7-C0013 — The first filtering round leads to Fig.116(b), not (c)

Confirmed, low severity, 7 November 2026. First digital release, March 2025, ISBN 978-0-13-532824-8. Main printed 51/file 66, paragraph immediately below Fig.116, sentence beginning “This domain filtering”.

Replace its reference to panel (c) by (b). The example has fixed queens (1,4), (4,8), (5,1). In panel (a), the remaining row domains for rows 2,3,6,7,8 are respectively {2,7}, {5}, {3,5,7}, {2,6,7}, {2,3,5,6,7}. The first displayed filtering round deletes cells (6,5), (7,2), (7,7), (8,5), (8,7), producing exactly panel (b), with row domain sizes (2,1,2,1,3). These include the two examples named by the prose.

From (b), four further cells are deleted: (2,7), (6,3), (6,7), (8,6). Their witnesses are respectively r6, c7, r7, r7. The resulting row sizes are (1,1,0,1,2), and column c7 is empty: this is panel (c). Thus (c) is the result of the further filtering, not the intermediate panel from which the sentence says it proceeds. The diagrams, no-solution conclusion and filtering method are unaffected.

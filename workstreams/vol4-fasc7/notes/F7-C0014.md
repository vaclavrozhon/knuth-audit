# F7-C0014 — The replacement matching recurrence skips row and column zero

Confirmed, low severity, 7 November 2026. This finding is in the current official errata, not in the March2025 book text: the 4 October2026 replacement for answer278, intended printed pages285–286, current err4f7 PostScript page33. The original book's answer278 is printed217/file232.

Algorithm L indexes rows and columns from zero. In the proof that row-first and column-first greedy matching agree, its indicator recurrence nevertheless starts both exclusion strings at1:

b_ij = r_ij AND NOT b_i1 AND ... AND NOT b_i(j-1) AND NOT b_1j AND ... AND NOT b_(i-1)j.

Use the nondegenerate 3-by-3 matrix with rows (0,0,1), (0,1,0), (1,0,1). Step L1 matches (0,2), (1,1), (2,0), so b22=0. But the displayed right-hand side at i=j=2 is r22 AND NOT b21 AND NOT b12 = 1. Both omission ranges here are nonempty; every row and column has a support.

Repair: start the exclusions at b_i0 and b_0j. With the actual zero-based index ranges, the recurrence correctly says that the edge is present and neither endpoint has an earlier match. The algorithm and the matching-symmetry conclusion are unaffected; this is a local indexing error in the printed proof.

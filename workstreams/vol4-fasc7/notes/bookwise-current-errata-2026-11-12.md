# Reconciliation with the current Fascicle 7 errata

The official TeX, current PostScript and cumulative PostScript were downloaded
on 12 November 2026. Both PostScript files were rendered and their relevant
entries read. The download manifest records the exact bytes and hashes.
The three files differ from the retained 7 November snapshot. Their new
mathematical entries are dated 6 November; the date printed on an entry is
not evidence of when it became accessible to this audit.

Three former findings are now classified as known errata:

* F7-C0007, answer 50: the added hypothesis excludes an inner line with
  both endpoints on the boundary. It excludes the retained shared-chord
  witness. Read this restriction as the scope of the boundary test and its
  supporting interpretation; do not preserve a novelty claim by narrowing
  it artificially to an earlier sentence.
* F7-C0009, answer 55(c): the published change from p3+1 to p3+3 is exactly
  the retained correction.
* F7-C0010, answer 159(f): the official correction instead reverses the
  parity replacement rule and changes the first transition count from 10
  to 01. Both sides now count 01, resolving the retained counting witness.
  The former alternative repair remains historical evidence, not a new
  finding against the corrected text.

The new answer-137 entry narrows the claimed cyclic-difference-set census
and lists six unresolved parameters. It falls within the explicit census
exception of the completed first-half review; no calculation was restarted.
The other TeX differences are an endchange delimiter and the wording of
the replacement for answer 297. They create no new mathematical issue.

F7-C0008 concerns the earlier exhaustive junction classification, a separate
claim; the new answer-50 restriction is not an amendment to exercise 50(a).
No attempt to strengthen that historical witness is made here. Its exact
scope remains as recorded. All other current finding arguments remain
unaffected by this source delta. In particular, the answer-305 correction
only removes the SIZE_j>1 restriction. Neither current nor cumulative
errata changes the interaction between Algorithm R and residual supports.

The ledger preserves the three retired records and their old evidence,
while their status excludes them from the finding cards and red page marks.
Personal annotations are untouched.

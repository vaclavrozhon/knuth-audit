# First-half review: SAT encodings

7 November 2026, main reviewer alone. Main printed39–48, questions216–260 and their printed answers were read together, including original Table2 and the relevant current errata. New finding: F7-C0012, the inconsistent order-encoding units in answer224.

The nine ternary encodings in Table2 have the intended semantics: one-hot, nonempty disjoint value sets, log, binary with two codes for0, support, highest-set-bit, reduced disjoint nonzero sets, prefix and threshold bits. The eight allowed quadruples219 and the variable/clause/literal totals220 agree; Order's literal total is2V+8E, and multivalued beats Reduced in literals precisely when E>3V/2. The radio-coloring XC216 uses negative pairs for adjacent values and a shared secondary color item for distance-two conflicts.

The Sierpinski vertex-pairing rule gives (3^n+3)/2 vertices and the triangle count. The color extension argument221 and its edge-deleted variant222 are consistent. The signed-binary coordinates223 require the published offset/exponent correction; the example1202 maps to(-5,5,1). The clause-learning steps in(70) and229 were traced through the identified corners and clique hints. The n=11 clause/variable arithmetic is correct. The recurrence226 has X2=Y2=5,Z2=2, gives X3=Y3=23,Z3=13 and the stated Lucas-number expression. No empirical SAT timings were reproduced.

The replacement227 supplies the minimal-subgraph recurrence, rather than the superseded open conjecture. Its type counts and recurrence structure were read; completeness of its geometric classification is not newly proved. The replacement231's displayed resolution chain was checked one pivot at a time: it derives unequal colors at the outer corners using a bounded number of steps per pasted subgasket. Thus the current linear-size refutation is accepted, not the superseded open lower-bound question. Research233 is now answered by a published optimal-nine-edge result; that external optimality proof is not reconstructed.

The generalized Sierpinski edge count238, contraction/line-graph correspondences and parity239 agree. Every color occurs d^(n-1) times among labels, impure labels are paired, and n>1 makes its pure occurrence count congruent to d modulo2. The augmented even-d graph is consequently uncolorable. The paired symmetry action243 preserves the forced first clique; the initial zero on a further pure corner uses that parity condition. The complete symmetry-breaking search was not reproduced.

The generalized encodings240 and hints241 were checked using their known corrections: d leaves in the prefix tree, include v0 in support clauses, corrected Order example, Prefix negative bit2 and Weakened's missing five-clique hint. For d=5, Log has31 hints, Order32 and Prefix32. These counts follow directly from how many distinct colors can have each bit zero or one. The haystack247 has too few available values in its first clique; its XC items enforce row permutations and exclude the two forbidden ordered pairs. The given SAT clauses implement the same model. Historical runtimes, including the corrected10M figure, are excluded.

The order comparisons(73)–(74), auxiliary comparison(75), direct table clauses(79), hidden-variable support clauses(80)–(82) and projections(83)–(86) were compared to the actual tuples. The page48 correction is essential: four+four+two forbidden binary pairs leave both011 and021, requiring two ternary exclusions. This is already published, not a new card. The implication example u4=u1=1,v2=v1=0 in(75) forces u8=0 and v8=1: the alternative activates the lower comparison and yields a contradiction.

For248, splitting off the highest bits yields its auxiliary thresholds and both printed four-bit examples. Trivial always-true/false subthresholds are simplified using the opening boundary convention; undefined auxiliary symbols are not interpreted as unconstrained fresh variables. For(73), the literal empty-conjunction formula is not certified when t>d: the impossible comparison needs a preliminary rejection. This trivial out-of-range case is recorded as a convention/scope exception, not promoted to a finding under the user's natural-reading standard.

The five clauses249 recover exactly the seven tuples: u=0 gives the five allowed tuples after the w1/v0 and w2/v1 restrictions; u=1 forces(v,w)=(2,1); u=2 forces(1,1). Minimality of the covering was not separately optimized. The direct/support/binarized versions250–252 have the correct projections and implications. The literal count253 is Nk for preclusions and (3k+1)G+sum(dj) for support clauses. Removing at-most-one constraints254 preserves existence by choosing one allowed value for each variable. The resolution argument255 works with its published v-for-u repair. The median-fixing projections258 are equivalent, because no two entries may be strictly above or strictly below c. The support-encoding argument260 retains exactly the surviving tuples and proves forcing for the original variable literals.

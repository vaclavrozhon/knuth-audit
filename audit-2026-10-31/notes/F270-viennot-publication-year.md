# F270 — Viennot's cited volume was published in 1986

**Confirmed; low severity.** Volume 4B, first edition, second printing (2022), printed p.267 / PDF page 287, the paragraph headed “Traces and pieces.”

The citation gives `Lecture Notes in Math. 1234 (1985), 321–350`. Change **1985 to 1986**. The conference took place in 1985, but the cited volume appeared in 1986. This corrects a bibliographic date and does not affect the mathematical exposition or the date when the ideas were presented.

The original supplied page and the author's September 23, 2015 pre-fascicle 6A, printed p.83 / PDF page 91, both visibly print 1985. The suspected error is not introduced by OCR.

Primary evidence:

- [Springer's chapter record](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/BFb0072524) identifies G. X. Viennot's chapter on pp.321–350 of volume 1234, gives copyright 1986, and specifies 1986 in its recommended citation. The separate 2006 online dates are not the print-publication year.
- [Springer's volume record](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/BFb0072503) distinguishes the May 28–June 1,1985 conference from the December 1,1986 print-publication date.
- The [original front matter](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/bfm:978-3-540-47402-9/1), PDF page 2, was downloaded and visually inspected. Its copyright line explicitly gives Springer-Verlag Berlin Heidelberg 1986.

The six local current/historical errata texts and three current TeX sources were checked for the relevant 4B p.267 / Fascicle 6 p.83 locations and for Viennot, the volume number and the year correction. No matching correction was located. Six focused public queries did not locate a previous report. This establishes the discrepancy in the supplied printing, not absolute priority or its status in every later printing.

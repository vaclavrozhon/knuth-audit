# F231 — The nine-column connection puzzle is (vi), not (vii)

Confirmed in Volume4B, answer392(b), printed627/file647.

The explanation of eight reversed connections refers to the nine columns in
(vii). Diagram(vi) has eight letters A–H and nine columns. Diagram(vii) has
seven letters A–G and eight columns. Replace the reference (vii) by (vi).

eight-column board is unaffected.

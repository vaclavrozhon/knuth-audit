# F182 — sum of the 27 largest pentacube weights

Minor arithmetic error. Volume 4B, first edition, second printing (2022),
§7.2.2.1 answer 343, printed page 512 / PDF page 532. The question is on
printed page 167. The original typesetting was inspected in
`images/first100-p512.png`.

The 29 listed maximum weights sum to 8708. The two smallest entries are
200 and 210, so the 27 largest entries sum to **8298**, not the printed
8296. The comparison with 311·27 = 8397 and the resulting bound h_T < 27
remain valid. This finding concerns the arithmetic of the displayed list;
it does not certify the exhaustive tower searches or reconstruct every
geometrically attainable maximum.

The complete list and direct arithmetic are preserved in
`results/first100-pentacube343.json`. All six historical/current relevant
errata layers and the three TeX sources were searched. The existing
12 September 2022 correction to answer 343 changes an example placement
in the earlier R-tower argument; it does not address this sum.

Public searches for Knuth with 8296, 8298, and the distinctive phrase about
the 27 heaviest weights found no matching correction. Knuth's public
[September 2019 pre-fascicle](https://www.ioccc.org/2019/ciura/fasc5c.pdf)
has the same list and total. The source search is recorded in
`sources/first100-pentacube343-novelty.json`; current errata hashes were
rechecked on 7 November 2026. No assertion of absolute priority or later
physical-printing status is made.

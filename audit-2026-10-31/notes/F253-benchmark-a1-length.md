# F253 — Benchmark A1 uses 99 input bits

Confirmed in Volume 4B, main printed 310/file 330; author draft 126.
The tuning discussion describes x1...x100 as the vector required by
benchmark A1. Table 6, printed 298, defines A1 with 99 bits and A2 with
100 bits. Replace x100 by x99 in the A1 reference, or explicitly identify
A2 instead. The shorter training vector of length 62 is unaffected.

# C025: The Acta Mathematica index is dated ten years after the stated birthday

Confirmed historical error in the footnote on printed p. 87 (PDF page 107)
of Volume 4B, first edition, second printing. The existing candidate ID is
retained. The September 11, 2019 author draft, p. 23, has the same wording.

The footnote calls publication of Marcel Riesz's 1913 index a celebration
of the journal's “21st birthday.” The original index has now been obtained:
its title page explicitly gives the period **1882–1912**, the volumes
**1–35**, Riesz's name, and the publication year **1913**. These are the
same work and edition cited by Knuth. Domar's history of the journal,
printed p. 8, independently records the first issue in December 1882.
The twenty-first anniversary was therefore in 1903, not at the end of
the thirty-year period covered by this index.

The narrow correction is to remove the incorrect birthday number. For
example: “After its first thirty years, the journal Acta Mathematica
published a special Table Générale des Tomes 1–35 ...”. The scan does
not establish the exact name of an anniversary celebration, so this
finding does not prescribe “30th” versus “31st birthday.” Uncertainty
about that editorial choice does not support the printed “21st.”

The original front matter has a title, contents, and then the volume
index; it does not contain the explanatory Riesz preface anticipated
by the earlier candidate note. That earlier requirement was unnecessarily
specific: the obtained title page resolves the identity, dates, and
coverage directly. The separate question of whether all 27 puzzle names
were selected exhaustively is outside this finding.

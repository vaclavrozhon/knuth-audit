# F188 — six-cell enumeration says five cells

Volume 4B, first edition, second printing (November 2022), §7.2.2.1,
answer393, printed530 / file550. Replace “each subset of five cells”
by **each subset of six cells**.

Exercise393 concerns six strong clues. The answer assigns six labels,
using restricted growth strings of length6 and the Bell number203,
then weights each string by the number of injective assignments of its
distinct labels from {1,…,5}. The position subsets must therefore have
six cells. The question's total is C(25,6)·5^6=2,767,187,500.
The examples on the answer page also have six entries.
The finding corrects the written enumeration procedure, without
recomputing its distribution of solution counts.

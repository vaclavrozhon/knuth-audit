# F233 — The reflected unary string ends at d−1

Confirmed in Volume 4B, main printed 284/file 304, immediately before (165).
For a value 0<=x<d, the order bits are x^j=[x>=j], with 1<=j<d.
To obtain d−1−x, complement and reverse the whole string of d−1 bits.
The prose instead ends that string at xbar^(j−1), leaving j as an unrelated
free index. Replace j−1 by d−1. Formula (165) itself is correct.
The author draft has the same typo on page 100.

The original Volume 4B typesetting and the corresponding September 23, 2015
author draft were inspected. Relevant current and historical Volume 4B and
Fascicle 6 errata, including their available TeX sources, have no matching
correction. This is not a claim about absolute priority or a physical copy.

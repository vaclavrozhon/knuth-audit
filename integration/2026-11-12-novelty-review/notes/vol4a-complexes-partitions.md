# Volume 4A: complexes and partitions, publication review

Reviewed on 12 November 2026 against the first edition, first printing
(2011). All sixteen baseline-confirmed findings F0073–F0088 are retained.
A simplicial complex is a family closed under taking subsets. A partition
of n is a multiset of positive integers with sum n; p(n) counts these
partitions. The detailed notes define the remaining notation.

The original relevant statements were inspected on pages 388, 743–745,
750–752, 755 and 758–761. The numerical witnesses and local algebra
were checked by hand. These findings concern the specified formulas or
proof steps; they do not automatically invalidate the subsequent results.

## Official and public-source comparison

Compared the refreshed [early Volume 4A changes](https://www-cs-faculty.stanford.edu/~knuth/all4a-pre.ps.gz),
[later changes](https://www-cs-faculty.stanford.edu/~knuth/all4a.ps.gz),
[current errata](https://www-cs-faculty.stanford.edu/~knuth/err4a.ps.gz),
[Fascicle 3 changes](https://www-cs-faculty.stanford.edu/~knuth/all4f3.ps.gz)
and [Fascicle 3 errata](https://www-cs-faculty.stanford.edu/~knuth/err4f3.ps.gz).
The Fascicle 3 page offsets here are 354 for main text and 637 for answers.
Relevant entries were read, including the replacement answer 23, the
clarification to 50(e), and the bibliography for answer 64. None repairs
these defects. In particular, the March 2021 Bernoulli correction to
answer 25(b) is separate from the contour problem.

Fresh searches combined Knuth/TAOCP with octahedron, Fristedt,
occupied diagonals, Jacobi 750, 7.2.1.3-107, Mellin errata,
7.2.1.3-102, shadow typo, second-smallest, and binary partitions errata.
The public [tamnd/brain solutions](https://github.com/tamnd/brain/tree/main/content/en/practice/maths/taocp/vol4)
were screened for 7.2.1.3 exercises 97, 101–104, 107 and 7.2.1.4
exercises 23, 25, 31, 50, 64, 70. Several solve different questions or
contain independent mistakes. The Mellin solution uses c>1 but also
has incorrect formulas; it does not identify the book's contour error.
No matching explicit report was found.

McKay and Robinson's [Eulerian-circuit paper](https://users.cecs.anu.edu.au/~bdm/papers/euler.pdf),
Section 6, equation (8), includes all vertex pairs before the tree sum
is replaced by a minor. This supports the correction to F0079 but
does not report a book error. Colthurst and Kleber's
[binary-partition paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0907.3873), Theorem 2,
uses the parity of the largest part's multiplicity. It is the cited
source of the correct construction, not a prior report of F0087.
The Fristedt publisher PDF was inaccessible in the earlier check;
its full text is not claimed as inspected. F0083 follows from the
exact removal bijection and the book's partition-ratio estimate.

No matching prior error report was found in the checked sources.
Absolute priority is not established.

# Publication review: Volume 1, tree algorithms and asymptotics

Reviewed 12 November 2026. The original pages, their context, and the refreshed official correction layers were compared with the existing arguments. No matching prior public report was located for these twelve findings. Priority and later physical printings remain unverified. Large finite verification runs were not repeated.

- **V1-C0043 — retain.** The PWR listing on p.574 subtracts two before testing the exponent for equality with two. With B=b^5 and v=-(B-2), both v and v-1 are representable, but v-2 wraps to negative zero. JAZ therefore selects the wrong branch and the derivative of x^v becomes v*x. Taking x>0 avoids a real-domain issue. This witness does not trigger the separate MULT boundary defect. The constant-node representation on p.338 does not exclude the input.
- **V1-C0044 — retain.** Reread the COPY entry/return and the complete answer on pp.343,572–573. The printed block counts have costs 6,2a,15(a+1),3(n-1),13(n-1-a),11n,2(n-a),5(n+1),2(n-a),4. Their sum is 36n+14. The STJ and final JMP add 3, yielding 36n+17. A one-leaf copy takes 53u, not 58u. The special entrances and the caller's jump cannot supply the missing five units. The copied tree is not alleged to be wrong.
- **V1-C0047 — retain.** The field declarations on p.325 and the explicit CMPi semantics on p.134 settle the interpretation: bytes 1–3 of an index register are zero during comparison. Therefore CMP5 with field (1:2) on p.568 compares zero with the right link, not P with the right link. A root with only a left leaf immediately takes the supposed thread-removal branch and omits the leaf. The existing I4 repair respects the documented working-register set. The published end-of-program-marker correction does not change this instruction.
- **V1-C0048 — retain.** Following the added optimization on p.567 in the original p.325 listing gives 12u for one node; both stated formulas give 11u. The jump bypasses the two lines whose changes are left to the reader, so their interpretation cannot remove this discrepancy. Recounting the general blocks gives 7n+11a-6 and 7n+26a-21, with a the number of null right links and n>0. The omitted cost is n-a+1. This concerns timing, not visit order.
- **V1-C0050 — retain, erratum only.** Rechecked the original and 1999 replacement displays and the complete saved derivation. The stack-height recurrence is h(T)=max(1+h(L),h(R)). With z=u/(1+u)^2 the total-height generating function is S=(1/u-u)D(u)-(1+u), where D(u)=sum u^j/(1-u^j). The Mellin residues give D(exp(-t))=(gamma-log(t))/t+1/4-t/144+O(t^3). Writing w=1-4z yields the singular terms -(1+2w/3+O(w^2))*log(w)+3w^(1/2)+3w^(3/2). The coefficient of sqrt(pi/n) after Catalan normalization is 9/8-2/3=11/24, and the 1/n term cancels. The error estimate is supported by the complex sector specified in the detailed proof. Thus the original formula is correct and the replacement -13/24 plus 1/(2n) is not. The relation to rooted plane-tree height requires adding one node and one level; it does not explain this replacement. A located 2009 discussion states only the leading plane-tree terms and does not discuss the disputed correction.
- **V1-C0051 — retain.** The exercise explicitly asks to print the cycles, and C5–C6 print every signed edge. For a path followed by all spokes from vertex 1, m=2n-3 and the printed cycle lengths are 3,...,n, so their sum is quadratic. The claimed O(m log n) time cannot cover that output. The saved strengthening for arbitrary DFS trees of the fan is sound: vertex 1 has at most three tree neighbors, and removing it leaves at most three paths. Their distance sum is at least (n-1)^2/12, forcing quadratic total output for the nontree spokes. This does not rule out short bases based on a non-DFS star. The linear DFS statement is correct for a compact representation, or with output cost added as O(n+m+L).
- **V1-C0052 — retain, bibliographic erratum only.** Compared the 2023 added citation with the first and last pages of the published Jeandel–Rao article. The article says 37 pages and ends on page 37; the citation ends at 39. The publisher still links this author-deposited published version. No mathematical claim about the tile construction is challenged.
- **V1-C0053 — retain.** The p.588 coefficient estimate contains binom(1/2,n) without its compensating (-1)^(n-1). This binomial coefficient is negative for every even positive n, whereas the tree counts are positive. The square-root expansion in the preceding part supplies exactly that missing sign. The final asymptotic in the exercise remains correct. The 2020 cross-reference addition in part (a) does not repair part (g).
- **V1-C0054 — retain.** The recurrence on p.380 feeds newest to oldest into f; the arcs in the p.584 correspondence list oldest to newest. For two binary arguments, f(u,v)=1-v gives the maximum-period sequence 0011 under the exercise, while the printed correspondence enters the shorter 01/10 loop. Reversing the arguments restores the correspondence without changing the final enumeration. The official changes on p.584 concern other paragraphs and the drawing.
- **V1-C0055 — retain as an omitted convention exception.** Reread pp.324,380,584–585. For the added head loop h placed last after the root arc r, the stated rules give ALINK(h)=r, BLINK(h)=h and BTAG(h)=1. Since BTAG is explicitly identified with RTAG, this disagrees with RTAG(HEAD)=0 in convention (8). The text mentions only the root exception. The general graph representation is consistent; the claimed exact agreement of conventions needs this additional exception or an explicit reset.
- **V1-C0056 — retain.** The well-ordering note explicitly claims uncountable level at every node. Its used index set has a least member a0. The final segment T[a0] has no proper ancestor among the T sets, and no single vertical dyadic set contains it; its level is zero. Reversing the ordering convention instead leaves a least-index node with no descendants, contradicting the required degree. This is independent of the separate disputed meaning of “uncountably many levels” in C0045. The existing R x Z construction supplies the intended property without a well-order.
- **V1-C0057 — retain as a low-severity endpoint omission.** The construction on p.393 explicitly permits nonnegative vectors but defines a component using the last of q entries. For n>0,q=0 that entry does not exist. The bijection therefore needs a separate zero-edge branch. The counting formula is correct, and the missing case is elementary; this is not a substantive counting counterexample. Raney's primary proof explicitly separates this case before using the last Prüfer entry. Lower the UI severity from medium to low to reflect the limited impact.

The [Jeandel–Rao publisher page](https://doi.org/10.19086/aic.18614) identifies the publication and links its published article. The first/last original pages were inspected locally.

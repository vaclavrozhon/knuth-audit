# Publication review: Volume 1, asymptotics and MIX examples

Reviewed 12 November 2026. The original 1997 pages, linked evidence, and refreshed official Volume 1 correction layers were checked together. The layers are byte-identical to the archived sources; see ../sources/manifest.json. No matching prior public report was located for the nine retained findings below. Priority and later physical printings remain unverified.

- **V1-C0017 — retain.** The original symmetric integral on p.505 contains u^(m+1), not |u|^(m+1). For the allowed m=2,f=1,r=0,s=2 it is identically zero, while the preceding difference of integrals is positive, because log(1+u/x)>u/x-u^2/(2x^2) for 0<u<=1,x>1. The absolute-value repair proves the intended result. This is a proof error only.
- **V1-C0018 — retain.** The exercise introduces an unrestricted real parameter and the answer explicitly discusses negative x, so positivity cannot be supplied as an implicit convention. At x=-4 its own exact identity and the alternating-series bound 3/4<R_(-1/4)(n)<1 imply alternating exponential growth. The asserted finite limit 1/5 fails. The required extra exponential term is already present in the other branch. The 2008 summation-index and parenthesis corrections do not fix this.
- **V1-C0019 — retain.** The normal integral in the exercise is from 0 to x with no restriction x>=0. The answer on p.506 has positive argument x^2/2 and loses the sign for negative x. Adding sgn(x) or restricting x repairs it. This is a small domain omission, not a defect in the incomplete gamma function.
- **V1-C0021 — retain, errata consistency only.** The 2022 correction changes PRIME to 99; the 2026 follow-up explicitly changes one derived 499 to 599. Neither changes the original negative-value comment on p.149, the displayed PRIME EQU -1 in Fig.15, nor the five derived object-listing entries on p.511. Their addresses were checked symbolically: 3004 has 599; 3010,3012,3020 have +99; the initial constant 2 moves to address 100. The unrelated literal -499 stays. The original 1997 examples are mutually consistent, and the prime algorithm is correct. The finding is incomplete propagation in the correction set, not a rediscovery of the published relocation.
- **V1-C0023 — retain.** Recounting the zero-matrix paths in the displayed programs gives 137u and 523u before HLT. In the second program, Phase 1 is 432u; Phase 2 is 3u initialization plus eight 11u iterations. Including the one-unit HLT yields 138u and 524u. The printed pair mixes conventions. Relevant 1999 and 2013 program changes do not alter this path; the 540-to-530 correction concerns a different random experiment.
- **V1-C0024 — retain, added answer.** A nonrecord row-minimum entry costs 6u; a new minimum costs 13u, including the reset and list-store instructions. This independently gives coefficient 7mH_n, not 5mH_n. The saved derivation's early-stop estimate is also sound: each possible saddle row has probability P/m, where P=(m+n)/binom(m+n,m), and each skipped row costs between 6n+13 and 13n+5m+1. On m=n=k this supplies a negative correction of order k^3/binom(2k,k), not the stated O(k^2/binom(2k,k)). These are defects in Knuth's concrete MIX formula. The Hofri–Jacquet preprint expressly leaves instruction multipliers unspecified and discusses relative rather than this displayed absolute error; it is not a prior report of the concrete mismatch. No exhaustive computation was repeated.
- **V1-C0025 — retain as historical wording.** Reread p.518 and the translated Liber abaci investment example, Sigler p.439. The explicit eighteen-year compound-interest calculation and repeated squaring provide a concrete medieval European arithmetic application unrelated to Easter. This refutes the unqualified exclusion of every other nontrivial application. The finding makes no claim about how widespread commercial arithmetic was.
- **V1-C0026 — retain, with its historical interpretation explicit.** Reread the original claim, PG10 cols.877–880 (table and instructions), and Victorius's own discussion on Mommsen p.678. Earlier cyclic paschal tables are finite systematic procedures; Victorius himself describes the preceding 84-,95-,112-year cycles. His own work is likewise called a canon, so excluding table procedures only for the predecessors would not be a consistent reading. Retain only the need to qualify his absolute priority, not a claim that Hippolytus has proven absolute priority or that every damaged inscription character has been reconstructed. The narrower possible distinction between types of cycle remains stated.
- **V1-C0027 — retain.** Independently recounting the printed blocks yields costs 3,26,43,29,26 units with multiplicities 1,4,907,911,4. Their sum is 65631 before OUT; OUT and HLT add two more units. Thus the 65595 figure is too small under either convention. The previously verified exact group counts and execution profiles were reused, not rerun across all radices. Treat the possible undercounting of CHAR as an explanation only, not a historical fact.

# Volume 3: external sorting and search trees

Reviewed on 2026-11-12. The 15 cards below are retained after checking the original second-edition, first-printing typography, the detailed local arguments, and all five refreshed official errata layers. C0059 concerns the 2022 erratum rather than the original theorem. Symbols in each row follow the definitions in that card's detailed note.

| Card | Decisive reason and scope |
| --- | --- |
| V3-C0050 | On p. 678 the final snowplow interval ends at kappa T, not T. The last T units of ordinary motion are [(kappa-1)T,kappa T]; the printed interval is empty when kappa>2. The later trajectory and run-length conclusion need not change. |
| V3-C0051 | On p. 679, during recycled input, conservation of the P internal records gives P=a(t) integral from x(t) to L of g(u) du, with a(t)=p+qt/T_1. Together with the answer's constant encountered density C, this implies x(t)=(P/C)ln(a(t)/p), contradicting the printed exponential trajectory. The remaining fresh-input interval gives run length P(e+ln(P'/P)) in this continuum model. This is not an assertion of an exact finite-capacity expectation or a proved stochastic limit. |
| V3-C0052 | The recurrence on p. 276 omits the outer factor x accounting for one additional merge. At n=1 it gives 5, whereas the five one-merge runs give 5x. The following generating function and table already use the correct factor. |
| V3-C0053 | The exact reverse-generation claim on p. 684 excludes simultaneous tape exhaustion. Initial counts (2,2,1,1,1) sort in two phases, but every distribution generated from (1,0,0,0,0) by adding one entry to all four others has total congruent to 1 modulo 4. Seven runs are impossible under that rule. Componentwise domination repairs the upper-bound argument; the optimum bound itself is not refuted. |
| V3-C0054 | The partial fractions on p. 685 need 1-z/rho, not 1-rho z, because rho is a root of q_4(rho)=rho and hence a pole of the generating function. The smallest positive pole is below 1/2, where the printed expansion is analytic. The main-text coefficient formula has the correct pole convention. |
| V3-C0055 | The three level labels in answer 4 on p. 694 should be 1,2,3, not 2,3,4. The root has level zero, and the displayed Ashenhurst tree already has three level-2 leaves while achieving the stated 2.8 passes. This corrects the proof sketch, not the optimum. |
| V3-C0056 | The final buffer count on p. 700 needs -gcd(P,D)/2, not +gcd(P,D)/2-1. Direct substitution into the preceding formula gives PD/2+3D/2+P/2-gcd(P,D)/2. For P=D=2, the stated offsets require five buffers under the answer's own accounting, not six. |
| V3-C0057 | On p. 704, answer 20 omits a factor 2 in its change in average circular-search cost, which sums ordered pairs. Probabilities (1,4,2,3)/10 on a four-cycle give actual improvement 2/25, while the printed sum gives 1/25. The sign and optimal-arrangement conclusion are unaffected. |
| V3-C0058 | On p. 705 the explanation for variants (a,c) needs K>K_l, not K>K_u. The exercise assumes K is present, so the latter condition is impossible in the maintained interval. For two keys and K=K_2, midpoint 1 and the modified assignment l=1 repeat forever. The answer's yes/no classification is correct; its supporting witness is outside the promised domain. |
| V3-C0059 | The January 2022 replacement N+1.063 in the AVL height bound on p. 460 fails for the ordinary two-node tree of height 2. Even with the separately corrected coefficient 1.4405, its right side is about 1.998629. The Binet remainder used in the accompanying argument is about .065248, not at most .063. The original N+2 bound avoids this defect. |
| V3-C0060 | The AVL search count on p. 470 must count the root initialization even when the root is balanced. In the three-node tree with root 2 and leaves 1,3, searching for 2 executes 12 MIX time units; the printed definition gives D=0 and predicts 10, even assigning frequency D-1=-1 to a program line. Set D to one plus the encountered nonroot nodes with nonzero balance. |
| V3-C0061 | The recursive TREE routine on p. 715 uses global k after the right-subtree call has changed it. With inorder balance factors (0,0,0), that call advances k from 2 to 3; the outer Q=X_k then returns the right leaf instead of the root. Saving the current node in a local pointer repairs this. The 1995 first-edition replacement has the same shared-index defect. |
| V3-C0062 | The fixed-height path-length upper bound on p. 714 must be maximized over feasible heights to bound all N-node AVL trees. The printed minimum does not follow from the preceding fixed-height extremal property. Maximization gives the stated asymptotic scale, so the final asymptotic theorem is not being rejected. The 1996 first-edition addendum repeats the minimum. |
| V3-C0063 | Exercise 27 on p. 480 defines N as internal nodes, but the formula on p. 718 uses the key count. A root [2,5] with children [1], [3,4], [6] has four internal nodes and six keys; searching for 3.5 needs four comparisons. The displayed 2 lg(N+2)-2 is below four with N=4 and equals four with N=6. The answer's drawn family has the same size mismatch. |
| V3-C0064 | On p. 721, the utilization fraction U=N/(M S), where S counts pages, lies in [0,1] and has variance at most 1/4. It cannot have the stated linear or superlinear variance. Mahmoud and Pittel's cited phase transition concerns occupied space, not that fraction. This is a mismatch of the named random variable, not a new variance theorem. |

Public searches combined Knuth/TAOCP with the affected exercises and snowplow intervals, shuffled reservoirs, run lengths, polyphase reverse generation, cascade partial fractions, Ashenhurst levels, gcd and buffers, circular organ-pipe searching, binary-search loop witnesses, AVL height 1.063, root counts, reconstruction from balance factors, extremal path length, 2-3 internal nodes versus keys, and utilization variance. Exact-number searches frequently produced unrelated results; broader topic searches were also checked.

The [Mahmoud–Pittel publisher abstract](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0196677489900230) explicitly identifies occupied space as its random variable and confirms the branching-factor thresholds. The abstract was available through search although direct opening returned HTTP 403. This supports C0064's interpretation; the paper predates the audited printing and does not report this book wording.

The public study guides for [replacement selection](https://cracked.guide/guide/knuth-w085-0), [polyphase merging](https://cracked.guide/guide/knuth-w086-0), and [cascade merging](https://cracked.guide/guide/knuth-w086-1) contain exercise summaries and no posted discussion identifying these defects. A further lead was the public [tamnd exercise collection](https://github.com/tamnd/brain/tree/main/content/en/practice/maths/taocp/vol3). Its matching exercise pages were inspected for a report of the specific book errors. In particular, the shuffled-reservoir page concludes 2P under a different, flawed model; the cascade page uses an eigenvalue argument; and the tree-reconstruction page discusses uniqueness, rather than the printed shared-index routine. They do not supply the corrections retained here. The binary-search page does give the standard correct lower-endpoint looping condition, so that witness is already public, but it does not identify the book's wrong subscript. Several pages criticize their own earlier generated arguments; that wording was not treated as a report about Knuth's answer. The collection is a prior-publication lead, not an authority used to certify our proofs.

No matching prior public report of the specific 15 defects was found. The underlying correct mathematics and several natural counterexamples may already be public. Priority and absence from private correspondence are not established.

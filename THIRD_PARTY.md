# Third-party material

Donald E. Knuth's books, scanned pages, and figure excerpts are not included.
The three distributed images are original audit diagrams, listed in
[the image inventory](data/IMAGE_SOURCES.md). Historical notes may refer to
omitted source images; those references do not make the images available.

Selected companion-program CWEB sources retain their original headers and
attribution. Source URLs and hashes are recorded with the arguments and
metadata files. The [author's MMIXware page](https://www-cs-faculty.stanford.edu/~knuth/mmixware.html)
identifies those programs as public domain. Audit change files are distinct
from the original program sources. The complete MMIX architecture document
and the MMIXware book are not included.

The frontend dependencies and their exact versions are listed in
`review-ui/package-lock.json`. Their own licenses apply. The prebuilt frontend
includes React, React DOM, Lucide icons, react-markdown and its Markdown/math
dependencies, and KaTeX assets. Their license notices are preserved in
`review-ui/DEPENDENCY_LICENSES.txt` and `review-ui/licenses/remark-math.txt`.

External papers, errata and public reports are linked and described in the
audit records. The package does not bundle their complete PDFs or HTML pages.

## Ryser's transversal conjecture

**§7, exercise 16, printed p. 37** · Exercise · [48] · `V4A-7-16`

Does every Latin square of odd order have a transversal, choosing one cell in every row and column with all symbols distinct?

**Partial results.** Results and computation on transversals of Latin squares give substantial special cases. The sources located do not prove that every odd-order Latin square has a transversal; this is the Latin-square Ryser conjecture, not the hypergraph conjecture with the same name.

## Counting self-dual threshold functions

**§7.1.1, exercise 123, printed p. 95** · Exercise · [46] · `V4A-7.1.1-123`

Determine the exact number of self-dual threshold Boolean functions on ten variables.

**Reported resolution.** A count of 144130531453121108 is recorded for nine-variable threshold functions and, via the stated bijection, ten-variable self-dual threshold functions. Anthony’s 2026 Table 2 uses this value from OEIS; OEIS credits Minfeng Wang (2010). The underlying enumeration was not independently verified, so this is a reported count rather than a certified computation.

Evidence: [Anthony, Chamber geometry and specification numbers of Boolean threshold functions, Table 2 (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29477); [OEIS A000609, original contribution and self-dual counting convention](https://oeis.org/A000609/internal).

## BDD size of the middle multiplication bit

**§7.1.4, exercise 179, printed p. 272** · Exercise · [M47] · `V4A-7.1.4-179`

## Counting maximal chains of the Tamari lattice

**§7.2.1.6, answer 34, printed p. 790** · Question in an answer · Unrated passage · `V4A-A-tamari-chains`

Enumerate the directed covering paths from the least to the greatest parenthesization in the Tamari lattice, beyond the initial counts given in the answer.

**Partial results.** Nelson gives a recursion and polynomial formulas, with prescribed initial values, for chains whose lengths are a fixed amount above the minimum. These results and extended exact counts improve the enumeration without giving a single simple formula for all maximal chains.

Evidence: [Nelson, A recursion on maximal chains in the Tamari lattices (2017)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.02987); [OEIS A027686, computational enumeration record](https://oeis.org/A027686).

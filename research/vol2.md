## Bounded continued fractions: Borosh–Niederreiter

## Online multiplication

**§4.3.3, exercise 15, printed p. 318** · Exercise · [M49] · `V2-4.3.3-15`

Find optimal multiplication algorithms in models where the next input digits cannot be read until earlier output digits have been produced.

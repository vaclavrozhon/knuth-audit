<a id="v3-t-linear-extensions"></a>

## Generalizing tableau enumeration to directed acyclic graphs

**§5.1.4, printed p. 62** · Question in the main text · Unrated passage · `V3-T-linear-extensions`

Find useful exact counting formulas extending the tableau hook-length formula to topological orderings of more general directed acyclic graphs.

**Partial results.** Brightwell and Winkler prove that counting linear extensions in general is #P-complete. This obstructs a universal efficiently evaluable exact formula under standard complexity assumptions; it does not rule out formulas for additional special graph classes.

Evidence: [Brightwell and Winkler, Counting Linear Extensions (1991)](https://www.math.ucdavis.edu/~deloera/MISC/LA-BIBLIO/trunk/BrightwellGraham/brightwelllinext.pdf).

[Recorded searches](SEARCHES.md): [S85](SEARCHES.md#s85).

## Optimal Shellsort gap sequences

**§5.2.1, printed p. 85** · Question in the main text · Unrated passage · `V3-T-shellsort-gaps`

Determine the best sequences of Shellsort increments for large input sizes, making the average-case or worst-case objective explicit.

**Partial results.** New gap constructions and lower bounds improve particular families. The September 2026 construction optimizes a specified practical regime and does not prove global optimality for arbitrary gap sequences.

Evidence: [Liu, A New Gap Sequence for Shellsort: RL-Driven Algorithm Discovery Beyond N^(4/3) (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29881); [Zang, Improved lower bounds of the time complexity of shellsort (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08997).

## Dynamic optimality of splay trees

**§6.2.3, printed p. 478** · Question in the main text · Unrated passage · `V3-T-splay`

Does the total cost of splaying stay within a constant factor of the optimum binary-search-tree execution on every access sequence, up to the usual initialization term?

**Partial results.** Chmel et al. prove an O(log log n · (log log log n)^2) competitive ratio in a July 2026 preprint. The original conjecture requires a constant competitive ratio, so this does not settle it.

Evidence: [Chmel et al., Splay trees are almost dynamically optimal (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18498).

## The van der Waerden number W(3,k)

**§7.2.2.2, exercise 5, printed p. 317** · Exercise · [M46] → [HM47] · `V4B-7.2.2.2-5`

Determine the asymptotic growth of the two-color van der Waerden number forbidding a 3-term progression in one color and a k-term progression in the other; the original quadratic-growth conjecture was replaced.

**Partial results.** The original quadratic-growth speculation is false by Green's superpolynomial lower bound. Cao's September 2026 preprint strengthens the lower bound to k^(c log k) for some positive constant c. The replacement question asks for the general asymptotic growth, which this does not determine.

Evidence: [Green, New lower bounds for van der Waerden numbers (2021)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.01543); [Cao, An improved lower bound for the van der Waerden number w(3,k) (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.31798).

Book amendment, 2023-05-18: Replace the quadratic-growth conjecture by the general asymptotic question. [Official record](https://www-cs-faculty.stanford.edu/~knuth/err4b.textxt).

[Recorded searches](SEARCHES.md): [S44](SEARCHES.md#s44), [S49](SEARCHES.md#s49).

## Gumball-machine and Feige lower-tail conjectures

**§MPR, answer 30, printed p. 375** · Question in an answer · Unrated passage · `V4B-A-gumball`

Minimize the lower-tail probability for sums of independent nonnegative random variables of mean at most one, in the integer-valued and general formulations of the answer.

**Partial results.** Milojevic and Sudakov's October 2026 preprint proves the sharp finite-n bound for independent nonnegative variables with means at most one and threshold n + delta when delta >= 1. At delta = 1 this answers the integer-valued gumball question. The stronger real-valued assertion printed here uses delta = 1/(e−1), which is below 1 and is not covered by that theorem.

Evidence: [Milojevic and Sudakov, Perfect matchings in hypergraphs and Feige's inequality (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10380).

## Longest-lived mobile Life pattern

**§7.2.2.2, answer 68, printed p. 561** · Question in an answer · Unrated passage · `V4B-A-mobile-life`

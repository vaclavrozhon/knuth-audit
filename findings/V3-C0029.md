# V3-C0029 — The mean-exchange linear coefficient has the wrong sign

**Confirmed · low severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Volume3 / §5.2.2 answer16, p.630, mean-exchange asymptotic: sign before(gamma+2)/(4 ln2)

The lower-order mean estimate is wrong; the sorting algorithm and the limiting one-quarter exchange ratio remain correct.

In answer5.2.2-16, p.630, +(gamma+2)/(4 ln2) must be -(gamma+2)/(4 ln2). A complete derivation from the finite path sum tracks the Laurent and integration constants, with justified remainders. The coefficient discrepancy is greater than1 and cannot be hidden by a periodic term bounded by0.0005 or the stated remainder. The periodic bound itself is verified with one small interval enclosure and an analytic infinite tail. Original typesetting and all five errata layers were checked; the historical first-edition replacement repeats the wrong sign. Sedgewick’s original theorem has the correct minus sign.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

In the mean Batcher-sort exchange count on p. 630, the linear coefficient contains -(gamma+2)/(4 ln 2), not the printed plus sign. Summing the stated per-index asymptotic proves the negative sign. The discrepancy exceeds 1 in the linear coefficient and cannot be absorbed in the stated oscillation of amplitude below .001. The 1977 first-edition replacement repeats the wrong sign; Sedgewick's original 1978 analysis gives the correct one.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

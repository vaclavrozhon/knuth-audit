# F016 — The smoothing step uses the wrong letter

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer MPR 28(b), p. 374

The variable should be epsilon rather than delta.

In 28(b), the assignment should be ε=−min(p_(n−1),1−p_n), not δ. The exact example a=1/4, b=3/4 gives ε=−1/4 and δ=−3/16. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript; no matching correction was found in the complete 4B and Fascicle 5 errata. The physical copy has not been checked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer MPR 28(b), p. 374: the endpoint perturbation uses epsilon=-min(p_(n-1),1-p_n), not delta. For the probabilities 1/4 and 3/4, epsilon=-1/4 gives the induced product change delta=-3/16. Substituting -1/4 for delta would make probabilities negative.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

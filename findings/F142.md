# F142 — Invalid parameter range for the Gompertz distribution

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / historical note after answer 7.2.2.1–189, p. 469

The stated range allows a cumulative probability of minus one.

The historical note gives F(x)=1−a^(1−b^x) for a,b>0 and x≥0. Set a=4, b=1/2 and x=1: F(1)=1−sqrt(4)=−1 exactly. A proper distribution of this form requires a>1 and b>1. Both the book PDF and author draft have the broad range, and no correction was found in the six relevant errata layers. A physical copy and priority of discovery remain unchecked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 189: the stated Gompertz formula is not a proper distribution for arbitrary positive a,b. With a=4, b=1/2, x=1 its value is -1. For a proper finite distribution of the displayed form require a>1 and b>1; both below one instead leave positive mass at infinity.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

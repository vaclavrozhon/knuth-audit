# F235 — The strict comparison count ends in−1

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / SAT414, answer633/file653: strict comparison clause count

Replace the final+1 by−1.

The printed count2^n+2^(n−1)+1 is two too large; n=2 gives five resolvents, not seven.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

SAT414's strict comparison count is 2^n+2^(n-1)-1, not the expression ending in +1. At n=2 elimination gives five clauses; the recurrence T(n)=2*T(n-1)+1 with T(1)=2 verifies the correction.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V3-C0050 — The last snowplow interval ends at kappa T

**Confirmed · low severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Answer5.4.1-22, printed678: final time interval ends at T instead of kappa T

Local interval-endpoint typo; subsequent solution and FIFO run-length formula are not refuted.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

On p. 678 the final snowplow interval ends at kappa T, not T. The last T units of ordinary motion are [(kappa-1)T,kappa T]; the printed interval is empty when kappa>2. The later trajectory and run-length conclusion need not change.

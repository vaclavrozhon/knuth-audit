# V2-C0019 — The Gaussian correlation formulas have incorrect normalization and moments

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.3.2 · Answer 20 · p. 565

Two supplementary Gaussian formulas fail simple exact checks; the main variance result is unaffected.

At w=z=0 the displayed generating function gives 2^(-n/2), although an exponential moment must equal 1. For n=3,k=1 the next formulas give -1/5 and 1/7, while the cyclic correlation is identically -1/2 and its square is 1/4. A spectral derivation confirms the corrected formulas. The final variance formula and the main result of exercise 20 remain correct. Rechecked against all errata layers on November 6, 2026; the latest physical printing and priority are unverified. The original Dixon paper was not read.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The normal generating function is not normalized at zero, and the printed moments contradict the constant correlation for three inputs.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

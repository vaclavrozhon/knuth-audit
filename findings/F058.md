# F058 — The construction adds 28 isolated items

**Confirmed · high severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–262(b), p. 483

The domain restriction x + y < n + 16 is missing.

The unprimed items need x+y<n+16; the printed domain adds 28 isolated items. The corrected domain gives both published large counts and the general linear ZDD formula. Visually confirmed on PDF p.483 and PostScript p.187; no correction was found for the historical Fascicle 5 p.291.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

DLX 262(b): the unprimed triangle coordinates also require x+y<n+16. Without that restriction there are 28 isolated items and 16n+92 items rather than 16n+64.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

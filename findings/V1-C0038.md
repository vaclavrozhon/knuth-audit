# V1-C0038 — The multiplication-time simplification has two wrong coefficients

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · §2.2.4 · Answer 16 · printed page 553

Replace 40pr+4αpr by 39pr+5αpr in the second formula. The first exact formula is correct.

Substituting the stated sums into the correct first formula gives 4αpr²+39pr+5αpr+8q′₀r+38r+29. The printed simplification instead contains 40pr+4αpr, an excess of (1−α)pr. For P=x+1, Q=x and M=1, the stated assumptions hold with p=2, r=1, α=1/2 and q′₀=0: the exact formula and accepted literal MIX execution give154u, while the simplification gives155u. This is an algebraic error, independent of the quality of the heuristic assumptions.

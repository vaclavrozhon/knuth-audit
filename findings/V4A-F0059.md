# V4A-F0059 — The exact reversal mean counts the terminating permutation

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: Answer 7.2.1.2–5(a), printed 703: exact average number of swaps in L4

The finite mean for step L4 is too large by floor(n/2)/n!. Algorithm L and the limit cosh(1)-1 remain correct.

The exact mean includes the final descending permutation although L2 terminates before L4. Subtract floor(n/2)/n!; for n=3 the mean is 1/3, not 1/2. The limiting constant is unchanged.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.2-5(a), p. 703: the reversal-swap average includes floor(n/2) swaps at the final descending permutation, where L2 already terminates. Subtract floor(n/2)/n!. At n=3 the actual average is 1/3, not 1/2; the limiting constant is unchanged.

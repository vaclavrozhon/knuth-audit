# V2-C0259 — Exact determinant operation counts fail at the base case

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · Section 4.6.4 · Answers 69 and 70 · Printed 717, 718

The exact counts need correction. The division-free algorithms and their asymptotic orders are unaffected; no general replacement counts are asserted.

At n=2, answer 69 assigns 22 additions to its one-subtraction base case. Answer 70 claims savings that reduce the multiplication count for a generic 2 by 2 determinant to zero.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The claimed exact addition count in answer 69 gives 22 at n=2, versus one subtraction for its specified base case. In answer 70 the claimed determinant-only saving removes all two multiplications at n=2, leaving an impossible affine computation of a quadratic determinant. These refute the exact counts, not their asymptotic orders; no general replacement count is asserted.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

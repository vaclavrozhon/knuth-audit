# F051 — The SAT encoding does not express distance ≤ k

**Confirmed · critical severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / SAT 390(d), p. 627

It encodes walks of exactly the specified length.

The encoding tracks walks of exactly k steps, not distance ≤k. A single edge with k=2 yields an incorrect SAT model. Adding the unit clauses s_j for every layer fixes it. Checked by a general proof and 185,739 parameter cases. The error also remains in the official 2016 replacement answer.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 390(d): the clauses propagate walks of exactly j steps. On a single edge s--t with k=2, layers (1,0),(0,1),(1,0) satisfy the printed clauses and -t2 although the distance is one. Forcing s at every layer repairs the at-most-k encoding. The 2016 official replacement still has this defect.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

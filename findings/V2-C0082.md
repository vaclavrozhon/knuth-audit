Unsent candidate / clarification, TAOCP Vol. 2, 3rd ed., answer4.2.1-7, p.612

Does "rounding ... is equivalent to truncation" here mean rounding only
at the radix-point position selected by the stated normalization?
That interpretation works, but truncation need not give the nearest
floating-point value.

For p=2 and excess0, take u=2/9 and v=-4/81. Both are normalized
balanced-ternary floating-point numbers. Their sum is14/81, whose
balanced expansion is .1(-1)(-1)(-1). It lies between1/6 and1/2,
so normalization leaves the exponent unchanged. Truncation gives2/9,
with error4/81. The representable neighbor4/27 has error2/81.

Perhaps it would help to distinguish this fixed-position rounding
from the best floating-point approximation discussed on p.231.
This is an interpretive candidate, not a claim that the fixed-position
rounding statement is false. No report has been sent.

# F053 — The reference points to unsuitable examples

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / SAT 443(e), answer p. 637

The cited part does not disprove the claim; a simple alternative does.

The reference to examples in (c) cannot refute UC_(n−1), since all those examples belong to UC0. The formula {(x),(¬x)} with n=1 suffices. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript; the general full-cube counterexample and cases n=1–5 were checked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

SAT 443(e): the examples in (c) have unit-contradiction level zero, so cannot establish failure at level n−1. The two clauses (x), (not x) provide the n=1 witness; the complete Boolean cube supplies the general family. The false assertion is correctly rejected, but its cited counterexample is wrong.

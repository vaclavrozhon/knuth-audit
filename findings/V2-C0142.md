**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

With alpha=sqrt(2)−1, beta=alpha/2 and gamma=alpha, the printed recursion returns 0; the first valid hit is 3. Reciprocal rotation swaps a closed-left interval with a closed-right interval. Alternating the two variants repairs the algorithm, with a proof of minimality and termination.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

With alpha=sqrt(2)-1, beta=alpha/2 and gamma=alpha, the printed recursion returns zero, outside the interval. The first valid index is three. Reciprocation reverses which interval endpoint is included; the stored two-function repair tracks that reversal and proves termination. This is an exact permitted-input counterexample, not numerical rounding.

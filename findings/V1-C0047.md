# V1-C0047 — MIX traversal skips a left subtree

**Confirmed · critical severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · §2.3.1 · Exercise 22 · printed 568

A two-node tree loses its left visit. The failure affects every supported MIX radix.

CMP5 1,6(RLINK) uses field (1:2). Those bytes of an index register are zero, so it compares zero with RLINK(Q), rather than P with that link. With root 1002 and left leaf 1004, both right links zero, the program visits only the root. A repair through I4 has a general proof and 1454 accepted MIX executions; these runs were not repeated.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

CMPi zero-extends the tested high bytes, so the printed field comparison skips a valid left subtree.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

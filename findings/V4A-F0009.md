# V4A-F0009 — Tautological self-loops invalidate the raw acyclicity condition

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / answer to exercise 7.1.1–85, p. 550

The necessity claim rejects valid reduced median sets when tautological clauses are retained. The characterization is correct after self-loops are removed.

Take (x OR y) AND (x OR NOT x). Its models are {01,10,11}, with one-bit witnesses for both coordinates, but its implication graph contains x→x and NOT x→NOT x. The book explicitly permits these loops and counts them as directed cycles of length 1. Every strong component is still a singleton. Replace raw acyclicity by acyclicity after removing self-loops; the complete necessity and sufficiency proof of this repair is supplied. Original typography and all five relevant 4A/F0 errata layers were checked, including the applicable V1 definition correction.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.1.1-85, p. 550: acyclicity must disregard self-loops. The formula (x OR y) AND (x OR NOT x) defines the reduced median set {01,10,11} but its implication graph has loops. The given construction does not exclude tautological clauses.

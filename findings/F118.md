# F118 — SUDOKU swaps O and the dot in the second row

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / p. 429, answer 7.2.2.1–65; SUDOKU, row 2, columns 5–6

The second row should read DUSKO., not DUSK.O.

The last column repeats O in rows 2 and 5. The dot represents the unnamed sixth symbol, so swapping the final two cells in row 2 repairs the grid. A coordinate solver and the original DLX1 agree on the full unique solution. Both our PDF and the author’s older PostScript show the incorrect row. The June 29, 2022 erratum swaps D and U in the first row; it does not fix this separate error. A physical copy remains unchecked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 65: SUDOKU's second row must be DUSKO., since the printed DUSK.O repeats O in the last column. The June 2022 erratum swaps D and U in the first row and does not fix this second-row defect.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

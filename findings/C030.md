# C030 — The general criticality claim depends on excluding isolated vertices

**Candidate · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–116(c), p. 444

An edge-only definition admits a counterexample; the stated Mycielski sequence is still critical.

Take an edge plus an isolated vertex. Removing its only edge lowers its chromatic number. After the Mycielski construction, deleting the apex edge to the isolated vertex’s clone leaves a 5-cycle, so three colors remain necessary. All 128 binary assignments and an explicit 3-coloring were checked. A stronger conventional definition of criticality excludes the example, so this is a scope candidate rather than a confirmed error in the requested theorem.

# V2-C0193 — The transformation needs the next u and g indices

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.6.1 · Exercise 13

In the right-hand triple, u_j(x) and g_j should be u_(j+1)(x) and g_(j+1). The left-hand triple already has these indices.

In the right-hand triple, u_j(x) and g_j should be u_(j+1)(x) and g_(j+1). The left-hand triple already has these indices.

For example, take u_1=x^3+3x+1, u_2=x^2+1, and w=x, with leading coefficient ell=1. The first remainder is u_3=2x+1. Multiplying both inputs by w gives U_3=2x^2+x and G_3=2. The printed right-hand triple would instead give x^3+x and 1. The powers of ell and the formula for h_j remain correct.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

In answer 13 the right sides of the displayed scaling formulas need index j+1. With u1=x³+3x+1, u2=x²+1 and common factor w=x, the first remainder is 2x+1, so the scaled next polynomial is 2x²+x and the next leading coefficient is 2. The printed indices instead give x³+x and 1.

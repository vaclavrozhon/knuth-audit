# V2-C0033 — Fibonacci run table reports tails instead of exact lengths

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.3.3 · Answer 27 · Printed 576

The table compares different probability events, although the general formula and the random-sequence row are correct.

The Fibonacci row gives Pr(L>k), whereas the comparison is for Pr(L=k). For k=1 through 5 the correct row is 1/2,1/6,7/30,7/120,41/1560. The printed general formula, triangular regions and random row agree with these corrected values. A general area proof and 15 independent exact polygon steps support the finding. Original typesetting and all errata layers rechecked on 6 November 2026; latest physical printing and priority remain unverified.

# V2-C0044 — The L1 bound uses the wrong argument

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.3.4 · Answer 16 · p. 581

The proof substitutes f(Y) where f(X) is required; a second instruction names the wrong algorithm step.

For Y=XU and the unscaled dual basis V, |x_k| is at most f(X) times the largest absolute coordinate of V_k. The printed f(Y) fails for U=((1,-1),(0,2)), X=(4,3): it would assert 3≤2. Norm updates belong in S4 and S5, rather than S4 and S7. The corrected L1 algorithm is valid for the unshifted lattice. The wrong step reference alone is not a demonstrated wrong numeric output. The shifted-lattice issue is separate (C0038). Original pages and all errata layers rechecked on 6 November 2026. Later physical printing and priority remain unverified.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The coordinate bound in answer 16 must use `f(X)`, where `f(X)=norm_1(XU)`, rather than `f(Y)` for `Y=XU`. With rows `(1,-1),(0,2)` and `X=(4,3)`, the printed bound implies `3<=2`. Norm updates also belong in S4/S5, not S4/S7. These repairs preserve the intended unshifted-lattice algorithm; the wrong step reference alone is not claimed to produce a demonstrated wrong numerical result.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# F155 — One blue path misses its domino diagonal

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–262(a), p.483, Randall illustration

The illustration violates its local crossing rule in one horizontal domino; the tiling itself is valid.

The blue bend at (13.5,1) should be (13.5,2), using cell coordinates from the upper-left corner. This repairs the horizontal domino covering cells (12,1) and (13,1). All other 135 local motifs pass. Both book and draft contain the same bend, and all 24 normalized colored paths agree between versions.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 262(a): one blue path follows a horizontal domino's top edge instead of its diagonal. Moving its bend from (13.5,1) to (13.5,2) repairs the local motif and preserves the rest of the path. The enlarged original was inspected.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

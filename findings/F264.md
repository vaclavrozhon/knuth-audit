# F264 — The construction uses about 6m variables, not 7m

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / SAT answer 204(c), p.587: the variable count has leading coefficient 6, not 7

A numerical typo in the size estimate. The clause bound, four-occurrence restriction, and reduction remain valid.

The final variable estimate in answer 204(c) counts one extra m; the displayed construction gives 6m+29 or 6m+30 variables.

## Publication review, 17 November 2026

Add 3m occurrence variables, 35 variables from the gadget, and 2 ceil(3m/2)-6 new extension variables. For m >= 2, the total is 6m+29 if m is even and 6m+30 if m is odd.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

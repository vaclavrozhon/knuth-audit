# V3-C0024 — The phase-shift bound needs a range qualification

**Candidate · low severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Volume3 / §5.2.1, answer42(a), p.627, both phase-shift8N/(gh) inequalities

The unrestricted helper inequality fails on a valid small Shellsort input; the final average-case estimate remains correct.

On p.627, answer42(a) uses8N/(gh) to bound the change under phase shifts. With N=36,h=18,g=17 and only keys1 and18 exchanged, the first pass is already complete. G(1,18)=1 and G(19,36)=0, while8N/(gh)=16/17. All assumptions and indices are valid. The bound does hold when N>=gh, which may be implicit in the asymptotic proof, so this remains a scope candidate. A general4ceil(N/(gh)) bound and the complementary regime prove the final result. All five errata layers were checked; the known earlier diagonal+1 correction is separate.

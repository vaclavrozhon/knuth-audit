# F087 — The probability should be 27/28

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2–38, p. 404

Binary words of length 9 give a different result from the printed 32/35.

The probability for n=9 is incorrect: m=2 gives 486/504=27/28, not 32/35. The corrected G_m, two independent algorithms, and a general expansion agree. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript; the known index correction in the same paragraph is a different issue.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 38: the n=9 probability at m=2 is 486/504=27/28. The printed 32/35 would give a noninteger number of aperiodic words. Expanding the correct preceding generating function yields the corrected general probability as well.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

The event whose probability is (1-1/p)^n should be
"n_j = n-j for 1 <= j <= t = n". The printed condition
"n_(j+1) = n_j-1 for 1 <= j <= t = n" reaches one place beyond the
last nonzero remainder. For n=t=1, n_1=0 and the following remainder
is zero of degree -infinity, not -1. The probability itself is correct.

All five available errata layers were searched; no matching correction
was located. The latest physical printing has not been checked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The definition of a normal sequence in answer 4 includes the last transition to the zero polynomial: its degree is minus infinity, not one less than zero. The range must stop before that transition. The stated probability remains correct.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V2-C0263 — Gamma distribution points to the wrong pages; exponentiation is misspelled

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Index/glossary, printed 741 and 749/files 753 and 761.

Navigation and spelling only; no mathematical conclusion changes.

The gamma subentry repeats the page range for floating point distributions. A second index entry prints exponention.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The gamma-distribution index subentry on p. 741 incorrectly repeats the floating-point-distribution pages 253–264, instead of the gamma pages 133–134 and 140. The m-ary index entry on p. 749 misspells exponentiation as exponention. Both are navigation/editorial defects.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

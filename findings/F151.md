# F151 — The matching ZDD asymptotic is missing a square root

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / main text, p. 121

The leading size estimate is too small by a factor approaching sqrt(5). The time-complexity order is unchanged.

Page 121 gives approximately (N/5)F(N+1). The exact expression in answer 255 instead implies (N/sqrt(5))F(N+1). This is independent of the older one-node correction F079. Both the book and author draft contain the coefficient; no matching correction was found in six errata layers.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The exact formula in answer 255 gives the leading term N*F_(N+1)/sqrt(5), not N*F_(N+1)/5. Indeed 1+2/phi=sqrt(5), where phi is the golden ratio. This coefficient error is independent of the one-node discrepancy F079.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

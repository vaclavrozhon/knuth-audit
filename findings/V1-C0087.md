The attribution gives Leibniz the year 1682. His relevant manuscript
has been identified as LH 35, 3 B 11, fol. 21r, in the Gottfried
Wilhelm Leibniz Bibliothek, Hannover. Its factorial-residue table and
statement match the transcriptions by Vacca (1899) and Mahnke
(1912-1913). The statement (p-2)! = 1 modulo p, for prime p,
is equivalent to Wilson's theorem.

The current working catalogue of the Leibniz edition dates this
individual leaf to approximately 1703 (record 31464, catalogue
number 39249). Mahnke's item 25, the same leaf, has no explicit
date: he places it between material dated December 1681 and June
1682 in a proposed chronological ordering. Vacca's nearby date
before 1683 concerns Fermat's theorem. The library's aggregate
date 1685 describes the whole manuscript bundle.

The exact year therefore merits review. This is not a proved
replacement of 1682 by 1703: an earlier discovery or another dated
witness has not been excluded, and the catalogue's dating argument
has not been independently established. The attribution to Leibniz
is supported. No matching correction was found in the four public
Volume 1 errata layers available on 2 November 2026.

Primary witnesses and detailed reading limits are preserved in
notes/leibniz-stickelberger-root-review.md and its linked manifest.

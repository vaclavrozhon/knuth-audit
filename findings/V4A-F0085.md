# V4A-F0085 — The Mellin contour passes through the pole at one

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.4–25(b), Mellin inversion contour, printed 751

Use a vertical line Re(z)=c with c>1 in both displayed integrals.

The contour Re(z)=1 is invalid after summing to zeta(z+1)*zeta(z)*t^(-z)*Gamma(z).

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.4-25(b), p. 751: after summation the vertical line Re(z)=1 passes through the zeta pole at 1 and does not justify the interchange by absolute convergence. Start with any line Re(z)=c>1, then shift it and take residues. The final expansion, with the separate known Bernoulli repair, remains valid.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

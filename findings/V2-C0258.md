# V2-C0258 — Bound for all second partial derivatives is false

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · Section 4.6.4 · Answer 71 · Printed 718

The final second-derivative claim is false. The first-derivative theorem remains valid; its displayed formula also needs y1 in place of the first yr.

The product of 20 variables needs only 19 multiplications, but its 190 distinct mixed second derivatives require at least 190 chain multiplications. The answer allows only 171 and no divisions.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The assertion that all second partial derivatives cost at most 9m+3d multiplications and 4d divisions is false. The product of twenty variables uses m=19,d=0, but its 190 distinct degree-18 mixed partials are linearly independent modulo affine polynomials. Each multiplication adds at most one dimension, so 171 multiplications cannot produce them. Repeating the scalar differentiation construction gives one Hessian row. The local p1-prime times y_r also needs y1.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# F023 — The tail-distribution series is missing its constant term

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / exercise 191, p.148, and answer p.469: tail object and generating-function constant

The printed series gives e at zero instead of 0. The intended mean and variance remain correct. The added wording correction identifies the intended combinatorial object.

Answer 191 needs an additional -e in the gamma-series expression for G(z). The question also calls the underlying set partition a set permutation.

## Publication review, 17 November 2026

Answer 191, p. 469: subtract e from the incomplete-gamma series. At z=0 the printed series equals e, although the probability generating function has zero constant coefficient. Its derivatives and reported moments are unaffected.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

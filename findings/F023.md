# F023 — The series has the wrong constant term

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / p. 469, answer 191

It gives e at zero rather than 0; subtracting e is missing.

The gamma series G(z) is missing the constant −e: at zero it gives e instead of 0. Checked by a general derivation and six numerical tests, and confirmed in the PDF and PostScript. The mean and variance are correct. No matching correction was found in the complete 4B and Fascicle 5 errata; the physical copy has not been checked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 191, p. 469: subtract e from the incomplete-gamma series. At z=0 the printed series equals e, although the probability generating function has zero constant coefficient. Its derivatives and reported moments are unaffected.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

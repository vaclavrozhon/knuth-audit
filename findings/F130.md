# F130 — A 53-cell rectangle contradicts the claimed 55-cell minimum

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–109, p. 442

The minimum-area claim is false under its stated conditions(i) and(ii).

Place ZERO.ONE.TWO.THREE.FOUR.FIVE.SIX.SEVEN.EIGHT.NINE.TEN in a single row, with each dot an empty cell. This uses 43 letters and 10 separators: 53 cells, fewer than 5×11=55. Every word reads across and adjacent letters belong to a listed word. The sentence explicitly drops connectedness; no minimum side length is stated. Both the local PDF and author’s PostScript make the 55-cell claim. This does not establish the true minimum or refute the connected examples.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 109: after explicitly dropping connectivity condition (iii), a single row containing ZERO through TEN separated by dots has area 53, below the claimed minimum 55. The original question does not require both side lengths to exceed one. No claim that 53 is globally minimal is needed.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

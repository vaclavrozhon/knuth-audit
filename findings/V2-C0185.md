# V2-C0185 — The BBS error sum skips stage zero

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.5.4 · printed 669

The claimed overall upper bound is not derived. This does not prove that actual failure probability exceeds9/10.

The loop begins at t=0 and performs two votes there, but the geometric error sum starts at1. With r=8, the stated stage bounds sum to19/18 before the other error allowance.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The book's stages begin at zero and still predict two new bits there. Its subsequent geometric error sum begins at one. For r=8 the listed bounds over all actual stages sum to 19/18, exceeding 8/9. This invalidates the displayed derivation, not necessarily the true success probability. Constant-factor larger samples and a finer initial grid repair it. The cited paper starts its different RSA loop at one.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V2-C0042 — The coarse approximation may use the wrong last digit

**Candidate · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.3.4 · Table 1, row 29 · p. 106

A candidate rounding issue affects one coarse squared-value entry; its logarithm and score are correct.

The exact squared minimum in dimension 5 is 2.6503385745833175... × 10^165, while the table prints 2 × 10^165. Ordinary rounding to one significant digit gives 3 × 10^165. The table does not specify the convention for these coarse approximations, so this remains a candidate. Two archived independent searches agree, and stored transforms and witnesses were checked again without rerunning the searches. The displayed logarithm 275 and score 1.15 are correct.

This earlier candidate or dismissed entry was outside the 806 confirmed-card publication review.

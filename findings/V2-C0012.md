# V2-C0012 — The replacement sum loses complete digit coverage

**Confirmed · high severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · 2001 public errata · Answer 3.2.2–21

The public correction breaks the complete-sequence construction.

The 2001 erratum replaces the original sXₙ+Yₙ by (Xₙ+Yₙ) mod rs. For r=2,s=3,k=1 the replacement gives 0,2,2,1,1,3, omitting digits 4 and 5. Its period is still 6, but it is not a complete sequence or an order-one recurrence. The original weighted sum is correct.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The erratum’s unweighted sum cannot cover the product alphabet, even for complete order-one component sequences.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

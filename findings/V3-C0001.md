# V3-C0001 — Excluding 25-cliques does not keep the output small

**Candidate · medium severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Volume 3 / exercise 5-23, p. 9; related answer on p. 590

The stated social-graph assumptions still allow hundreds of trillions of 24-cliques.

A disjoint union of forty complete 20-partite graphs with parts of size 5 and one complete 24-partite graph with parts of size 4 has exactly 4096 vertices, degrees 92 or 95, and 194416 edges. It has no 25-clique, yet it has 4^24 cliques of size 24. The enumeration algorithm is valid; the informal feasibility claim remains a candidate because “enormous” is not quantified.

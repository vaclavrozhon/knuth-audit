# F054 — The derivation definition conflicts with the answer

**Confirmed · high severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / SAT 442 definition p. 359 and answer (b,f) p. 636–637

After a contradiction, it does not allow all the required literals.

The printed definition does not automatically allow every literal after a contradiction. Six clauses on four variables have a as the only first step and satisfy F⊢₂ε but F⊬₂¬a; P₂ returns a contradiction and, according to the answer, every literal. Allowing termination at a lower-level contradiction corrects the definition. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript, with independent enumeration of sequences.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

SAT 442: for clauses (a or b), (a or not b) and the four clauses (not a or ±c or ±d), the strict sequence definition permits only a as its first forced literal. It detects a level-two contradiction through (a,c) and (a,not c), yet cannot derive not a. Thus the asserted property that every literal follows from a detected contradiction does not hold under this definition. This is a defect in the inference relation, not a counterexample to the intended UNSAT decision procedure.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V2-C0250 — A missing plus turns the parity-count identity into a product

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · Section 3.5 · Answer 29 · Printed page 596

A local displayed identity is false as printed; the surrounding proof uses the correct sum.

Section 3.5 answer 29 omits the plus between two normalized sums. With k=1 and the 2-distributed period 0011, the left side equals 1/2 at n=4M, while the printed product is zero. Adding the missing plus restores the counting identity; the final upper bound and the extremal construction remain valid.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The two normalized terms on p. 596 need a plus between them. The 2-distributed period 0011 gives left side 1/2 and printed product 0 for n divisible by four. The later upper bound already uses the intended sum.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V2-C0059 — Invalid product of lengths in the proof of Theorem A

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.5

Two displayed probability equalities are invalid at boundary cases. Theorem A remains true; clipping empty and out-of-range intervals repairs the proof.

The displayed probabilities of the inner and outer boxes need clipping.
As printed, the equalities need not hold for all the bases b_j.

For the inner equality, take k=2, b=2, u_1=u_2=0, v_1=v_2=1/4.
Then u'_i=v'_i=0. The inner event requires 1/2 <= U < 0 in each
coordinate, so its probability is 0. The printed product is instead
(-1/2)^2=1/4. For the outer equality, k=1, u=0, v=1, b=2 gives
Pr(0 <= U_n < 3/2)=1, whereas the printed length is 3/2.

The theorem remains true. One repair is to use

inner_i = [ceil(b*u_i)/b, floor(b*v_i)/b),
  outer_i = [floor(b*u_i)/b, ceil(b*v_i)/b),

interpreting the inner interval as empty if its endpoints are reversed.
The correct volumes are

product_i max(0, floor(b*v_i)/b - ceil(b*u_i)/b),
  product_i (ceil(b*v_i)/b - floor(b*u_i)/b).

All endpoints lie in [0,1]. These boxes sandwich the target box,
and their volumes converge to product_i(v_i-u_i) as b tends to infinity.

The original page has been inspected. The historical all2-prepre,
all2-pre, all2 and err2 lists, and the current err2 TeX, were checked.
The April 21, 2019 change on page 153 concerns only the phrase about
2^j-ary sequences. No correction of these box probabilities was found.
The newest physical printing and priority have not been checked.
Nothing has been sent.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

With two coordinates, base 2 and interval `[0,1/4)`, the inner box is empty but the printed product is 1/4. The outer equality can likewise exceed probability 1 at a boundary. Clipped grid intervals repair the proof, and the limiting distribution theorem survives.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# F096 — The asymptotic estimate hides a growing term

**Confirmed · high severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / 7.2.2–70, p. 60, asymptotics ρ_m

The difference from 2^m is about 3m − 15/2, rather than O(1).

Instead of 2^m+O(1), the expansion is 2^m+3m−15/2+O(m²/2^m). Checked by a general proof from the exact recurrence, independent path enumerations, 21 pairs of polynomials, and 14 rationally certified roots. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript; no matching erratum was found.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Exercise 70: the correct growth constant is 2^m+3m−15/2+O(m²/2^m), so an additive O(1) is false. The derivation from the exact recurrence in NES_NOTES.md controls the remainder and identifies the positive pole. The cited preprint also has an erroneous second coefficient; the corrected asymptotic is derived independently, not inferred from that expression.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

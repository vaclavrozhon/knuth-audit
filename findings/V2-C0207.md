In the paragraph treating p mod 4 = 1, the first gcd need not be 1.
For p=5 and u=1 it is gcd(x^2-1,x^2-1)=x^2-1 over F_5.
In general it is the whole polynomial when u^((p-1)/4)=1, and is 1
when that value is -1. Thus "the gcd will be trivial" would suffice.
The recommendation to omit that first gcd remains correct.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

For p=1 mod 4 the first square-root gcd is trivial, but may be the entire quadratic rather than 1. For p=5,u=1 it is x²−1. The recommendation to skip this test is still right.

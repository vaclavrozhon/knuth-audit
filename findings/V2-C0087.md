# V2-C0087 — An extra zero in the unnormalized Avogadro example

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · See attached source pages

One unnormalized example in the 2020 replacement has an extra zero, changing its value by a factor of ten.

In the replacement for page238, lines -12 through -8, the unnormalized
representation of Avogadro's number is printed as

(26,+.000602214).

There is one extra leading zero. The fraction has nine decimal places,
whereas this is an eight-digit format; its value would also be
6.02214e22 rather than approximately6.02214e23.

The intended representation appears to be

(26,+.00602214).

This has eight places and the correct magnitude. The same corrected
pair already appears a few lines lower in the same errata page as
N (+) h, and it gives the stated unnormalized product with Planck's
constant. The normalized Avogadro value in the replacement paragraph
also has the correct magnitude.

The official SI definition independently gives6.02214076e23:
https://www.bipm.org/en/si-base-units/mole

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The 2020 p. 238 replacement prints (26,.000602214), which has nine fractional digits and is ten times too small. Its later occurrence on the same errata page is correctly (26,.00602214). The SI Avogadro constant confirms the scale.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

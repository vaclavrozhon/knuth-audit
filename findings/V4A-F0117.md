# V4A-F0117 — The meet comparison uses a false order chain

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.6–27(f), printed 787

Use that the Kreweras meet is below each input in the Tamari order, then invoke the Tamari meet property.

The proof asserts that each input forest lies below its Tamari meet. Those inequalities are reversed; the claimed comparison of the two meets is nevertheless true.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.6-27(f), p. 787: the printed chain reverses the direction of the inequality for a meet, the greatest common lower bound. The scope vectors 10 and 00 already contradict that direction. The proof should use the greatest-lower-bound property; the conclusion is true.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

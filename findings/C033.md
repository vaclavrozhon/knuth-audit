# C033 — The symmetry convention omits an explicit global heads/tails swap

**Candidate · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–138(a), p. 452; exercise p. 140

The stated counts are correct when all heads and tails may be interchanged globally; preserving polarity gives a different count of classes.

Two complete independent enumerations agree on 85,360 toroidal arrangements with all four orientations of the top-left ABcd tile. Identifying a global heads/tails swap gives 42,680 arrangements and 1,056 classes, reproducing every stated automorphism count. Using only geometric operations and four-color renaming gives 1,361 classes. The lower-left answer figure has 12 symmetries without polarity reversal and 24 with it. Part (a) does not explicitly name the polarity swap. This remains a low-severity clarification candidate: the author may intend a broader relabeling of the paired Algorithm C colors, and the numerical results are correct under that convention.

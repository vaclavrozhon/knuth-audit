# V1-C0088 — The Kronecker delta is documented before the stated year

The stated introduction date, 1868, is later than a surviving
original publication. In the Monatsberichte der Koeniglich
Preussischen Akademie der Wissenschaften zu Berlin, aus dem
Jahre 1866, p. 601, Kronecker explicitly defines delta(i,k)
as 1 when i=k and 0 otherwise. The volume's title page bears
the printing year 1867. Page 597 dates the communication
to the meeting of 15 October 1866.

The later reprint in Journal fuer die reine und angewandte
Mathematik 68, p. 273, explicitly identifies itself as a reprint
of the notice already published in the Monatsberichte. Its
p. 276 repeats the same delta definition.

Suggested direction: date the communication to 1866 and cite
the original Monatsberichte p. 601, while distinguishing its
1867 volume title date. The exact release date of the individual
issue and the earliest possible use of the notation have not
been established here. The mathematical definition is correct.

Primary scans:
https://archive.org/download/monatsberichtede1866knig/monatsberichtede1866knig.pdf
https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/download/pdf/PPN243919689_0068/LOG_0020.pdf

The original TAOCP page, the earlier title and pages 597/601,
and the reprint pages 273/276 were visually checked. The four
public errata layers all1-prepre, all1-pre, all1, and err1 contain
no matching correction. No later physical printing was checked.

Local status: confirmed discrepancy in the audited sources.
This is an unsent draft, not a claim of discovery priority.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

A surviving original published by 1867 defines the delta before the stated 1868; no TAOCP-specific earlier report was found.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V3-C0010 — The Shellsort example saves 75 time units, not 88

**Confirmed · low severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Volume 3 / answer 5.2.1-10, p. 620

The second numerical saving in answer 5.2.1-10 is incorrect.

For Table 4, the passes with gaps 7,5,3,1 have 5,9,6,9 insertions requiring no movement. Thus C=29, T=4 and the stated formula 3(C−T) gives 75u. The 87u value for Table 3 and the general formula are correct. The two printed examples were traced; original typesetting and relevant errata were checked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

For Table 4 the no-move counts over its four passes are 5,9,6,9. The stated saving 3(C-T) is therefore 75 units, not approximately 88 on p. 620. The 87-unit claim for Table 3 is unaffected.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

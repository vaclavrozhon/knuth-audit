# V2-C0180 — The prime-power half-jump has the wrong sign

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume2 · printed 663

This changes the exact bounded remainder. An unspecified O(1) term is not invalidated.

The book counts primes up to and including x. At x=2, the midpoint value is1/2 and needs plus1/2 to give1; the printed minus would give0.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The book counts primes with p<=x, explicitly giving pi(2)=1. Consequently its prime-power counting function is the symmetric explicit-formula value plus 1/(2k) at x=p^k, not minus. At x=2 the printed correction gives zero instead of one. The 2011 n-to-x correction is separate. This concerns the exact remainder description, not the unspecified O(1) bound.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

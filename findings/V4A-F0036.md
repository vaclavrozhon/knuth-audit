# V4A-F0036 — The clique restriction leaves necessary edges unset

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.1.4-130(b), printed page 642: claimed restricted clique function

Adding the missing clique condition restores the reduction; the lower-bound theorem is not refuted.

The construction isolates the first k vertices and joins the middle and last vertex sets, but leaves the middle set internal edges unset. The claimed equivalence fails unless that set is also made a clique.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 130(b), p. 642: the restriction omits edges within the middle k vertices. At m=8,k=2, isolate vertices 1,2, join 3,4 to 5 through 8, and put only edge 5--6 among the latter. The listed restrictions and the later two-clique hold, but the missing edge 3--4 prevents a four-clique.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

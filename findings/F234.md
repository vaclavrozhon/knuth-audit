# F234 — A floor omits the central product constraint

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / SAT406(a), answer630/file650: product threshold rounding

Use ceil(sqrt(a+1)) for the first upper limit; the missing clause rejects9>7.

At d=5,a=7, the printed406(a) clauses accept x=y=3.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

SAT406(a) omits the first product-threshold clause when sqrt(a+1) is not integral. At d=5,a=7 the printed clauses admit x=y=3. The first upper limit needs ceil(sqrt(a+1)); the second endpoint has its separately derived bound.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V4A-F0023 — The maximal-element update filters equality instead of containment

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / answer 7.1.3-132(d), Q7, p. 604

The update can retain a strict subset and violates its stated maximal-family invariant. This trace does not claim that the final clique list for the example is wrong.

For the graph with closed-neighborhood words (15,11,5,11), the vertex-2 update sends the maximal family {11,13} to {11,9,5}. Word 9 is a proper subset of 11. Q7 checks equality where containment is required.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 132(d), p. 604, Q7: for closed neighborhoods (15,11,5,11), the update appends word 9 despite existing word 11 containing it. Equality must be replaced by containment. The intermediate maximal-family invariant fails; this example alone does not claim a wrong final clique list.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V3-C0019 — Updated Gaussian tail bound drops a polynomial factor

**Confirmed · low severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Errata all3-prepre, PDF p.7: 1 Jan 2006 replacement for Volume3 p.64, line2 above (52)

A local estimate in the 2006 erratum is too strong. The original weaker bound and the final asymptotic formulas (52) and (53) remain valid.

For t=0, replacing the finite limits in the unnormalized integral term of (50) changes it by asymptotically (1/2)n^(1/4−epsilon)exp(−2n^(2epsilon)). For fixed 0<epsilon<1/4 this is not O(exp(−2n^(2epsilon))) as required by the replacement. The proof handles the shifted unit lattice, including the integer family n=m^1000. A weaker bound such as O(exp(−n^(2epsilon))) suffices. All five errata layers were checked; the defect is in the replacement itself.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The January 2006 replacement on p. 64 is too strong. For t=0, the two Gaussian tails beyond n^(1/4+epsilon) are asymptotic to (1/2)n^(1/4-epsilon) exp(-2 n^(2 epsilon)), so the claimed bound lacks an unbounded polynomial factor. The original weaker bound and final formulas (52)-(53) remain valid. This card concerns an error introduced by an erratum.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# F044 — The boundary solution has the wrong y

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / SAT 357, p. 619

For (p,q) = (1,0), the correct value is y = 0.

In the boundary case (p,q)=(1,0), y should be 0 rather than 1. The printed x=1/2, y=1 give the field (0,1/2,1/2). Checked directly against (155) and in the PDF and PostScript.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 357: for (p,q)=(1,0), use y=0. The printed x=1/2,y=1 gives field (0,1/2,1/2); the defining r=xy/(x+y-xy) also immediately excludes y=1.

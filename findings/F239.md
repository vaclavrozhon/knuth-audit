# F239 — Negative input initialization breaks the monotone construction

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / SAT428(b), answer634/file654: propagation circuit initialization

Initialize only positive present-edge signals; the same propagation recurrence then gives the claimed monotone bound.

The proposed monotone chain initializes a signal to the complement of an edge input.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

SAT428(b) initializes a circuit signal to the complement of an edge input, violating the claimed monotonicity. Initialize only positive signals for present edges and leave absent edges unassigned in the simulated propagation. The same recurrence then yields the claimed monotone bound; the theorem is not refuted.

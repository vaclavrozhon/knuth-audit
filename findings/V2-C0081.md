# V2-C0081 — Clarify the finite-digit and nonempty-pattern scope

**Candidate · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · Section 4.1 · Exercise and answer 33 · Printed pages 213, 610–611

The stated domain and the domain used by the counting method need explicit qualifications; the intended finite result has a complete proof.

Exercise 33 allows any digit set, while its ordinary generating function requires finite counts. Its displayed counting formula also needs a nonempty pattern: for base 2 and the empty pattern it gives 2 instead of 1. The subsequent system explicitly uses nonempty patterns. The finite-digit theorem, including base 1, and the Fibonacci example are correct; this remains a scope clarification candidate.

# V4A-F0115 — The active-block sums use the complementary vector in the wrong factor

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.6–37, displayed formulas for K,L,Z, printed 792

Keep the stated summation range, but use D(n′)k(n-n′), D(n′)l(n-n′), and D(n′).

Every D(n-n′) in the displayed sums is evaluated at a nonempty specification with zero roots, hence vanishes; this contradicts a five-tree example with Z=1.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.6-37, p. 792: the convolution constraint f(n')=f(n) makes the complementary composition a nonempty zero-root forest, forcing its D value to zero. The printed Z sum therefore vanishes. For n_0=4,n_2=3, the listed degree words 2020200,2022000,2200200,2202000,2220000 have Z=1. The corrected sums use D(n')*k(n-n'), D(n')*l(n-n'), and D(n') respectively.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V2-C0032 — Wrong reference norm in the Cauchy inequality

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.3.3 · Answer 12 · Printed 573

The auxiliary proof states a false inequality; the exercise statement and its applications use the correct bound.

In answer 3.3.3-12, replace sigma(h,k,0)^2 on the right by sigma(1,k,0)^2. For h=2,k=5,c=1 the printed inequality becomes 9/25≤0. The two sawtooth vectors are permutations of the same vector, whose squared norm is sigma(1,k,0)/12. Rechecked against the original typesetting, exact arithmetic and all four errata layers on 6 November 2026. Latest physical printing and priority remain unverified.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The Cauchy bound uses the wrong first argument; h=2,k=5,c=1 makes the printed inequality false.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

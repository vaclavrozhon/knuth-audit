# V1-C0027 — The harmonic-sum program takes more than 65595u

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · §1.3.2 · Exercise 16 · Printed page 519

The stated 65595u is too small. Using the instruction costs on p.138, the code takes 65631u before OUT, or 65633u including OUT and HLT at 1u each, apart from the printer's physical delay.

The stated 65595u is too small. Using the instruction costs on p.138,
the code takes 65631u before OUT, or 65633u including OUT and HLT
at 1u each, apart from the printer's physical delay.

For n=2,3,4,5 the numbers of nonzero groups after the initial term
are 18,61,198,630, totaling 907. Counting the printed blocks gives:

START initialization             3u, once
  OUTER through JMP 1F            26u, four times
  INNER through LDX TEMP          43u, 907 times
  1H through JAP INNER            29u, 911 times
  LDA S through J1NN OUTER        26u, four times

Their sum is 65631u. Thus even excluding both OUT and HLT cannot
recover the printed figure. The difference 36u would result from
counting each of the four CHAR instructions as 1u instead of 10u;
this is only a possible explanation of the discrepancy.

All 37 byte radices give the same execution profile and correct
complete printed output. The group counts were independently
computed from the exact integer intervals on which the rounded
reciprocal is constant. The numerical sums themselves are correct.

No matching time correction was found in all1-prepre, all1-pre,
all1, or err1, searched by page and subject on 2026-11-01.
The adjacent 1999 erratum corrects answer17's harmonic-number symbols,
not this running time. Nothing has been sent; no priority claimed.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The printed blocks total 65631u before output, exceeding 65595u under either timing convention.

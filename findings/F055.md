# F055 — The definition omits required symbol counts

**Confirmed · high severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / SAT 212 introduction and (a,b), p. 335

Without them, equivalence with the tensor model fails.

The definition only specifies allowed symbols, not their required counts. Consequently, equivalence with exact marginals and the necessity of the equalities in (b) fail. For n=1, r=c=[1], p=[0], the empty table is valid but no tensor exists. The primary paper explicitly states the missing counts. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

SAT 212: the partial Latin-square definition restricts allowed colors but omits the cardinality requirements needed for the prescribed three-dimensional margins. For N=1, row and column margin one and symbol margin zero, the blank partial square satisfies the printed restrictions while no corresponding tensor exists. The cited Irving–Jerrum formulation explicitly includes the missing cardinality requirement.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

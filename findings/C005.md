# C005 — The shift can introduce internal zeros

**Candidate · high severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer MPR 125(b), p. 391

The conclusion depends on whether the no-internal-zeros condition is part of the definition.

A right shift can introduce internal zeros even when ca₁≤a₀². It remains unclear whether having no internal zeros is part of the definition or only an initial assumption. This occurs in 129 of 644 finite cases and is not a confirmed finding.

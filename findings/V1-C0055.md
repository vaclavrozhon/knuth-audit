# V1-C0055 — The header's right tag needs an exception

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · §2.3.4.2 · Answer 25 · printed 585

The claimed agreement with the standard header convention omits a second right-tag exception.

Let r be the arc R->H and h the added loop H->H. When h is last
in F_H=(r,h), the rules on page 584 give

ALINK(h)=r, BLINK(h)=h, BTAG(h)=1.

Thus there is an additional exception to convention 2.3.1-(8):
RTAG(HEAD)=1, whereas (8) on page 324 explicitly requires 0.
The stated exception RTAG=0 at the root is not the only one.
This already occurs for an original tree consisting of one root.

A correction can mention the head tag as well, or explicitly reset it to 0
when adopting the standard sentinel conventions. This objection
uses only the prescribed identification BTAG=RTAG, not an assumed
identification of ATAG with LTAG. The general graph representation
itself is consistent with its defining rules.

Original pages 324, 380, 584 and 585 were independently inspected.
No corresponding correction was found in the four errata layers.
No priority or physical-print claim is made. The November 5 sequential review reconfirmed the original location and general argument; all four refreshed errata layers still lack a repair. Accepted computations were not repeated.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The head-loop construction has an additional right-tag exception omitted from the claimed agreement.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

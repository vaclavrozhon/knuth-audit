# V2-C0257 — Incorrect indices and approximation orders in tensor construction

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · Section 4.6.4 · Answer 67 · Printed 716, 717

The printed constructions do not establish the stated result as written. Local repairs are given; the border-rank conclusions themselves are not refuted.

The proposed realization omits every product involving the last B variable. Its repair needs consistent one-based indices. The added output slice is an identity matrix, not an all-ones matrix, and the approximate construction drops a factor u^d from both stated leading orders.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 67 has inconsistent one-based indices in its length mn+1 construction, a C coefficient labelled B, missing diagonal indicators in parts (c) and (g), and a missing extra d in the approximation order in (g). The m=n=2 construction already fails as printed. Use the corrected indices, identity block, and order 2d+e+2 recorded in the report; the border-rank conclusions are not refuted.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

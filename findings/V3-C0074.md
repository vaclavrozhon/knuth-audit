# V3-C0074 — The three-gap normalizer uses the wrong tail

**Confirmed · low severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Answer 6.4-9, printed 729: definition of Q_k

Replace the tail index k-2 by k-1; the final ratio argument is unaffected.

# V4A-F0124 — The Schroder recurrence has n-1 instead of n+1

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.6–67, notes near the bottom of printed 798

Replace n-1 by n+1 on the left of the recurrence.

The recurrence for the little Schroder numbers has (n-1)S_n on the left; it must have (n+1)S_n.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.6-67, p. 798: the Schroder recurrence begins (n+1)*S_n, not (n-1)*S_n. At n=2 the printed recurrence asserts 3=9, contradicting the exercise's own values. The generating function is correct.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# F169 — The binomial-window probability is missing its centering

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / Mathematical Preliminaries Redux, answer96,p.384

Replace |X| by |X−n/2|. The corrected central-window statement and all four final classifications are valid; a positive-probability annulus completes the proof for part(iv).

Answer96 prints Pr(|X|<sqrt(n))=Theta(1), although X is the number of heads. For n≥16 this probability is at most exp(−n/8), and therefore tends to zero.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer MPR96 uses an uncentered binomial window. For the stated head count X, P(X<sqrt(n)) tends to zero, contradicting Theta(1). Replace X by X-n/2 in the window. The final classification is unchanged.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

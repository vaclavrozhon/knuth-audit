# V2-C0018 — The last labelled circular spacing is length-biased

**Confirmed · high severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.3.2 · Exercises 26(a),27; printed 78,566–567

The stated joint distribution and the first invariance claim use the wrong labelling model. Sorted-spacing formulas remain valid.

The last circular spacing contains the fixed origin, giving density n! S_n rather than (n−1)!. For n=2, S_1=|U_1−U_2| and Pr(S_1≥1/2)=1/4; the printed formula gives 1/2. Starting the labels at a designated observation repairs the model. Exercise 27 inherits the same issue. Thresholds must also be nonnegative, or clipped to zero. Rechecked against the first printing and all four errata layers on 6 November 2026. A recent physical printing and priority remain unverified.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The origin-containing circular gap is size-biased; the printed joint law fails already for two points.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

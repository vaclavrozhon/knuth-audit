# V4A-F0091 — The first row gap is two cells too large

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.5–29(b), row-gap term in text and displayed sum, printed 769

Replace both occurrences of i_2-i_1+1 by i_2-i_1-1.

The first gap is i_2-i_1-1, not i_2-i_1+1. The same error occurs in the exponent of the following sum.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.5-29(b), p. 769: rows i1 and i2 leave i2-i1-1 intervening rows, not i2-i1+1. With selected rows 1 and 3 the first gap contributes one free cell, not three. Both the prose and exponent need the sign change; the final Gaussian-binomial formula is correct.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

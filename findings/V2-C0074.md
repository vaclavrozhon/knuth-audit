# V2-C0074 — The last expansion index is missing n

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.6 · Exercise 14

The last term of the displayed recursive expansion is printed as X_{u_{k-1}}. It should be X_{n+u_{k-1}}.

The last term of the displayed recursive expansion is printed as
X_{u_{k-1}}. It should be X_{n+u_{k-1}}.

Already for k=2,n=1 the printed formula asserts X_201=X_1+X_126
modulo2, whereas the recurrence gives X_201=X_1+X_127. With X_27=1
and all other initial100 bits zero, these right sides are respectively
0 and1, and X_201=1.

The printed counts of sparse relations remain correct. They were
verified independently using binary linear algebra for all100 phases.
Original typography and all four errata layers plus saved current TeX
were checked. Later physical printings and priority are unverified;
this draft has not been sent.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The final discarded-bit index omits its n contribution. At k=2,n=1, with only initial bit X_27 equal to one, the printed relation predicts zero while X_201 is one. Replacing its X_126 by X_127 gives the correct relation. Retain as an index typo; the relation count is unaffected.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V4A-F0070 — Wrong coefficient in the upper-shadow formula

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.3–74, printed 738: final formula

For a single pair in a four-element universe, the formula gives four triples instead of two.

The coefficient of N must be n-1-n_t, not n+1-n_t. The printed expression overcounts the upper shadow by 2N.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.3-74, p. 738: n=4,t=2,N=1 gives an upper shadow of size two, while the displayed formula gives four. The coefficient is n-1-n_t, not n+1-n_t; the excess is 2N. The 2025 parentheses correction is unrelated.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V2-C0076 — 150 high halves do not guarantee reconstruction

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.6 · Exercise 7

The stated hypotheses do not guarantee recovery of X_1-X_0 from 150 values of floor(X_n/2^16).

The stated hypotheses do not guarantee recovery of X_1-X_0 from
150 values of floor(X_n/2^16).

Take X_0=0 and a=1. Both c=1 and c=3 give period exactly 2^32,
since c is odd. For 0<=n<150 their states are respectively n and
3n, so all 150 observed values are zero. Their first differences
are 1 and 3. No algorithm can distinguish these inputs, regardless
of running time.

The requested universal guarantee needs additional assumptions or
a qualification allowing ambiguous cases. This does not challenge
the intended warning against this encryption scheme.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The universal recovery claim fails for modulus 2^32, multiplier 1, initial state 0, and increments 1 versus 3. Both generators have full period and their first 150 high-16-bit observations are all zero; their first differences differ. Additional hypotheses or ambiguous outputs are necessary. This does not establish cryptographic safety.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

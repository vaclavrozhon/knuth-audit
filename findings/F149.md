# F149 — Four internal graph edges are drawn as cuts

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / figure (116), p. 116; answer 7.2.2.1–246, p. 479

A cut drawing contains an unnecessary removed edge; the displayed partitions and quoted costs remain valid.

AL–MS in the right figure 116, SD–WY in both left answer 246 diagrams, and IN–KY in the lower-right diagram are dashed even though their endpoints are joined by solid two-edge paths. They should be solid when depicting the induced components. The upper-left answer 246 drawing has 46 dashed edges, while its partition has 45 actual boundary edges and the stated cost 90. Both source versions share the defect.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Four dashed USA graph edges have endpoints already joined by solid two-edge paths: AL–TN–MS, SD–NE–WY (twice), IN–IL–KY. The diagrams therefore omit internal edges of the induced components. Restoring them preserves every partition and the correctly stated costs.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V3-C0031 — Large Quicksort thresholds can exceed the MIX address range

**Candidate · low severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Volume3 / ProgramQ, p.117, lines25 and27: effective addresses M+j and M+l

The literal parameter range permits an undefined intermediate address; whether the program intends such large thresholds is unresolved.

On binary MIX, N3000, M2000 and input(3000,1,...,2999) give j3000 in the first partition. Line25, DEC4 M,5, then forms effective address5000, exceeding the two-byte maximum4095 even though all records and code fit in memory. Volume1 explicitly makes such an address undefined. M+N<=4095 or two separate decrements resolves it; the latter adds2A to the timing. The recommended M9 is safe. Candidate status remains because the surrounding text may intend only small practical thresholds. Original typesetting and all five errata layers were checked.

This earlier candidate or dismissed entry was outside the 806 confirmed-card publication review.

# F036 — The middle factor is not a pyramid

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / SAT 339(b), p. 346

It has two maximal elements; the correct decomposition has four factors.

The middle factor b7 a8 c3 is not a pyramid: it has two maximal elements, a8 and c3. The correct four factors are b6 c2 e4 d1 · b7 c3 · f5 · a8. The general theorem remains valid. Confirmed in the PDF and the author’s PostScript.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Exercise 339(b): b7 a8 c3 has two maximal pieces, a8 and c3, because a and c commute; it is not a pyramid. The example needs four factors b6 c2 e4 d1, b7 c3, f5, a8 with top labels 1,3,5,8. The theorem is unaffected.

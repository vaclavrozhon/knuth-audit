# F083 — The trie labels are reversed

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2–33, p. 402

The counts are correct, but they are assigned to the wrong word lengths.

The word-length labels are interchanged: 6,347 nodes belong to five-letter words, and 63,060 compressed entries to six-letter words. Direct prefix and edge counts confirm all four numbers. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript; no matching erratum was found.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 33: the 6347-node trie belongs to five-letter words, while the 63060-entry compressed trie belongs to six-letter words. Prefix counts and the compression formula reconcile all four printed numerical sizes; only the two length labels are exchanged.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

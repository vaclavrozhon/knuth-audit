# V1-C0019 — Sign in the normal integral

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · §1.2.11.3 · Exercise 12

For negative x the printed answer has the wrong sign; it needs the factor sgn(x).

For real x, the answer needs the factor sgn(x):

integral_0^x exp(-t^2/2) dt
    = sgn(x) gamma(1/2,x^2/2)/sqrt(2).

The left side is negative for x<0, while the printed right
side is positive. The statement on page 122 imposes no
nonnegativity restriction on x. Alternatively, add x>=0
to the exercise statement. Both sides vanish at x=0.

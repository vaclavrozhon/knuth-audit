# V1-C0056 — The well-ordering construction has a node of level zero

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · §2.3 · Exercise 11, answer p.562

The proposed modification fails its stated requirement. A different construction using the lexicographic order on R × Z provides two children and continuum many ancestors at every node.

The note claims that every node has uncountable level after replacing the order by a suitable well-order. The least used index a0 gives a final segment T[a0] with no proper ancestor in the collection. Its level is zero. This contradicts the explicit universal claim independently of the terminology issue in V1-C0045.

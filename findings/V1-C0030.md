# V1-C0030 — The answer predicts incorrect outputs for nonpositive I1

**Confirmed · high severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · §1.4.1 · Answer 3, printed page 527

The answer gives the wrong maximum and index for a case explicitly required by the exercise.

Exercise 3 explicitly asks about nonpositive I1. Program M on page 145 does not replace it with n=1. With I1=0, X[0]=5 and X[1]=7, it returns A=5, I2=0 and I3=-1; the answer predicts 7, 1 and 0. For q=I1≤0, valid addresses and representable q-1, the actual results are A=X[q], I2=q, I3=q-1 and J=EXIT+1, with I1 and CI unchanged. The answer is correct for positive I1.

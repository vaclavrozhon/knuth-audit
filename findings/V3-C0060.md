# V3-C0060 — The AVL search counter omits a balanced root

**Confirmed · low severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: 6.2.3 p.470 equation (15), with Program A frequencies on pp.464–465

The literal definition makes a root search cost 10 units although the listed instructions take 12.

The root initialization is counted even when its balance factor is zero; D must be one plus the nonzero-balance nonroot nodes.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The AVL search count on p. 470 must count the root initialization even when the root is balanced. In the three-node tree with root 2 and leaves 1,3, searching for 2 executes 12 MIX time units; the printed definition gives D=0 and predicts 10, even assigning frequency D-1=-1 to a program line. Set D to one plus the encountered nonroot nodes with nonzero balance.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

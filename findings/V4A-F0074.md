# V4A-F0074 — The duality identity uses the wrong shadow degree

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.3–97(d), final identity, printed 743

Restore the +1 in the last kappa subscript; the duality result is unchanged.

The last identity uses kappa_(n-t) on combinations of size n-t+1. Its index must be n-t+1.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.3-97(d), p. 743: the duality inequality needs kappa_(n-t+1), not kappa_(n-t). For the complex consisting of the empty set and three vertices on a four-vertex ground set, at t=2 the printed inequality reads 2>=3.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

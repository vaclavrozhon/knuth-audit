# V4A-F0084 — The preliminary calculus formulas need a constant and a range

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.4–25(a), preliminary integral and derivative, printed 751

Add Li_2(e^(-t))/t to the integral and state the first derivative separately.

The definite integral omits its lower-limit term; the derivative expression also needs n>=2.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.4-25(a), p. 751: the definite integral is missing Li2(e^-t)/t; at x=1 the printed right side is nonzero. The derivative formula also fails at n=1, where f'(x)=-t/(e^(tx)-1). These are local calculus errors, not a refutation of the final coarse asymptotic estimate.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

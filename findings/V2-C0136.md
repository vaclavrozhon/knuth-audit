# V2-C0136 — The maximum division bound is off by one

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · Section 4.5.3 · Printed page 378

The claimed upper bound with a final−2 fails at T(5,3)=3.

The claimed upper bound with a final−2 fails at T(5,3)=3. The valid form is L(n)<log_phi(sqrt(5)n+1)−1, or the corresponding ceiling minus 2. The requested lower bound is correct and has a separate general proof.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

At n=3, T(5,3)=3, but the original upper bound log_phi(3*sqrt(5)+1)-2 is below three. The corrected final constant -1, or an added ceiling with -2, follows from the Fibonacci step bound. The separate lower-bound exercise is unaffected.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

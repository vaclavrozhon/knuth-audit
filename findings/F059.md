# F059 — The asymptotic constant needs a different power

**Confirmed · high severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–262(a), p. 483

At m = 1, the printed value is 5 rather than 1/√5.

The coefficient a_(2m) should be a_(2m)^(-1/2). For m=1, the printed expression gives 5 instead of 1/√5. Checked by a general product proof and 16 exact rectangles. Stanley’s original equation (2) and lemma support the correction; his equation (9) also has an error in the PDF. Confirmed on PDF p.483 and PostScript p.187.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

DLX 262(a): the asymptotic prefactor is a_(2m)^(-1/2), not a_(2m). At m=1 this follows immediately from the Fibonacci count and a_2=5. Stanley's cited paper has the same error in its equation (9); its equation (2) and preceding lemma give the correct factor. The cited erroneous formula is not independent verification.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

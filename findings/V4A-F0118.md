# V4A-F0118 — Two bounds are misstated in the Tamari meet-fiber proof

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.6–32, final line on 788 and semidistributivity paragraph on 789

Reverse s′′_k<=s_k to >=, and use A=F meet G=F meet H as the lower endpoint in the final paragraph.

The constructed maximal scopes are at least the starting scopes, not at most them. The semidistributivity application also retains F where its common meet should be substituted.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.6-32, pp. 788–789: the upper fiber bound has s'' >= s, not s'' <= s. When both inputs have scope vector 100, the maximum vector in the fiber is 210. The subsequent semidistributivity argument also needs the actual lower endpoint A=F meet G where the inherited interval notation uses F. The construction and lattice laws remain valid.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

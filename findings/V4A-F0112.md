# V4A-F0112 — The ballot-number specialization has too many leaves

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.6–37, parenthesis after the first displayed formula, printed 791

Replace n_0=(t-1)q+1 by n_0=q+1.

The specialization to C_pq^(t) requires n_0=q+1, not (t-1)q+1.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.6-37, p. 791: the ballot specialization needs n_0=q+1, not (t-1)*q+1. At t=3,p=1,q=2 the intended count is one, while the printed substitution gives three.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

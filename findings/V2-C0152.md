# V2-C0152 — The hardware throughput is converted to the wrong time

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · Section 4.5.4 · Printed page 390

At 6144 million tests per second,256 tests take 125/3 nanoseconds, about 41.7 ns

At 6144 million tests per second,256 tests take 125/3 nanoseconds, about 41.7 ns. The printed 5.2 ns is smaller by a factor of eight. The source hardware description supports the stated throughput.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

At 6144 million tests per second, 256 tests take 41.6667 ns, not 5.2 ns. Lukes's thesis confirms 32 chips, eight tests per chip per clock and a 24 MHz clock. The 5.2 ns figure instead corresponds to 32 tests. The separate AND correction does not repair the conversion.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

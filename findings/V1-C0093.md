# V1-C0093 — Consecutive triple-order visits can be the same node

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · §2.3.1 · Exercise 21 · printed 568

A minor error in one proof sentence; the algorithm and final distance bound are correct.

The proof says consecutive visited nodes are always parent and child. A singleton has triple order (P,P,P), so consecutive visits can instead be the same node. Adding that case fixes the sentence. The distance bound of at most three edges and Algorithm W remain valid.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Consecutive triple-order visits to a leaf can be equal; the distance bound remains correct.

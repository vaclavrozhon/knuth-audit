# F252 — Fresh solver seeds do not prevent overfitting to fixed instances

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / SAT509, answer 650/file 670

Distinguish seed noise from generalization to held-out formulas; actual overfitting is not asserted.

The answer confuses random solver runs with random training problems.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

SAT509 treats fresh random solver seeds as if they supplied fresh problem instances. A parameter choice can have cost 1+U on training instances and 100+U elsewhere, while a competitor costs 2+U everywhere, for fresh seed noise U. Rerandomizing U does not remove instance overfitting. This is a defect in the stated justification, not evidence that the historical measurements actually overfit.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

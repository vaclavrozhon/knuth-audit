# V4A-F0121 — The second moment and variance of forest path length are misstated

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.6–54, second display and final sentence, printed 795

Add the first derivative to obtain the raw second moment, and replace the variance by (5/6-pi/4)n^3+O(n^2).

The second derivative counts S(S-1), not S^2, where S is total internal path length. The stated variance also has n^(3/2) where the leading order is n^3.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.6-54, p. 795: the second derivative sums S*(S-1), not S^2, where S is total internal path length. For n=2 the raw sum is one and the printed expression zero. Add the first-moment sum. The variance grows as (5/6-pi/4)*n^3+O(n^2), not a constant times n^(3/2).

# V2-C0041 — The hexagonal basis has the wrong scale

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.3.4 · Hexagonal example · p. 109

The stated vector length gives the wrong lattice density; the general upper bound remains correct.

Two equal vectors of length l at 60 degrees have determinant l² sqrt(3)/2. The printed length 2/sqrt(3m) gives determinant 2/(sqrt(3)m), instead of 1/m. The correct length is sqrt(2/(sqrt(3)m)). The three-dimensional example and Hermite constants are correct. Original typesetting, primary reference constants and all errata layers rechecked on 6 November 2026. No applicable correction found; later physical printing and priority remain unverified.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The proposed hexagonal basis has determinant `2/(sqrt(3)m)` instead of `1/m`. Its length must be `sqrt(2/(sqrt(3)m))`. The stated Hermite constant and separate three-dimensional construction remain correct.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

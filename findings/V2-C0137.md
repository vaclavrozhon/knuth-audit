# V2-C0137 — The maximum continuant ordering fails with repeated entries

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · Section 4.5.3 · Printed page 378

For the allowed multiset {1,2,2,2,2}, the prescribed order gives K(2,2,1,2,2)=45, but K(2,1,2,2,2)=46.

For the allowed multiset {1,2,2,2,2}, the prescribed order gives K(2,2,1,2,2)=45, but K(2,1,2,2,2)=46. The maximum rule is proved after requiring distinct inputs. The minimum rule remains valid with repetitions.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The stated maximizing order for the permitted multiset {1,2,2,2,2} gives continuant 45; another ordering gives 46. The original hypothesis does not require distinct entries. The literature distinguishes the distinct-input Motzkin–Straus result from the later general problem. Retain the missing hypothesis; the minimum claim and the maximum with pairwise distinct inputs have supporting stored proofs. Do not claim a new general continuant theorem.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# F267 — Disabling wraparound jumps beyond the candidate arrays

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / SAT exercise 173(ii), p.331: disabling wraparound jumps past the arrays

An implementation error in the exercise instructions. The ordinary Algorithms X and Y handle the boundary correctly. Their reported timing measurements are not refuted.

Exercise 173(ii) sends an exhausted candidate scan to X6 or Y3, which reads the undefined entry at index S.

## Publication review, 17 November 2026

X4 defines LL[j] and LO[j] only for 0<=j<S. Follow X7 with j=S, or Y4 with j_hat=S: X6 and Y3 immediately index those arrays. End X normally and route Y to its Y6 finish step instead.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

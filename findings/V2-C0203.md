In the bracketed paragraph about irreducible quadratic factors, the displayed
sum is the probability of having no such factor. It should be preceded by 1-.
For p=n=2, only x^2+x+1 among the four monic quadratics has an irreducible
quadratic factor. The probability is 1/4, whereas the printed sum is 3/4.

The corrected probability is
  1 - sum_{0<=k<=floor(n/2)} binom(p(p-1)/2,k) (-1)^k p^(-2k).
The bounds and limiting estimate that follow are consistent with this correction.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The inclusion–exclusion expression in answer 4.6.2–1 counts the complement of having an irreducible quadratic divisor; insert 1 minus the printed sum. Over F2, among the four monic quadratics only x²+x+1 qualifies, giving 1/4 rather than 3/4.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V2-C0189 — U and V are exchanged when d=n

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.6.1 · Exercise 3

In the exception "d = min(m,n), say d = n", U and V are interchanged. The exercise asks for u*V+U*v = gcd(u,v). Taking u=x^2 and v=x, the printed U=0,V=1 gives x^2 instead of x. For monic v, use U=1,V=0; for a general v and a monic gcd, use U=1/leading(v),…

In the exception "d = min(m,n), say d = n", U and V are interchanged.
The exercise asks for u*V+U*v = gcd(u,v). Taking u=x^2 and v=x, the
printed U=0,V=1 gives x^2 instead of x. For monic v, use U=1,V=0;
for a general v and a monic gcd, use U=1/leading(v), V=0.

All five available errata layers were searched; no matching correction
was located. The latest physical printing has not been checked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

In the d=n termination branch of answer 3, U=0 and V=1 give u, whereas the gcd is the normalized v. For u=x², v=x the printed branch returns x². Interchange the roles, with the leading-coefficient normalization.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V2-C0124 — The golden-ratio example has the wrong right-hand side

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.5.2 · Exercise 26

The general functional identity is correct; its final worked substitution is not.

Substituting phi=(1+sqrt(5))/2 into the proved identity gives the right-hand side−2G(1+4phi), whereas the answer prints2G(2phi²). The corrected value is positive and the printed value negative because G is negative above1. This is an exact sign contradiction.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Direct substitution of phi=(1+sqrt(5))/2 into the correctly printed general identity gives -2G(1+4phi) on the right, not 2G(2phi^2). The tail function's sign for arguments above one distinguishes the two values. The general identity and proof survive.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

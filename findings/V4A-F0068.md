# V4A-F0068 — Wrong constant in the near-perfect path count

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.3–33, printed 730: first paragraph

The displayed formula overcounts by eight after doubling; the subsequent small-count table agrees with the corrected formula.

The path-count formula needs -2 in place of +2; n=3 gives 14 paths instead of six after doubling. The final endpoint inequality must also be j < i.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.3-33, p. 730: for n=3 the endpoint sum is three, not seven. Replace the constant +2 by -2; the resulting doubled counts agree with the table. The direction omitted by reversal is j<i, not the already counted j>i.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

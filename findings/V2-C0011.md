# V2-C0011 — The added π feedback mask has a nonzero fixed state

**Confirmed · high severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · 2013 public errata · Added answer 3.2.2–20

The recommended mask does not have the required full period.

The 2013 added answer recommends mask 0x243F6A8885A308D3. With the permitted state 0xE3EAD987836107B1, shifting and applying that mask returns the same state: the period is 1. The polynomial has factor x+1. Changing the last byte from D3 to C3 gives one independently checked repair. The faulty recommendation is absent from our original scan.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The published feedback mask has a nonzero fixed state, contradicting its required maximal period.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

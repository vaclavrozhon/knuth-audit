# V4A-F0086 — The partition polynomial uses n as its family index

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.4–31, first solution, printed 752

Replace f_(n,n mod k!)(n) by f_(k,n mod k!)(n).

The first subscript of f must be the fixed number of parts k, not the varying integer n.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.4-31, p. 752: the first index of the polynomial family must be k, not n. At n=3,k=2 the printed f_(3,1)(3)=2/3 cannot equal the single partition into two parts.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V4A-F0022 — A strong chain's right shift leaks between packed fields

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: Exercise 7.1.3-114 and its answer, printed pages 193 and 601: same-length packed evaluation

The copied construction fails, and the same-length assertion is false already for two two-bit fields. Boundary masks require extra operations.

The strong one-step chain f(x)=x>>1 on two-bit inputs cannot be copied to packed fields: input (01 00)_2 shifts to (00 10)_2 instead of zero. Fixed shift counts do not prevent bits from leaking between fields.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Exercise 114 and its answer, pp. 193 and 601: a strong two-bit chain consisting of right shift by one does not act independently on packed fields. The packed input 0100 shifts to 0010 rather than 0000. The one-operation obstruction in the note also refutes the exact same-length claim, not only the proposed construction.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V4A-F0051 — A valid blocker identity is crossed out

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.1.4-236(d), printed 669: classification of identity (xvi)

Remove (xvi) from the crossed-out list and remove its claimed exceptions.

The blocker of a union is the minimal family of joins of the two blockers, for all finite families. The answer incorrectly crosses out this identity and claims exceptions for empty families.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 236(d), p. 669: identity (xvi) is valid, including empty families. A set hits f union g exactly when it contains a minimal hit of each; minimizing their unions proves the identity. The blocker of an empty family is the unit family, so the asserted exception is also incorrect.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

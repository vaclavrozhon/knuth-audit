# V3-C0033 — The binomial-ratio Stirling correction has the wrong sign on x^4

**Confirmed · low severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Answer5.2.2-52, printed637/file648, displayed exponential expansion.

A local sign error in the intermediate expansion; the final answer retains its requested accuracy.

Combining the three 1/(12u) terms gives -x^2/(6n(1-x^2)), hence -(x^2+x^4+...)/(6n). The printed expansion instead has x^2-x^4.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

In the binomial-ratio expansion on p. 637, the term multiplying -1/(6n) has x^2+x^4+..., not alternating signs. Stirling's 1/n correction gives x^2/(1-x^2); terms of order n^-3 cannot change it. The final divisor-sum approximation to its stated precision is unaffected.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V1-C0016 — Incorrect limit of 1+1/u

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · §1.2.11.3 · Exercise 4

In the first sentence, "goes to zero" should be "goes to one". Indeed, v=u-log(1+u), with u>0, increases to infinity with u. Consequently 1+1/u tends to 1. The required boundedness and the exponential tail estimate remain correct.

# V1-C0051 — The time bound ignores the length of the cycle output

**Confirmed · high severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · §2.3.4.2 · Answer 11 · printed 581

Explicitly printing the requested cycles can take quadratic time on a sparse graph, contradicting both stated bounds.

The exercise on page 377 asks for the fundamental cycles to be printed.
Steps C5-C6 explicitly print each edge of each cycle, with its sign.
Consider the input arcs, in this order,

(1,2),(2,3),...,(n-1,n), (1,3),(1,4),...,(1,n).

There are m=2n-3 arcs. The first n-1 form the accepted spanning path.
The later fundamental cycles have lengths 3,4,...,n. Their total
printed length is L=n(n+1)/2-3=Theta(n^2). Consequently the stated
O(m log n) bound cannot hold for this output contract, regardless
of improvements to connectivity bookkeeping.

The proposed DFS alternative needs the same qualification. In the
underlying fan graph, vertex 1 has degree at most three in every
DFS spanning tree: before reaching it, the DFS path is an interval
of vertices 2,...,n, leaving at most two unvisited path components.
Removing 1 from the DFS tree leaves at most three paths of sizes s_i.
Their attachment positions give

sum_{v=2}^n dist_T(1,v) >= sum_i s_i^2/4 >= (n-1)^2/12.

At most three spokes (1,v) belong to the tree. The other spokes alone
produce cycles of total length at least (n-1)^2/12+(n-1)-6.
Thus every DFS tree in this sparse family requires Omega(n^2)
explicit output. This is not a lower bound for arbitrary cycle bases:
a non-DFS star spanning tree yields short triangles.

A precise replacement is: DFS constructs a spanning tree and its
non-tree arcs in O(n+m) time, representing the cycles implicitly.
Explicitly listing these cycles requires and permits O(n+m+L) time,
where L is their total length. The original C1-C6 time claim should
be removed or replaced by a justified bound for its actual contract.

The cited Tarjan primary text constructs a compact palm tree and
fronds; it does not list all edges of all fundamental cycles.
All four errata layers were searched without a matching correction.

# V4A-F0014 — Two determinant products have the wrong signs

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / answer7.1.2-70, p.575

The circuit gives a wrong determinant-sign result. Swapping two product definitions preserves the stated cost23.

The printed y2=x1*x6*x8 is a negative determinant term, while y5=x3*x4*x8 is positive. The matrix with rows100,001,010 has determinant-1 but the printed comparison and final circuit return1. Swap the definitions of y2 and y5.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.1.2-70(c), p. 575: two determinant products have the wrong signs. For the matrix with rows 100,001,010 the determinant is -1 but the printed circuit reports positivity. Interchanging products y2 and y5 fixes this sign mismatch.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

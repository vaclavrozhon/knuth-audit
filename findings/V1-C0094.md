# V1-C0094 — A2 moves a nonzero polynomial into the new child

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · §2.3.3 · A2 · printed 358

A misleading explanation of a correct insertion step.

The explanation calls the newly inserted child a dummy zero polynomial. The assignments instead copy the old CV and DOWN of Q into R. With P=x and Q=1, R represents 1 and has exponent zero; exponent zero is not a zero coefficient. The full algorithm correctly returns x+1. A clearer explanation would say that the old polynomial is moved into an exponent-zero child. The original page, general assignment argument and a small trace agree. No repair appears in the four refreshed errata layers. Physical printing and priority remain unverified.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The newly inserted child holds the previous nonzero coefficient; the explanation alone calls it zero.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

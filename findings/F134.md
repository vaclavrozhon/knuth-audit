# F134 — One triangle is rotated incorrectly in the third tetrahex

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–140(c), p. 454, third tetrahex

Two shared vertices have conflicting colors in the printed example. The stated count of 48 solutions is correct.

In the third figure of answer 140(c), the rightmost downward-pointing triangle has white at its right corner instead of its bottom corner. Rotate this tile 120 degrees clockwise to repair both white/pink conflicts. All 24 tile types remain present exactly once. A standalone certificate checks all corners and all 48 single-tile rotations; two complete independent censuses include the repaired figure. The same defect is present in the original author PostScript. No corresponding correction was found in the current and historical errata refreshed on November 2, 2026. A physical copy and priority have not been checked.

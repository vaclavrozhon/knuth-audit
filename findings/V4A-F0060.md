# V4A-F0060 — Gray traversal of Sims factors needs conjugation

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.2–52, printed 710: final paragraph on Gray traversal of arbitrary Sims tables

Using the stated tau transitions directly can repeat permutations. Gray traversal of the product representation remains valid with the required conjugation.

A Gray change of a middle Sims factor requires P tau P^-1, not tau alone. A three-element table already gives repeated permutations under the stated unconjugated updates. Say conjugates of tau or its inverse.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.2-52, p. 710: changing a middle factor s in P*s*Q requires premultiplication by P*(s'*s^-1)*P^-1. The reflected Gray path need not make P identity. The three-symbol witness requires (1 2), absent from the advertised tau values and inverses. Conjugation is missing.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# F7-C0006 — A clasp edge has a half-edge arrow where it needs a plus

**Confirmed · low severity.** Published fascicle · Digital release 1 · 2025.

Location: Fascicle 7 / 7.2.2.3, Fig. 104(c), p11

Replace this one arrow by a convex-edge tick. The outline and the stated 15 T junctions are correct. The alternative arrow passes the local V constraint, so abstract CSP validity alone does not certify the pictured solid.

The downward arrow above the lower inner V in Fig. 104(c) labels an edge as a half edge. Its two incident faces, identified in the original transparent diagram 102(c), project onto opposite sides of that visible edge; both are front-facing. The convex rim therefore needs a plus. Original typesetting, exact visibility and all relevant errata were checked; the main reviewer has independently accepted this finding.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The specified downward arrow in Fig. 104(c) lies on a visible edge whose two incident faces are both front-facing. It must be the convex-edge mark, not a half-edge arrow. The claim concerns the depicted clasp; it does not rule out a different spatial scene with an abstractly admissible labeling.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V1-C0021 — The PRIME=99 erratum leaves dependent examples unchanged

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · §1.3.2 · 2022/2026 corrections and dependent pages 149, 152, 511

Applying the later correction leaves the explanatory listing inconsistent with the relocated program.

The 2022 correction moves PRIME from −1 to 99; the 2026 follow-up changes PRIME+L to 599. Several dependent occurrences still describe the old layout: the negative-symbol comment on page 149, Fig. 15 on page 152 and five entries in answer 4 on page 511.

In the answer, address 499 becomes 599 at 3004; address −1 becomes +99 at 3010, 3012 and 3020; the constant at 0000 moves to 0100. The literal −499 at 2051 remains correct because it represents 1−L. Both versions generate the same primes.

This concerns incomplete propagation of later errata. The original examples agree with the original program.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Unchanged dependent examples conflict after the two published relocations; retain only as incomplete errata propagation.

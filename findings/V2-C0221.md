cahier 37 (1858), 1-34. Research-paper bibliographies instead cite
Rouche, "Memoire sur la serie de Lagrange", volume 22,
cahier 39 (1862), 193-224, for this theorem.

The two original articles have not been obtained here. Before
proposing a correction, check the 1858 article and the theorem
on pp. 217-218 of the 1862 article. A different usual citation
alone does not prove the printed reference is wrong.

# V2-C0252 — Radix-four FFT count includes two extra multiplications

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · Section 4.6.4 · Answers 14 and 53 · Printed pages 700 and 710

Subtract 2 from the exact complex count. The propagated 1024-point totals become 10928 real multiplications and 25944 real additions. Transform correctness is unaffected.

The answer omits products by ±1 and ±i, yet its four-point count is 2 instead of 0. For every even n>=2, the displayed expression exceeds the count of its radix-four stages by exactly 2.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Under the stated free quarter-turn convention the radix-four FFT count on p. 700 is two too large for every even exponent n>=2. A four-point transform uses zero counted products, not two. The count is (9n−26)2^n/24+4/3; the propagated 1024-point totals on p. 710 become 10928 real multiplications and 25944 real additions. Transform correctness is unaffected.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V2-C0014 — The pole substitution also needs the corrected sign

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · Answer 3.2.2–37 · p. 559

The published sign correction leaves one dependent step unrepaired.

The 2005 erratum changes denominators to x+uⱼ on p.558. The next page still substitutes x=uⱼ; it must use x=−uⱼ to isolate the coefficients. For p=7,a=c=1 and lag2, the pole is 6 rather than 1. The hyperplane theorem remains correct.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The initial pole sign was corrected in 2005, but the next page still substitutes the wrong pole.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

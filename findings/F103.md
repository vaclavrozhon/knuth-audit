# F103 — The Strand citation gives the wrong year

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2–32, p. 402, Strand reference

The reference directs the reader to 1919 instead of the July 1918 issue.

In answer 32, change Strand 56 (1919), 74 to Strand 56 (1918), 74. The original volume title and July contents both give 1918, and page 74 contains the exact NESTLES word square quoted in the book. The incorrect year appears in both the 4B PDF and the author’s PostScript. No matching erratum was found; the physical copy has not been checked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 32: Strand volume 56 is July–December 1918; its page 74 contains the specified NESTLES word square. The printed second citation's 1919 should be 1918. The archived title and contents pages corroborate the year.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

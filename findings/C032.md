# C032 — The residual 1% for three or more nut-puzzle solutions is a coarse estimate

**Candidate · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–137(c), p. 452; exercise p. 140

The exact proportion is 0.3450337671%; interpreting the final 1% as its own estimate overstates the frequency by roughly a factor of three.

An exact census gives 205,252,200 sets with at least three equality solutions out of all 59,487,568,920 seven-nut sets. Every one of the 5,623,238 nonempty color-renaming orbits was checked with a second complete solver and all 720 relabelings. A separate enumeration by shared edge labels confirms completeness. The four proportions are 93.195198%, 5.345689%, 1.114080%, and 0.345034%. The book gives coarse values 93%, 5%, 1%, and a residual 1%. This remains a candidate for improved precision: the exact counts are established, but the intended tolerance of the rounded statement is unspecified. The 2019 author draft has the same wording, and no corresponding published correction was found.

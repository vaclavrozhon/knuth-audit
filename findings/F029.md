# F029 — The proof starts with the wrong assumption

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer SAT 306(h), p. 609

The required index exists under E∞ = ∞.

The assumption N*=∞ does not ensure the required n′: the example from 306(j) has ell(2)=4 and every q_N ell(N)<3. The initial assumption should be E∞=∞; the theorem remains valid. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 306(h), p. 609: N*=infinity does not imply the claimed n' exists. The book's own example (j) has ell(2)=4 and q_N ell(N)=3-2^(2-N)<3. Begin instead with E_infinity=infinity and argue by contraposition; the theorem itself is correct.

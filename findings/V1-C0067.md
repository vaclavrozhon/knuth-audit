# V1-C0067 — A valid buddy at the pool boundary is excluded

**Confirmed · high severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · Exercise 26 · page 611

A valid free buddy is ignored, causing avoidable allocation failure.

A buddy starting at P with length 2^k fits whenever P+2^k≤M. The printed test treats P≥M−2^k as unavailable and wrongly rejects equality.

In a six-word pool, freeing adjacent units at 5 and 4 should form the two-word block [4,6). The printed rule prevents that merge. Replace ≥ by >.

The original pages and all four current/historical errata layers were reviewed on November 5, 2026. This does not establish priority or inspection of a later physical printing.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The generalized buddy test rejects a buddy ending exactly at the pool boundary.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

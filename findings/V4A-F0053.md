# V4A-F0053 — The vector-addition loop never advances

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.1.4-255(b), printed 675: bit-vector addition loop

Insert k <- k+1 after computing the sum family and next carry.

The loop initializes k=0 and repeats while k<m, but never changes k. For m>0 it repeatedly processes bit zero.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 255(b), p. 675: k starts at zero and the while-k<m body never changes it. For positive m the loop does not terminate. Increment k after computing the sum and carry. The full-adder formulas themselves are correct.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

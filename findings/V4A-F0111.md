# V4A-F0111 — The forest generator leaves its initial prefix undefined

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.6–21, step G1, printed 784

At the end of the initialization loop, set b_1 through b_(l-1) to zero.

Step G1 omits the first f-1 zero degrees when the forest has f>1 roots; these entries are visited before being assigned.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.6-21, p. 784: step G1 leaves b_1,...,b_(f-1) undefined for a forest with f roots. With three leaves and two unary nodes the first output must be 00110; the first two entries have not been assigned. Initialize this prefix to zero. The single-tree case is unaffected.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V2-C0005 — Periodic extension need not preserve the recurrence at zero

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.2.1.1, Answer 12(d) · p. 546

The proof needs a consistent initial representation or an extension defined through the congruential states.

The allowed initialization x₋₁₀=2, all other supplied digits zero and b₀=1 gives X₀=1 and x₀=1. Extending only negative indices periodically changes x₋₁₀ to 1 while leaving b₀ unchanged; the recurrence would then require x₀=0. The final radix identity remains true, but this intermediate proof step fails.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Extending only the negative indices can make the borrow recurrence fail at zero, although the final identity remains correct.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

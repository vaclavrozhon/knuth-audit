# V2-C0243 — Does the opening definition require periodic extension?

**Candidate · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.3.4 · Introductory definition · pp. 94–96

Candidate clarification: covering the finite point set alone can allow arbitrarily large spacing, while the later lattice definition is precise.

For the full-period generator m=2, a=1, c=1, the two points are (0, 1/2) and (1/2, 0). Every family x+y=1/2+kR covers them for any R>0, so its spacing R/sqrt(2) is unbounded. After all integer translates are included, the standard dual condition is u+v=0 modulo 2 and nu2=sqrt(2). Pages 96–98 explicitly introduce the periodic lattice, so this remains an interpretive candidate about the preliminary wording, not an algorithm defect. No applicable clarification found in the errata reviewed on 6 November 2026.

# V1-C0054 — Arguments to the feedback function are reversed

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · §2.3.4.2 · Answer 23 · printed 584

The printed correspondence fails for a function with maximum period; the final count remains correct.

Replace f(x_1,...,x_k)=x_(k+1) by f(x_k,...,x_1)=x_(k+1).
The recurrence in the exercise on page 380 lists the newest argument
first; the consecutive arcs in the answer list entries oldest first.

For m=k=2 and f(u,v)=1-v, the exercise generates 00110011...,
of maximum period 4. But the printed correspondence forces the arcs
00 -> 01 -> 10 -> 01 -> ..., which repeat without visiting 11.
It therefore does not produce the claimed Eulerian closed trail.
The corrected argument order gives precisely the stated recurrence.
The final enumeration of functions remains unchanged.

Original pages 380 and 584 were independently inspected. All four
errata layers were checked; their page-584 corrections concern
other text and a figure. No priority or physical-print claim is made. The November 5 sequential review reconfirmed the original location and general argument; all four refreshed errata layers still lack a repair. Accepted computations were not repeated.

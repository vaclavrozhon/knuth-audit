# V4A-F0092 — The additional rook identity needs an offset and a factor

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.5–29(c), additional generating-function identity, printed 769

Use the corrected offsets and divide the right-hand side by 1-z, with k ranging from 0 to m.

The additional identity uses a_j+m-j+t instead of a_j+j-m+t and omits a factor 1/(1-z) on its right side.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Additional identity after answer 7.2.1.5-29(c), p. 769: one row of length one shows a missing factor 1/(1-z) on the right. Shape (2,1) shows that the product offset must be a_j+j-m+t, not a_j+m-j+t. These are defects in the additional identity, separate from the known correction to the exercise's factorization. The upper summation index should be m.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

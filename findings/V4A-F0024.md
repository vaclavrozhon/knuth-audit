# V4A-F0024 — The parabola curvature is understated by a factor of four

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.1.3-183, printed page 614: maximum curvature of a parabola

The error affects the numerical test for the stated curvature-based sufficient condition for Algorithm T.

The stated maximum curvature alpha/2 for y=alpha*x^2 should be 2*abs(alpha). The following numerical example is about 21.08, not 5.27.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 183, p. 614: the maximum curvature of y=alpha*x^2 is 2*abs(alpha), not alpha/2. The corresponding translated and rotated numerical example has maximum curvature about 21.08, not 5.27.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V4A-C0003 — Global uniqueness does not ensure the cut function used in Theorem M’s proof

**Candidate · medium severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / §7.1.4, pp.214–215, proof of Theorem M

The table-transfer step needs a stronger subnetwork assumption or another argument; the stated BDD bound is not disproved.

Consider three modules: M1 outputs u=v; M2 outputs a=u and v=u XOR b; M3 outputs b=a XOR x3 and f=a. Every complete input has the unique consistent assignment u=v=a=x3, b=0. Yet the left two modules have two possible a values when the cut input b=0 and no solution when b=1. Thus they do not define the function A(b), independent of the right inputs, assumed by the proof on pp.214–215. Original typesetting and five relevant errata layers were checked. Kept as a candidate because an implicit stronger interpretation of the network model may be intended; this is not a counterexample to the bound itself.

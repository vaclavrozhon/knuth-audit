# V4A-F0062 — Cumulative Cayley step count needs an additive term

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.2–74, printed 714: cumulative alpha-step count

The displayed recurrence is false; the corrected recurrence gives the stated Hamiltonian-cycle criterion.

For r=g/c, k(j+r)=k(j)+k(r), not k(j). A three-element cyclic group with an all-alpha Hamiltonian cycle has r=1 and k(j)=j. The 2008 Fascicle 2 erratum repeats the error.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.2-74, p. 714: k(j) counts all alpha steps in a prefix, so periodicity of the choices gives k(j+r)=k(j)+k(r), not k(j+r)=k(j). In the order-three cyclic example r=1 and k(1)=1 while k(0)=0. The 2008 replacement repeats the defect.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

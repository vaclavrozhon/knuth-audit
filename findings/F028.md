# F028 — The probability threshold is shifted

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer SAT 307(b), p. 609

The product corresponds to T_(j−1), not T_j.

P_j is Pr(X>T_(j−1)), not Pr(X>T_j). At j=1, the empty product is 1, whereas a simple example gives Pr(X>T1)=1/2. The subsequent proof uses the product correctly. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 307(b), p. 609: the product of the first j-1 failure probabilities is Pr(X>T_(j-1)), not Pr(X>T_j). Already j=1 has empty product one versus tail probability 1/2 in the preceding example. The subsequent expected-cost expansion uses the correct product.

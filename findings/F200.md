**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / SAT answer 64, printed559–560/file579–580: Wrong index for trailing-one constants

Use at least n+1−j trailing ones for wire j; otherwise 0101 gives conflicting constants.

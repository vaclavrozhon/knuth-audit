# V3-C0069 — The general-alphabet search expansion keeps a binary coefficient

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Answer 6.3-28, printed 726: successful-search normalization and general-M logarithmic correction

Correct the normalization and replace (log_M N)/N by (log_M N)/((M-1)N).

The intermediate expression omits division by N, and the expanded logarithmic-over-N term needs coefficient 1/(M-1).

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 6.3-28 omits division by N in the U_(N+1) term of the average. Also the coefficient of log_M(N)/N is 1/(M−1), not 1. In the exact product formula, writing u=M^(−j) gives (1−product_(r≥1)(1−u M^(−r)))/u=1/(M−1)+O(u); summing the occupied scales gives log_M(N)/(M−1)+O(1) in the total. This is a successful-search correction.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

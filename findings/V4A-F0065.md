# V4A-F0065 — Wrong matching-cost square-root coefficient

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.2–91: asymptotic cost, printed 718

The leading correction is wrong; M=O(N) remains valid.

For n pairs, M/N=1+sqrt(pi)/(2 sqrt(n))+O(1/n), not 1+2/sqrt(pi n)+O(1/n). The next-to-last double factorial supplies the leading correction.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.2-91, p. 718: for n pairs, the penultimate summand divided by N is (2n-2)!!/(2n-1)!!=sqrt(pi)/(2sqrt(n))+O(n^(-3/2)). The others contribute O(1/n). The printed coefficient 2/sqrt(pi) is incorrect; the linear-time conclusion remains valid.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V3-C0005 — The vacated cell is one row above the final pointer

**Confirmed · low severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Volume 3 / §5.1.4, p. 56

The evacuation instruction names a cell outside the original shape.

After Algorithm S finishes on a nonempty tableau, its last S5 has advanced r. The vacated cell is P_(r−1),s, not P_rs. For the one-cell tableau (1), the algorithm ends at (r,s)=(2,1) after clearing P11; the next-page instruction would place the deleted entry in P21. Algorithm S itself deletes correctly. Original typesetting and all relevant errata checked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

After Algorithm S stops, its final S5 has advanced r. Thus the vacated cell used on p. 56 is (r-1,s), not (r,s). Even a one-cell tableau demonstrates the mismatch. The deletion algorithm itself is sound.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

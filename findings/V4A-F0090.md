# V4A-F0090 — The crossing and nesting pairs must be consecutive in their blocks

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.5–27, crossing/nesting definitions, printed 768

Add the consecutive-element condition to both definitions.

Membership in the same block is insufficient for the claimed duality. Each selected pair must be an arc between consecutive elements of that block.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.5-27, p. 768: crossing/nesting endpoint pairs must be consecutive within their blocks. Under tableau transposition, the partition with blocks {1,5},{2,3,4} maps to {1,3,5},{2,4}. At the cut 2, the latter has the printed broad nesting (1,5),(2,4) while the former has no corresponding crossing. The pair (1,5) skips the middle element 3 and is not an arc.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V3-C0003 — The inversion generating function is missing a monomial factor

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Volume 3 / answer 5.1.2-14, p. 599

The printed formula omits the fixed inversions between blocks of equal top-row letters.

With n12=n21=1 and n11=n22=0, the only permitted word is ba, so its inversion generating function is z; the printed quotient equals 1. In general multiply the quotient by z^K, where K is the sum of nij*nkl over i<k and j>l. The intended symmetry under matrix transposition remains valid. Original typesetting and all relevant archived errata layers checked; no matching correction found.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The fixed-matrix inversion generating function on p. 599 omits the inversions between different blocks. The matrix n12=n21=1, with other entries zero, has one inversion, while the printed product equals 1. A factor z^K is needed, where K is the number of these forced inversions. The transpose-symmetry conclusion remains valid.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

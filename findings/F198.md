# F198 — McGregor coloring bound needs rounding

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / SAT exercise 18, printed 318/file 338: McGregor coloring bound needs rounding

Use ceil(5n/6); the answer’s coloring distribution is correct.

For n=8, at most 5n/6 permits six uses, but every four-coloring needs at least seven of every color.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

SAT18: at n=8 the printed 5n/6 bound permits only six uses of a color, whereas the answer's table requires seven. The archived n=8 CNF has an independently verified DRAT unsatisfiability proof for six; the displayed construction has class sizes (20,23,22,7). Use ceil(5n/6). General optimality remains outside this finding.

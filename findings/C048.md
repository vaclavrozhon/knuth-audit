# C048 — The cited prime-number error bound has the wrong exponent

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / Mathematical Preliminaries Redux, notes after113, p.388

The note overstates the cited mathematical result. Its exponential-growth conclusion and the combinatorial argument remain valid. No disproof of the stronger asymptotic formula is claimed.

The note prints exp(−C log n) and attributes this fixed power saving to the 1896 paper. The original sources instead give a later bound with sqrt(log n) in the exponent.

## Publication review, 17 November 2026

Read the 1896 reprint, p.62 (PDF p.71), and the 1899 paper, §55, p.54 (PDF p.67), with its introduction on p.6. Use exp(−c sqrt(log n)) and cite 1899, or retain the 1896 reference and write psi(n)=n+o(n).

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

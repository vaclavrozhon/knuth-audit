# V2-C0219 — The final Perron inequality needs correction

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.6.2

In the lower bound, replace r^3,...,r^n by |r|^3,...,|r|^n. The real number r may be negative. For u=x^3+4*x+1 and r=-1/2, |1+4*r^2+r^3|=15/8, whereas the printed lower bound is 17/8. This checks the algebraic inequality, not the assumed-root condition.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The lower bound in the Perron proof, p. 690, needs absolute values on the powers of r. For u=x³+4x+1 and r=−1/2 the two sides of that algebraic inequality are 15/8 and 17/8. This r is a counterexample to the intermediate inequality, not a claimed root of u; the irreducibility theorem remains true.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

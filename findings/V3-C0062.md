# V3-C0062 — The path-length bound needs a maximum over heights

**Confirmed · low severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Answer 6.2.3-4, printed 714: min over heights in the path-length bound

The extremum in the proof is reversed; the subsequent asymptotic bound remains the intended one.

A bound proved for each fixed height must be maximized over feasible heights to bound all trees; the answer instead prints min_h.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The fixed-height path-length upper bound on p. 714 must be maximized over feasible heights to bound all N-node AVL trees. The printed minimum does not follow from the preceding fixed-height extremal property. Maximization gives the stated asymptotic scale, so the final asymptotic theorem is not being rejected. The 1996 first-edition addendum repeats the minimum.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

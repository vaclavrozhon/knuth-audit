# V2-C0163 — The2019 addition mistypes a Mersenne exponent

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume2 · published addition to p.409

This is an uncorrected defect inside an erratum, supported by the original announcement. It is not an already-published correction to hide.

The addition prints77232017 instead of77232917. The printed exponent equals19*29*140167, so its Mersenne number has the proper divisor524287.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The 2019 erratum prints exponent 77232017 instead of 77232917. GIMPS's January 3, 2018 announcement gives the latter and the December 26, 2017 discovery date. The wrong exponent equals 19*29*140167 and therefore cannot define a Mersenne prime.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

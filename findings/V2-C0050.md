# V2-C0050 — The Gauss-sum formula needs a zero-frequency exception

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.3.4 · Answer 28 · p. 583

The exact magnitude is wrong at k=0, while the upper bound used in the following argument is still valid.

For the trivial multiplicative character, k=0, the sum over all nonzero residues is -1 and has magnitude 1. The displayed sqrt(p) magnitude applies only to nontrivial characters. The example p=3,a=2 already shows the exception. Both cases satisfy the upper bound sqrt(p), preserving the subsequent discrepancy estimates in their stated period and dimension scope. Original pages and all errata layers rechecked on 6 November 2026. Later physical printing and priority remain unverified.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The Gauss-sum equality requires a nontrivial character. At frequency zero the sum is -1, of magnitude 1 rather than `sqrt(m)`. The upper bound needed for the discrepancy argument survives.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

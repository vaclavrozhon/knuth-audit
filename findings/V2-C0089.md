# V2-C0089 — The interval upper endpoint must end in 9

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · See attached source pages

The replacement interval-sum upper endpoint excludes permitted endpoint combinations. This is distinct from adding two known exact SI constants.

The updated intervals on p240 are

N = [(24,.60221407)..(24,.60221408)],
 h = [(-33,.66260701)..(-33,.66260702)].

The replacement for p241 gives the interval sum with the same upper
endpoint as N. Under the directed interval addition defined on p240,
that endpoint should instead be (24,.60221409).

Indeed, N_hi is representable and h_hi is strictly positive but less
than one ulp at N_hi. Thus upward rounding of N_hi+h_hi must return
the next representable number, not N_hi. The corrected sum is

N (+) h = [(24,.60221407)..(24,.60221409)].

The lower endpoint and the displayed interval product are correct.
Both the original example and the 1999 replacement correctly incremented
the upper endpoint in the analogous addition.

This concerns the stated interval operation. The narrower printed
interval may still contain the sum of the exact fixed SI constants when
one uses that additional information beyond the two input intervals.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

In the 2020 interval-sum replacement, the upper endpoint .60221408 is already exactly representable and the added upper endpoint is strictly positive. Directed upward rounding therefore requires .60221409. This concerns the specified interval operation; it does not claim that the narrower interval excludes the exact sum of the known physical constants.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

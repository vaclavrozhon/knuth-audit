# V4A-F0039 — A dependency claim in the uniquely thin proof is false

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.1.4-135, printed page643: cofactor dependencies

The auxiliary proof assertion needs an additional case; the uniquely thin conclusion is not refuted.

For n=5, fixing x1=0 gives theta5=x5 AND x4 AND NOT x3, which does not depend on x2. This is outside the sole stated exception and makes the claimed chart edge weight wrong.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 135, p. 643: the recurrence for the purported uniquely thin example gives theta5 restricted to x1=0 as x5 AND x4 AND NOT x3, independent of x2. Thus the claimed dependency exception is not unique, and the indicated weight is one rather than two. The existence of other uniquely thin functions is not refuted.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

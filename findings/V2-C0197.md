The asserted coefficient bounds N^3, N^7, N^17 omit factors caused by addition. With
  u=x^3+x^2+x+1, v=x^2-x-1,
all input coefficients have absolute value at most N=1, but
  u=(x+2)v+(4x+3).
The next pseudo-remainder is the constant 5, so every degree drop is 1 as assumed. The first claimed bound N^3=1 already fails.

For a degree drop of 1, a valid bound is 4AB^2 when the current inputs have coefficient bounds A,B. Thus (2N)^alpha_i/2 is a valid bound, with alpha_1=alpha_2=1 and alpha_i=2alpha_(i-1)+alpha_(i-2). The qualitative exponential growth conclusion is preserved.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The literal coefficient bounds N³,N⁷,... omit constants coming from additions. Inputs x³+x²+x+1 and x²−x−1 have coefficient bound 1 but produce remainder 4x+3, then 5. The qualitative warning about exponential coefficient growth remains valid.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

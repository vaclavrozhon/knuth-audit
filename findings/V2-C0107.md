# V2-C0107 — The exact bit bound is not established by the cited algebraic bound

**Candidate · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.3.3 · Exercise 15

Candidate: the quoted article includes extra logarithmic factors in its stated bit-cost result. This does not prove that the sharper bound is false.

The2020 correction uses O(R_*(n)), the algebraic complexity in van der Hoeven2014. For binary p-adic multiplication, its Theorem17 instead directly gives O(R_*(n)(log log n)^2 log log log n). An additional specialization or argument may remove those factors; none is established in the saved evidence. Retain this as an unresolved source/model question, not a confirmed impossibility result or a solved research exercise.

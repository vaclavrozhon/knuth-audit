The quotation attributed to Oscar Wilde (1894) has "thing nowadays / that".
The original publication has "thing that nowadays":

"It is a very sad thing that nowadays there is so little useless information."

Source: "A Few Maxims for the Instruction of the Over-Educated",
The Saturday Review 78, no. 2038 (17 November 1894), pp. 533-534,
specifically p. 533, right column, fourth maxim.
Full issue:
https://archive.org/download/sim_saturday-review-uk_the-saturday-review-of-p_1894-11-17_78_2038/sim_saturday-review-uk_the-saturday-review-of-p_1894-11-17_78_2038.pdf

The original article is unsigned at both its beginning and its conclusion
on p. 534. Attribution to Wilde therefore requires a separate bibliographical
source; the original scan itself proves the wording, not that attribution.

Suggested correction: move "that" before "nowadays".
The original page scan and the 1997 TAOCP page were inspected directly.
No matching correction was found in the four available errata layers.
This identifies a textual discrepancy; a different authorized reprint has
not been ruled out by an exhaustive edition history.
The textual difference is established; its classification as an error
rather than an editorial adaptation or variant remains open.

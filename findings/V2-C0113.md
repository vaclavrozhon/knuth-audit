# V2-C0113 — The maximum intermediate-value formula has a small-bound exception

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.5.1 · Exercise 7

The formula misses N=2; its general result for N≥3 is correct.

For N=2 the valid fractions1/1 and1/1 give the addition intermediate t=2, while (N−1)^2+(N−2)^2 gives1. State N≥3 or handle N=2 separately. The archived argument proves the exact printed maximum for every N≥3.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

At N=2, the legal sum 1/1+1/1 has intermediate numerator two, exceeding the claimed maximum one. The formula works for N>=3. Retain only as a small-format exception.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

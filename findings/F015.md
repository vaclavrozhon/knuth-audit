# F015 — The difference needs a positive part

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer MPR 137(d), p. 395

An intermediate step is wrong; the main claim remains valid after correction.

For x<m, the expression needs a positive part: c−m+2 max(0,x−c). A valid median example gives Δ=−1 instead of the printed −3. The theorem and subsequent bound are correct. Confirmed only in the PDF so far. No matching correction was found in the complete 4B and Fascicle 5 errata; the physical copy has not been checked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer MPR 137(d), p. 395: for x<m the formula needs c-m+2*max(0,x-c). With median m=1, c=0, x=-1, the difference of distances is -1, not -3. The later lower bound and theorem remain true.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V4A-F0054 — Both descendant-family extrema are wrong

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.1.4-267, printed 678: minimum and maximum ZDD size

Correct both extrema; the stated auxiliary recurrence and comb construction support the repair.

The minimum is n+2, not n. The maximum is floor(n^2/4)+n+1 for n>=2, not floor(n^2/4)+2n+1; for n=1 it is 3. Both n=2 forests have size 4.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 267, p. 678: the minimum descendant-family ZDD has n+2 nodes, not n. The printed maximum already fails at n=2, where both forests give four rather than six. The stated auxiliary recurrence gives maximum floor(n^2/4)+n+1 for n>=2, with value three at n=1.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

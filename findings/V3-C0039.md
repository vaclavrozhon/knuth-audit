# V3-C0039 — A binary leaf bound is applied to ternary comparisons

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Answer 5.3.1-11, printed655: invalid binary lower bound for ternary comparisons

Invalidates the printed lower bound and proof of the fixed-alphabet asymptotic; the sharp asymptotic is not established here.

The stated lower bound fails for every n>=2 when the alphabet has two values.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The weak-ordering argument on pp. 194 and 655 applies a binary information bound to comparisons with three possible outcomes. For two possible distinct values, Q=2^n-1 weak orderings give ceil(lg Q)=n, but the preceding construction uses n-1 comparisons. The claimed binary lower bound is false; this does not refute the separately stated sharp fixed-m theorem.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

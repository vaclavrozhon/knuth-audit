Candidate only: the recorded evidence does not yet establish a book error.

The replacement bibliography gives Commentarii volume 8 as
"8 (1735), 9-22" for Euler's E47. The original volume's title page
reads TOMUS VIII, AD ANNUM MDCCXXXVI, and bears the imprint 1741.
The nominal volume year is therefore 1736. Euler Archive separately
dates composition of E47 to 1735 and publication to 1741.

Possible clarification: "8 (1736), 9-22", or explicitly distinguish
the date of composition from the nominal volume year.

Original source, PDF page 7 (title) and pages 23-36 (printed 9-22):
https://archive.org/download/commentariiacade08impe/commentariiacade08impe.pdf
Euler Archive record:
https://scholarlycommons.pacific.edu/euler-works/47/
Public replacement:
https://www-cs-faculty.stanford.edu/~knuth/all1-pre.pdf

Limits: this is a candidate about the date convention, not an objection
to the mathematical citation or to the known delay before publication.
The 1997 first printing cites only volume 6. The intended convention
and a complete later-print book have not been verified. No later
matching correction was found in all1-prepre, all1-pre, all1, or err1
as checked on 2 November 2026. The report has not been sent.

# V2-C0190 — Shift in the polynomial gcd reduction

**Candidate · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.6.1 · Exercise 6

Direct reversal of division requires m-n+1. The printed equation with m-n can describe another valid gcd reduction, so this is an interpretation issue rather than a proof that the equation is impossible.

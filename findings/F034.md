# F034 — An edge weight is missing from the recurrence

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer SAT 341, p. 615

Two vertices with weight 2 give x² − 2 rather than x² − 1.

The first recurrence is missing the factor w_ij before W(S\{i,j}). Two vertices with w12=2 give x²−2 rather than the printed x²−1. The theorem about roots is valid; a general proof and exact Sturm tests were checked. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 341: the recurrence omits w_ij before W(S minus {i,j}). With two vertices, diagonal weights one and off-diagonal weight two, the defined polynomial is x^2-2 but the recurrence gives x^2-1. The next paragraph uses the weights correctly.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

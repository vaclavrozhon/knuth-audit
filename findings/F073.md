# F073 — The worst-case claim for each level is false

**Confirmed · high severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–216, p. 473

A particular edge order of K₇ gives a different profile.

The claim of a worst case at every level fails for K7 with first edges 12,13,23: its profile is 1,6,2,4 instead of 1,6,4,2. Checked by a direct tree and the original DLX2. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

DLX 216: lexicographic option order is not worst at every level. On K7, placing edges 12,13,23 first and then using lexicographic order gives profile (1,6,2,4), compared with (1,6,4,2). Both trees have thirteen nodes; the witness does not claim a larger total tree.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

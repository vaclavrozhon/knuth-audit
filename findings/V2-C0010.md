# V2-C0010 — The attributed Reiser result needs primitivity

**Confirmed · high severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · Exercise 3.2.2–26 · p. 39

The printed generality does not match the cited theorem. This does not solve the intended primitive-polynomial conjecture.

For Xₙ=4Xₙ₋₃ modulo 3ᵅ with seed (0,0,1), the least period is 3ᵅ and it contains 2·3ᵅ⁻¹ zeroes. This exceeds every fixed multiple of 3^(α/2) as α grows. The primary Reiser report assumes a primitive characteristic polynomial; the exercise omits that condition while describing degree three as established.

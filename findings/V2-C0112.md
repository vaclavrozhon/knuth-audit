# V2-C0112 — The replacement round-trip example contains three typos

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.4 · Exercise 18

The numerical example added in the1999 erratum has incorrect exponents and a binary digit string. The general round-trip theorem remains correct.

For the stated ten-bit input, u=1017·2^774, the decimal intermediate is .1011·10^237=1011·10^233 and the returned binary mantissa is .1111111010. The replacement text prints236 and235 where237 is required, and an eleven-digit mantissa. Exact midpoint inequalities certify the corrected conversion. The claimed smallest example must be restricted to positive integers; unrestricted real counterexamples occur arbitrarily close to zero.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The 1999 replacement needs decimal exponents 237 in both places and binary mantissa 1111111010. Exact integer midpoint comparisons were freshly checked for u=1017*2^774 and U=1011*10^233: 2021*10^233 < 2u < 2023*10^233 and 2035*2^774 < 2U < 2037*2^774. The earlier smallest-example claim is restricted to positive integers, not arbitrary real floating-point values.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

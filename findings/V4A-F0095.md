# V4A-F0095 — The scaled factorial approximation has the wrong correction

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.5–40, final correction factor, printed 771

Correct both the sign and the position of n.

The printed factor 1+n/12+O(n^-2) must be 1-1/(12n)+O(n^-2).

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.5-40, p. 771: Stirling's formula for c^(n-1)/(n-1)! gives multiplier 1-1/(12n)+O(n^-2), not the diverging 1+n/12+O(n^-2). The leading factor and saddle point are correct.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

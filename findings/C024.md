# C024 — Ambiguous uniformity in the random Dominosa model

**Candidate · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / p. 131, exercise 7.2.2.1–72(ii); answer on p. 431

Uniform arrangements favor matrices with more solutions; uniform distinct solvable matrices give a different distribution.

Question 72 describes equally likely matrices, while its answer samples equally likely complete domino arrangements. A matrix with S solutions then has probability S/N. The two matrices in exercise 70 have 2 and 1 solutions, so the answer generates them in a 2:1 ratio, versus 1:1 under uniform sampling of distinct solvable matrices. The mathematical distinction is exact, but matrix may be shorthand for an arrangement with visible domino divisions. This remains a wording candidate; the N/M expectation is correct.

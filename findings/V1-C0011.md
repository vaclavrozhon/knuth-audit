# V1-C0011 — The 2024 Laplace-transform addition gives an insufficient convergence condition

**Confirmed · high severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · 2024 errata addition to §1.2.9 · Exercise 13

Positive Re(s) does not ensure that the integral exists for an arbitrary generating function with positive radius of convergence.

The explicit false guarantee was added in the January 1, 2024 errata. It is absent from the 1997 exercise, which asks for the transform formula.

Take a_k=2^k and s=log 2. The generating function has radius 1/2, but the integral from 0 to N equals (N−1+2^(−N))/log 2 and diverges.

A sufficient condition is sigma=Re(s)>0 and exp(−sigma)<R, where R is the radius of convergence. Absolute integrability then justifies G(exp(−s))/s.

The expression called the exact probability is only asymptotic.
Writing a_j for the number of monic irreducibles of degree j and k_j
for the requested number with multiplicities, the exact expression is
p^(-n) product_j binom(a_j+k_j-1,k_j), where sum_j j*k_j=n.

For p=2,n=2,k_1=2, three of the four monic quadratics have two linear
factors with multiplicities, so the probability is 3/4. The printed
formula gives 1/2. The limiting permutation-cycle formula remains correct.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 37 calls an asymptotic expression exact. The exact count uses multisets: p^(−n) times the product of binomial(a_j+k_j−1,k_j). For p=2 and two linear factors it is 3/4, not 1/2. The limiting permutation-cycle law is unaffected.

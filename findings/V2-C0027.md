# V2-C0027 — Partition reciprocity is missing a parity sign

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.3.2 · Answer 30; printed 569

The exact identity fails for even n; the asymptotic substitution remains valid.

With quasipolynomial continuation, q_n(m)=(−1)^(n−1)p_n(−m), m>0. For n=2,m=3, q_2(3)=1 whereas p_2(−3)=−1. The missing sign cancels the sign of (−m)^(n−1) when deriving the final asymptotic formula. Rechecked against the first printing and all four errata layers on 6 November 2026. A recent physical printing and priority remain unverified.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The quasipolynomial reciprocity identity is missing its parity factor.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

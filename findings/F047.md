# F047 — The construction produces only tautologies

**Confirmed · high severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / SAT 363(g),p. 620

The negative literals are indexed by the wrong set.

The negative part of the clause should be indexed by L, not L′. Since l∈L′, the printed construction produces only tautologies. The family {∅,{1},{1,2}} gives an exact counterexample. The correction was proved generally and checked on 639 families with ground-set size at most 4. This is separate from the known 2024 terminology change in the exercise. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 363(g): index the negative literals by L, not L'. Since the positive literal belongs to L', every printed clause is tautological. The family empty,{1},{1,2} supplies an explicit failure; the corrected clause x1 or -x2 excludes the unwanted predecessor.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

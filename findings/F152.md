# F152 — Circular seating uses 6n−9 cache entries, not 6n+9

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–260, p. 481

The stated cache count is 18 too large. The algorithm still runs in linear time.

Answer 260 gives 6n+9 memos for the circular reseating problem. An explicit classification of all states gives 6n−9, including the empty memo. For n=4 there are 15, not 33. Both implementations agree for n=3..24. The ZDD-size formula is correct; the update formula needs n>=4, with 46 updates at n=3.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 260: the six explicit families of residual states total 6n-9 including the empty state, not 6n+9. The small n=4 instance has 15 memos rather than 33. The printed node count is correct; the update formula needs n>=4, with 46 updates at n=3.

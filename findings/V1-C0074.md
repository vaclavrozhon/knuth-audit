# V1-C0074 — Does the buddy sentinel also require a nonzero link?

**Candidate · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · Exercise 27 · pages 611–612

The overflow check depends on more than the sentinel sign alone.

A positive sentinel tag with a zero forward link becomes negative zero after LD4N. J4NN still branches, so the intended overflow stop can be missed. A nonzero self-link prevents the problem.

The extra sentinel’s self-link may be implicit in the list-head convention. This remains a candidate about an unstated initialization condition, not a confirmed failure under the intended convention.

# F080 — The state count has the wrong binomial term

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–259(b), p. 481

The expression should be binom(n+1,2) + 1.

The mem count in 259(b) is binom(n+1,2)+1, not binom(n,2)+1. There are l+1 states at level l, plus the empty state. At n=2 this gives 4 instead of 2. The verbal description of Y also needs a complement; the other numerical formulas are correct. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript, with two computations.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

DLX 259(b): levels ell=0 through n−1 contribute ell+1 cache states, followed by the empty state. Thus the count is binomial(n+1,2)+1. At n=2 the four states already contradict the printed two. The accompanying signature description uses covered Y items, and needs explicit complementation to agree with the active-item convention.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

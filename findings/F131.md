# F131 — The November 1965 citation has the volume digits reversed

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / exercise 7.2.2.1–120, p. 138

The reference points to volume 231 instead of the correct volume 213.

The publisher’s November 1965 issue page and the deposited metadata for Hao Wang’s Games, Logic and Computers identify Scientific American 213(5). Both our local PDF and the author’s PostScript print 231. No matching correction was located in the full current and historical errata.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Exercise 120: Wang's November 1965 Scientific American article is in volume 213, issue 5, pages 98–106, not volume 231. Fresh Crossref metadata for DOI 10.1038/scientificamerican1165-98 agrees with the archived primary bibliographic evidence.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V1-C0017 — The remainder integral needs an absolute value

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · §1.2.11.3 · Exercise 8

In the symmetric integral estimating |R(x)|, the numerator u^(m+1) should be |u|^(m+1). When m is even, the printed integral vanishes identically.

In the symmetric integral estimating |R(x)|, the numerator
u^(m+1) should be |u|^(m+1). When m is even, the printed
integral vanishes identically.

For example, choose f(x)=1, r=0, s=2, so that m=2. For u>0,
log(1+u/x)>u/x-u^2/(2x^2); hence g(u,x)<h(u,x) and the
difference R(x) is strictly positive for every x>1. But the
displayed integral from -1 to 1 of u^3/x^2 is zero.

With the absolute value, the desired bound follows and the
exercise statement remains valid.

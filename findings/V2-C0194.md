# V2-C0194 — The probability denominator must be |S|^n

**Confirmed · high severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.6.1 · Exercise 16

In the displayed probability bound, the first denominator should be |S|^n, rather than |S|. The numerator counts vectors in S^n.

In the displayed probability bound, the first denominator should be |S|^n, rather than |S|. The numerator counts vectors in S^n.

For f(x_1,x_2)=x_1 and S={0,1}, there are two zero vectors. The printed left-hand side is 1, while the next expression is 1/2. With denominator 4 the two sides agree. The same missing exponent occurs in the April 20, 1995 replacement for answer 16 on p. 620 of the second edition.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The final probability bound in answer 16 divides the number of zero tuples by the size of S, instead of its nth power. For f(x1,x2)=x1 and S={0,1}, two zero tuples give the printed left side 1 against a right side 1/2. The induction and the intended root bound are sound.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

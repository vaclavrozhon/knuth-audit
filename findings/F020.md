# F020 — A minus sign is needed before the integral

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / p. 148, exercise 189

Even the constant term has the opposite sign.

R(z) needs a minus sign before the integral. The coefficient of z^0 should be −g; the printed expression gives +g. The sign of the z^1 coefficient is also reversed. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript; the absolute-value estimates remain valid. No matching correction was found in the complete 4B and Fascicle 5 errata; the physical copy has not been checked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Exercise 189, p. 148: R(z) needs a minus sign in front of the integral. At z=0 its definition is -g, whereas the printed integral is +g. Absolute-value estimates survive.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

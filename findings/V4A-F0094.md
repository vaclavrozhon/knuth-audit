# V4A-F0094 — The restricted-growth maximum is off by one

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.5–38(b), first sentence, printed 771

Replace max(a_1,...,a_n)<=m by max(a_1,...,a_n)<m.

The condition k_i<=m corresponds to maximum digit strictly less than m, not at most m.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.5-38(b), p. 771: entries bounded by m correspond to restricted growth digits strictly below m. For n=3,m=2 the printed non-strict bound admits 012 and hence the forbidden cycle 333. The final Stirling-number sum is correct.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

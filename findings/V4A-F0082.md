# V4A-F0082 — The second-smallest-part estimate has the wrong exponent

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.4–50(f), first estimate, printed 755

Use O(n^(-(k-1)/2)); also write G_k(z), not G_k(n), after coefficient extraction.

The stated O(n^(-k/2)) estimate already fails for k=1, where the normalized coefficient tends to 1.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.4-50(f), p. 755: at k=1 the coefficient ratio is 1-1/p(n), which tends to 1, not O(n^-1/2). The fixed-k exponent should be -(k-1)/2; the displayed argument G_k(n) should also be G_k(z). The later estimate for c(m)/p(m) remains consistent with the repair.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

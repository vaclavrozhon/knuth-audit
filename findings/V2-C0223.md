The date "in 1793" is retained in the correction of June 17, 2002.

Mignotte and Stefanescu, Revue d'histoire des mathematiques 7 (2001),
page 68, distinguish Schubert's presentation on June 19, 1794 from
publication in 1798. Their bibliography on page 89 dates the journal
volume 1793. The dates refer to different events.

This does not rule out discovery in 1793. The original article and
Academy records have not been examined, so no definite error is
claimed. A clarification might be useful if the date is revisited.

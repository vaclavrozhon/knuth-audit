# V3-C0076 — Ordered hashing uses the wrong table-size index

**Confirmed · low severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Answer 6.4-66, printed 741: unsuccessful and insertion costs

Use C_(N+1) for the unsuccessful search and correct the Nth-insertion convention.

C_(N-1) predicts one probe with two keys present, although some searches require three.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The ordered-hashing unsuccessful-search mean in answer 6.4-66 must use C_(N+1), not C_(N−1). For N=2 the latter equals 1, contradicted by the positive probability of requiring further probes. The insertion sentence also needs a consistent N versus N+1 convention.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

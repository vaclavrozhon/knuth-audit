# V2-C0036 — The 2003 correction omits a potency-two family

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.3.4 · Answer 3 · 2003 erratum replacing p. 577

The replacement for answer 3 omits m=8q, a=4q+1. In this full-period family both squared spectral accuracies are 2, rather than 6 and 4.

For m=8, a=5, c=1 the orbit is 0, 1, 6, 7, 4, 5, 2, 3. The dual vector (1, 0, -1) has squared length 2, and no unit vector is admissible. The same proof applies to every m=8q, a=4q+1. The original printing had a missing 1+ in this binary exception; the 2003 erratum replaces all three exception lines with two ternary lines and drops the binary family. The issue here is in the replacement. Rechecked against original typesetting and all four errata layers on 6 November 2026; physical later printing and priority remain unverified.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The July 2003 replacement of the last lines of p. 577 omits a binary family of potency-two exceptions. For `m=8q,a=4q+1,c=1`, the dual vector `(1,0,-1)` has squared norm 2; for `q=1` the full orbit is `0,1,6,7,4,5,2,3`. Padding with zero also gives the four-dimensional exception. The complaint concerns the replacement text, not the superseded original typo.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

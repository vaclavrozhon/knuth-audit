# F009 — The asymptotic estimate overlooks rounding

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer MPR 127(b), p. 391

The stated error term is too small.

Rounding in 127(b) creates a nonzero bounded term. An exact ratio of binomial coefficients for θ=1/3 proves the limit −1/3. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript; no matching correction was found in the 4B or Fascicle 5 errata. The physical copy has not been checked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer MPR 127(b), p. 391: at theta=1/3, n=3k+1, the omitted rounding contribution tends to -1/3; at n=3k it tends to zero. It cannot fit O(1/n). Add -frac(theta*n)*log_2((1-theta)/theta), or use O(1). The final order estimate is unaffected.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

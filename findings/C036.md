# C036 — Boundary question: the bishop formula needs even n ≥ 4

**Candidate · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–172(a)(iii), p. 464, general bishop formula

An editorial domain question about 2×2 boards; the 8×8 results are correct.

The answer states 2^(n−3) classes of bishop paths of length 2n−4 when n is even. For n = 2 this gives half a class, while the actual longest path has length 1. A direct proof using diagonal incidence confirms the formula for all even n ≥ 4. Because the exercise itself asks about 8×8 boards, the restriction to larger boards may be implicit; this remains a candidate.

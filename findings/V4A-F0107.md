# V4A-F0107 — The Bell ratio estimate linearizes an exponent outside its small range

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Equations 7.2.1.5–(50) and (51), printed 427

Keep the correction in an exponential and use a remainder that accounts for logarithmic coefficients.

For k of order sqrt(n), the ratio correction tends to sqrt(e), while (50) predicts 3/2. The fixed-k remainder and the intermediate error in (51) also need revision.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Equations (50)–(51), p. 427: writing A=k*xi*(k*xi+k+1)/(2*(xi+1)^2*n), the normalized Bell ratio is exp(A+error), not uniformly 1+A+O(k^3/n^2). At k=floor(sqrt(n)), the former tends to exp(1/2), the latter to 3/2. Even for k=1 the next coefficient grows like -xi/12, so the printed O(n^-2) omits a logarithm. The final weaker bound in (51) survives.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

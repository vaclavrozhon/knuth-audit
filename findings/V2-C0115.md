# V2-C0115 — The zero-rounding region needs a qualification for small formats

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.5.1 · Exercise 12

The smallest floating-slash formats are exceptions; the practical27-bit and58-bit formats are unaffected.

For q=1 the only finite value is zero. At x=1 its neighboring infinity wins the mediant tie because its denominator is smaller, contradicting the printed closed zero region. The zero interval is(-1,1). For q=2 the signed-numerator tie rule gives another endpoint exception. The stated closed formula is correct for q≥3.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

For q=1 the finite nonnegative value is zero and infinity is representable. At x=1 the mediant tie rule chooses infinity, which has smaller denominator, whereas the stated zero region includes this endpoint. The definition gives no q>=3 restriction. The signed q=2 interpretation is secondary; the q=1 witness suffices.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

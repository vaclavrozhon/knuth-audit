# F7-C0018 — The concatenation reduction can have exponential output size

**Confirmed · low severity.** Published fascicle · Digital release 1 · 2025.

Location: Fascicle 7, answer 364, printed 239/file 254: final polynomial-time parenthetical

Remove or qualify the polynomial-time assertion. The satisfiability equivalence and the exercise permitting inefficient constructions remain valid.

Concatenating m two-tuple binary relations creates 2^m tuples of length 2m. An explicit family has O(m log m)-bit input, so the printed construction is not polynomial-time in unrestricted input size.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 364’s explicit-table construction can have exponentially many tuples: m distinct two-tuple relations yield a product with 2^m tuples. Its unqualified polynomial-time parenthetical is false when the input relations are part of the input. Satisfiability equivalence and the exercise’s permission to ignore efficiency remain valid.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

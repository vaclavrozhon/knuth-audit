Please add "nonconstant" to the polynomial in the exercise. Under the
definition on pp. 421-422, the constant polynomial 2 is irreducible in
Z[x], but is a unit in Q[x], hence is not irreducible there. The answer's
assertion that u is primitive also requires this restriction.

With the positive-leading-coefficient convention for pp on p. 423, the
answer's first displayed equality additionally needs a possible minus
sign: u = +/- pp((m*v)(n*w)). For example, take u=v=-x-1 and w=m=n=1.

All five available errata layers were searched; no matching correction
was located. The latest physical printing has not been checked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The irreducibility equivalence in 4.6.1–8 needs a nonconstant hypothesis. The definitions on pp. 421–422 explicitly allow constant irreducibles such as 2 in Z[x], but 2 is a unit in Q[x]. In the converse proof, taking the primitive part also requires the sign normalization already defined on p. 423.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

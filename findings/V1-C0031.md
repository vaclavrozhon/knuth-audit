# V1-C0031 — SMAX1 omits its change to I1 from the exit conditions

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · §1.4.1 · Answer 4, printed page 528

A caller relying on the documented register preservation would overlook that SMAX1 sets I1 to 1.

SMAX1 executes ENT1 1 although its entry conditions do not restrict I1. Its exit conditions omit that change; page 188 explicitly promises preservation of unmentioned registers. For n=1 and initial I1=2, the returned I1 is 1. Add I1=1 for the SMAX1 entrance; the general SMAX entrance preserves I1. The maximum and final I3 formula are correct. This is a small contract omission. Original pages and all four current errata layers were compared on November 5, 2026; no matching correction was found.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

SMAX1 changes an ordinary register omitted from its exit contract.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# F180 — Khinchin proof uses the wrong product limit

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / MPR answer145, printed397/file417: Khinchin proof uses the wrong product limit

Change the upper limit to n. With n=3,m=2 and k=(0,0,2), the printed formula gives3 instead of1. The theorem is unchanged.

The coefficient-ratio denominator runs through m, although there are n coordinates.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

MPR145: the product has one factor for each of the n coordinates, not m. At n=3,m=2 and k=(0,0,2), the printed ratio is 3 while the actual ratio is 1.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

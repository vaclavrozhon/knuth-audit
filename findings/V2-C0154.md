# V2-C0154 — The corrected probability formula gives a strict zero inequality

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · Section 4.5.4 · Printed page 661

The 2002 correction multiplies both sides of a strict inequality by a compositeness indicator

The 2002 correction multiplies both sides of a strict inequality by a compositeness indicator. For prime n=3 both sides are zero, giving 0<0. A non-strict inequality repairs this line; the main error bound below 1/4 is valid.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Multiplying both sides of a strict probability inequality by the nonprime indicator produces 0<0 when n is prime, for example n=3. Non-strict inequality repairs that displayed comparison. The intended overall probability bound is unaffected.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

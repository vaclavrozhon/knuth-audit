# V1-C0053 — Missing alternating sign in the coefficient estimate

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · §2.3.4.4 · Answer 4(g), page 588

The intermediate estimate is incorrect. The final positive asymptotic formula in the exercise on page 396 is correct.

The answer on page 588 omits the factor (−1)^(n−1). Its displayed estimate is negative for every even n, although the coefficients being estimated are positive.

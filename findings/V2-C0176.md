# V2-C0176 — The Fibonacci search loses its stated upper invariant

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.5.4 · printed 666

The proof invariant fails even after the published2002 correction. This does not alone assert a wrong final f(35) output.

For n=35, the allowed initialized state has the required endpoint residues. After the printed update, the new upper central-binomial residue is20 rather than0.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

For n=35, s=5,s'=8,m=8 satisfies the stated initial binomial residues. The corrected Fibonacci update moves m to 11, whose new upper endpoint 14 has residue 20 modulo 35 rather than zero. The base is large enough to exclude coefficient carries. Restricting the search to the factorial-coprime interval repairs the invariant. This is a proof defect, not a claim that the trajectory ultimately returns a wrong factor or that Shamir's theorem fails.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

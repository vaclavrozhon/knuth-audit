# V2-C0106 — The cited source gives a stronger bound for the stated tape model

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.3.3 · Exercise 15

The lower bound labeled best is weaker than the result already in the cited paper for standard multitape machines.

The answer labels Ω(n log n/log log n) as the best known lower bound for multitape Turing machines. Paterson–Fischer–Meyer1974, Theorem2(ii), gives Ω(n log n) for one-dimensional multitape machines; its weaker Theorem2(i) applies to a broader model. Fischer–Stockmeyer1974 explicitly makes the same distinction. The weaker inequality is true; the defect is the best-bound claim and model attribution. The full external lower-bound proof has not been independently reconstructed.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The cited 1974 paper's Theorem 2(ii) explicitly gives Omega(n log n) for the ordinary one-dimensional multitape Turing-machine model, whereas the book calls the weaker Omega(n log n/log log n) bound the best known one. The latter belongs to the source's broader machine class. The theorem and model definition were re-inspected. Retain as a source/model correction, not a new complexity lower bound. The 2020 erratum only updates the upper bound.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

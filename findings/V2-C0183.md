# V2-C0183 — The BBS membership test samples the wrong distribution

**Confirmed · high severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.5.4 · printed 668

The printed majority test can be wrong with overwhelming probability. A gcd-aware sampling repair is proved; this is not a claim of breaking BBS.

Uniform x does not make x^4 uniform over all quadratic residues. For the valid modulus209, an oracle with uniform-Q success31/60 has sampled success93/209.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Q_m explicitly includes nonunits, and uniformly squaring residues does not sample it uniformly. For m=209 the stored predictor succeeds on 31/60 of uniform Q_m but only 93/209 of the printed samples, so the majority in part (a) can favor the wrong answer. The gcd-based repair transfers advantage to units or finds a factor. Merely restricting samples to units without transferring the premise is insufficient. The existence of the intended reduction is not refuted.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

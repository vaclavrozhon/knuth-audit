# V4A-F0105 — The derivative bound has a sign and power error

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.5–56(c), first inequality chain, printed 776

Use 0<(-sigma)^(n+1)*g^(n+1)(sigma)<n!*(1-beta/alpha), for n>=1.

The printed chain has a positive lower bound and zero upper bound. The correct chain uses (-sigma)^(n+1) and puts zero on the left.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.5-56(c), p. 776: the printed upper bound is negative, although its middle expression is asserted positive. Parts (a) and (b) give 0 < (-sigma)^(n+1) g^(n+1)(sigma) < n! (1-beta/alpha). Restore the missing power and the sign of this bound.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

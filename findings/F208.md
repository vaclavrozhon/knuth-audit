# F208 — General-clause lookahead discards its retained undo prefix

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / SAT answer175, printed582/file602: General-clause lookahead discards its retained undo prefix

After restoration set E←G and retain G; otherwise a later lookahead treats a two-literal residual clause as a unit.

The restoration loop preserves visible literals, but the unchanged initialization G←E←F then overwrites their stack entries.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

SAT175 restores expired clause reductions, then unchanged routine (72) resets G to F and loses its retained visible prefix. The four-literal witness loses a's undo record and later reaches a spurious unit case. Retain G and set E←G, also in double lookahead. SAT11K does so.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

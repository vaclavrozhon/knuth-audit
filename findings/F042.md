# F042 — The claim overlooks isolated vertices

**Confirmed · high severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / SAT 356, p. 349

Covering every edge does not ensure the required positivity.

The edge cover overlooks isolated vertices: the empty graph on two vertices with U1={1}, θ11=0, and p=(0,1) satisfies the assumptions but has M=0. Require a cover of all vertices, or p_i<1 for uncovered vertices. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Exercise 356: covering edges does not necessarily cover isolated vertices. With two isolated vertices, U1={1}, theta11=0, p=(0,1), all stated parameter conditions hold but the Mobius polynomial is zero. Cover all vertices or require p_i<1 separately for uncovered ones. This retained baseline finding concerns only the omitted isolated-vertex condition.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V4A-F0104 — The higher Taylor terms have the opposite sign

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Equation 7.2.1.5–(41), sign before the sum, printed 425

Change the minus sign immediately before the summation to plus.

The Taylor sum starting at k=3 must be added, not subtracted.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Equation (41), p. 425: Taylor's formula requires a plus before the sum of higher derivatives. The printed minus reverses every term of order at least three. At sigma=log(2) the third derivative is nonzero. The later normalized expansion and first correction use the correct signs.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

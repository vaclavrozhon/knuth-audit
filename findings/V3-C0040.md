# V3-C0040 — An average lower bound incorrectly includes a ceiling

**Confirmed · low severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Exercise 5.3.1-23, printed196: ceiling of lg(n!) is not an average lower bound

A local introductory error; the exercise about distribution sorting and its answer remain valid.

The worst-case integer ceiling is reused for an average, which need not be an integer.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The average comparison lower bound on p. 196 cannot include the ceiling: three distinct elements can be sorted in an average of 8/3 comparisons, below ceil(lg(3!))=3. The non-rounded information lower bound remains valid.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

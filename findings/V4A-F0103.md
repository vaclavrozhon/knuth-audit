# V4A-F0103 — The Newton denominator has the wrong exponent sign

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.5–55, Newton iteration, printed 775

Remove the minus sign in the denominator exponent.

The denominator must be 1-c*exp(beta_k), not 1-c*exp(-beta_k). The printed iteration does not have the desired root as a fixed point.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.5-55, p. 775: Newton's denominator is 1-c*exp(beta), not 1-c*exp(-beta). Substitution of the desired positive root into the printed update gives a strictly smaller value, so the root is not even a fixed point.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

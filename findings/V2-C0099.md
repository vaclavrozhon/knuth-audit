# V2-C0099 — The right-to-left division update is incorrect

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.3.1 · Exercise 40

The update u'=u-w_0 v appears to need division by b:

The update u'=u-w_0 v appears to need division by b:

u'=(u-w_0 v)/b.

Otherwise u'_0 is always zero, and the next formula gives w_1=0.
For example, take b=2, n=4, u=165=(10100101)_2, and v=15=(1111)_2.
The exact quotient is 11=(1011)_2, so w_0=w_1=1. The printed update
gives u'=150 and w_1=0; the corrected update gives u'=75 and w_1=1.

The example has a 2n-place dividend, an n-place odd divisor, and zero
remainder, as required. The identity follows immediately from
u=v(w_0+b*q_1), where q_1 is the remaining quotient.

The original page and all four available errata layers were checked.
The 2000 change to answer 40 concerns its references. The latest
physical printing and priority of this correction have not been verified.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The explicit update u'=u-w_0*v lacks division by the radix. With radix 2, n=4, u=165 and v=15, it makes the next quotient bit zero although 165/15=11 has that bit one. Using (u-w_0*v)/2 gives the correct next bit. This is distinct from the well-publicized trial-quotient and overflow repairs in Algorithm D.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V4A-F0078 — Deleting a 1 lowers the weight by one

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.3–104, Notes, first sentence, printed 745

Change the final weight t in this sentence to weight t-1.

The Notes call the resulting 1-shadow strings weight t, although one 1 has been deleted.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.3-104, p. 745: deleting a 1 from a binary string of weight t produces weight t-1, not t. The other, zero-deletion shadow retains weight t.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

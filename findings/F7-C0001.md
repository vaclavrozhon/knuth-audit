# F7-C0001 — Covering every girl does not make a matching perfect

**Confirmed · low severity.** Published fascicle · Digital release 1 · 2025.

Location: Fascicle7 / Preface p.xi, paragraph after Algorithm H

Delete “perfect and”, or restrict the perfectness claim to equal part sizes. The algorithm and optimality claim remain correct.

The preface says a matching is perfect when no girl is free, although Algorithm H allows different numbers of girls and boys. In K₁,₂, a maximum matching covers the only girl and leaves one boy unmatched. The matching is optimal but not perfect under TAOCP’s stated definition. Original typesetting and relevant errata checked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

With unequal bipartition sizes, covering all girls need not give a perfect matching: K(1,2) is a direct counterexample. The matching is still maximum. Remove the perfection claim or qualify it by equal side sizes.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V2-C0008 — The added universal-generator exercise misses all-maximal digits

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · 2019 public errata · Added exercise 3.2.2–38

A boundary case invalidates the stated strict bound in the later addition.

The 2019 added exercise permits every periodic digit sequence. For b=2 and all digits 1, its construction gives m=1 and X₀=1, so the error is 1/2 instead of strictly below 1/2. Excluding the all-maximal sequence repairs the construction. The exercise is absent from our original scan.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The universally quantified digit construction fails for an all-maximal stream; the primary patent excludes this boundary.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V2-C0100 — A logarithmic step in the BBP derivation is incorrect

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.3.1 · Exercise 39

The logarithmic expression appears to contain two coefficient errors:

The logarithmic expression appears to contain two coefficient errors:

2l_0 - (2-2i)2l_1 + 2l_4 + (2+2i)l_7

should read

2l_0 - (2-2i)l_1 + 2l_4 - (2+2i)l_7.

The values stated immediately above give
l_0+l_4=-ln(2), l_1=-ln(2)/2-i*pi/4, l_7=-ln(2)/2+i*pi/4.
Thus the printed expression is (pi/2-ln(2))*(1+3i), whereas the corrected
expression is pi. The eighth-root filter also gives the corrected
coefficients directly. The BBP series itself is correct.

I checked the scanned original and the historical and current online
errata, including the September 30, 2024 historical addition. I have not
checked a physical copy of the latest printing or established priority.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Substitution of the logarithms printed immediately above the BBP proof line gives (pi/2-ln(2))*(1+3i), not pi. Removing the extra factor two before l_1 and changing the final plus to minus gives pi. The original image confirms both coefficient errors. The series for pi is correct, and the 2024 historical citation addition does not repair this line.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# F220 — Binary reasons use the outer trigger instead of their source

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / SAT answer267, printed599/file619: Binary reasons use the outer trigger instead of their source

Use -l0 and not l0 or l-prime in the take-account procedure.

The BIMP(l0) loop records reason -l and conflict not l or l-prime without setting l to l0.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

SAT267 records binary reasons using the outer trigger l rather than the current source l0. In the fixed ternary-then-binary chain it records not-s OR c, which is not even implied by the input. Use l0 for both reasons and binary conflict clauses. This is independent of the starting-polarity correction.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

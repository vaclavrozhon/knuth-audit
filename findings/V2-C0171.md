# V2-C0171 — Nonzero-coordinate sampling does not give uniform norms

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.5.4 · printed 671

The exact distribution claim is false; this counterexample alone does not show that the whole method is inefficient.

For n=7,a=3, sampling u,v from1 through6 gives unequal counts4,4,8,4,8,8 for the six nonzero norms. Sampling all residues gives equal fibers.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

For n=7,a=3,b=1, sampling both coordinates from 1 through 6 yields counts 4,4,8,4,8,8 for the six nonzero norms; all gcd prechecks pass. Fact 2 counts all residue pairs, including zero coordinates, and cannot prove the claimed uniformity for this sample space. Sampling all residues with a suitable gcd branch repairs it. This does not by itself disprove efficiency.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

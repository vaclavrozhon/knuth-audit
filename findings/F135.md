# F135 — The cited book title is missing its final s

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–140(c), p. 454, historical note

Surprenant Triangles should be Surprenants triangles. The authors, year and mathematical results are unaffected.

The French national library catalogue identifies Marc Odier and Yves Roussel’s 1976 book as Surprenants triangles. The original Trioker rules also print the plural spelling. Both our Volume 4B PDF and the author’s 2019 PostScript omit the final s in the first word. No matching correction was located in six current and historical errata layers checked against the official files on November 2, 2026. Physical-copy verification and priority remain separate.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The cited title is Surprenants triangles, with a final s in the first word. The archived BnF record cb346060085, field 200$a, and original Trioker rules agree. The current BnF web fetch was unavailable; the previously retrieved primary record remains the evidence.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

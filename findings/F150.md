# F150 — Exercise 248 calls options “items”

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / question 7.2.2.1–248, p. 153

A terminology typo; the algorithm and computed results are unaffected.

The question says “LEN(i) such items” where it should say “LEN(i) such options”. LEN(i) counts the options containing item i. The opening sentence and the answer use the intended quantity correctly. Book page 153 and author draft page 89 share the wording; no matching correction was found in the six relevant errata layers.

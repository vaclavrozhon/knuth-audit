**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / Mathematical Preliminaries Redux, answer124, p.390

Use the triangle inequality and take expectations before bounding the sample error. Both corrected bounds remain valid; this is a local proof error, separate from known E002.

The known erratum changes only the statement. The proof still has a signed equality giving1/2=−1/2, a pointwise sample bound giving8≤1/2, and a squared-error/variance equality giving0 or1/4 instead of1/8. All witnesses satisfy the corrected assumptions.

Answer MPR 124, p. 390: after the known statement correction, the proof still needs the triangle inequality and expectations.

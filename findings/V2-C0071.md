# V2-C0071 — A congruence does not imply ordinary distance

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

The displayed implication |n-n'| >= |h(s)-h(s')| does not follow from
the preceding congruence modulo Q=2^100-1. Take the permitted seeds
s=0 and s'=2^29. The formula in (b) gives

h(s)=2^69, h(s')=2^99+2^69.

For n=2^99-1 and n'=0, the exponents h(s)-n and h(s')-n' differ by
-Q, so the 100-bit blocks agree. Nevertheless

|n-n'|=2^99-1 < 2^99=|h(s)-h(s')|.

The claimed stream separation remains true. Replace the intermediate
bound by the circular distance min(d,Q-d), where d=|h(s)-h(s')|.
The seed exponents are distinct odd multiples of 2^69, between 2^69
and (2^31-1)*2^69. Their interior gaps are at least 2^70 and their
wraparound gap is at least 2^70-1. This proves the bound in the exercise.
For the original restricted seed range, the stronger 2^70 also holds.

The original page was visually checked and the field equality was
verified independently by binary polynomial exponentiation. No repair
was found in the four historical/current errata layers or saved TeX.
Later physical printings and priority are unverified; not sent.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The congruence gives circular separation, not the asserted ordinary index distance. Seeds 0 and 2^29 permit indices 2^99-1 and 0 with the same block, although their distance is less than 2^99. The exponent difference is a multiple of the period 2^100-1. The overall stream separation conclusion survives with circular distance.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

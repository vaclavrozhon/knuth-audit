# C025 — The Acta Mathematica anniversary is ten years out

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / str.87, historická poznámka pod obrázkem71

A historical date is wrong; the word-search construction is unaffected.

The footnote links a 1913 index to the journal’s 21st birthday. The original index explicitly covers 1882–1912, and the first issue appeared in December 1882.

## Publication review, 17 November 2026

Compare the title page of the original Riesz index (PDF page 7) with Domar’s history, p. 8. The twenty-first anniversary was in 1903. A safe correction states that the index followed the first thirty years without guessing the exact name of a celebration.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

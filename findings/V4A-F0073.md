# V4A-F0073 — The octahedron vector has eight edges instead of twelve

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.3–97(a), octahedron size vector, printed 743

Replace (1,6,8,8,0,0,0) by (1,6,12,8,0,0,0).

The octahedron size vector has N_2=8; it must be N_2=12.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.3-97(a), p. 743: the octahedron has twelve edges, not eight. Its eight triangular faces give 8*3/2=12 edge incidences after division by two.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

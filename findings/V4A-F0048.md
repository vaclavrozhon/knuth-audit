# V4A-F0048 — The last two chessboard coverage counts have wrong parity

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer7.1.4-213, printed663: final coverage-coordinate pairs

The numerical pairs need correction. The impossibility argument and the fact that column61 is covered at least twice remain valid.

The two printed pairs (2,1) and (3,2) would make the total coverage odd, although each domino contributes two incidences. In the stated cell order the possible pairs are (2,0) and (3,1).

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 213, p. 663: the last two coverage counts (2,1) or (3,2), added to sixty ones, give an odd total although every domino contributes two. The color balance requires their difference to be two; the possible pairs are (2,0) and (3,1).

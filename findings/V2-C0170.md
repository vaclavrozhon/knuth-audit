# V2-C0170 — The short-vector bound loses a factor of |m|

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.5.4 · printed 670

The intermediate bound is false. Restoring the determinant factor gives the proved reduction bound.

Fact3 omits |m| from the upper bound. For a=2,s=17,z=6, m=2 and the minimum is2, exceeding the printed sqrt(8/3).

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Fact 3's lattice estimate omits a factor abs(m), the lattice determinant's scale. With a=2,s=17,z=6,m=2, the minimum of (6u+2v)^2+2u^2 is 2, exceeding sqrt(8/3). The corrected bound is abs(m)*sqrt(4*abs(a)/3). After division by abs(m), the intended bound on t and the final theorem still follow.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V3-C0014 — The radix-exchange spectral sum includes a pole and mis-scales the oscillations

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Volume 3 / answer 5.2.2-53, p. 637–638

The displayed sum is undefined as written; fixing the excluded root still leaves an incorrect oscillation amplitude.

Exclude z=-1, not z=1: p+q=1 makes -1 a root, and Gamma(-1) is a pole. For p=q=1/2, the other roots have denominator derivative -ln(2); their residue contribution requires delta=sum Gamma(z)n^(-1-z). The extra division by h_p in the printed definition is incorrect because n/h_p is already outside the parentheses. Original typesetting and all relevant archived errata layers checked; no matching correction found.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

On p. 638 the root z=-1 is not excluded by z!=1 and makes Gamma(z) singular. Even after excluding it, the p=q=1/2 case has an extra entropy divisor in the oscillating term. The general residue also depends on the derivative at each root. This does not assert a new general convergence theorem for the residue sum.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V4A-F0109 — The paired-multiset estimate drops dominant terms

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.5–76, last asymptotic equality, printed 779

Retain the full umbral sum, equivalently the Peirce entry w_(2n+1,n+1).

The omitted third term in (B^2+B)^n already exceeds the retained first two Bell terms by an unbounded factor. The claimed relative approximation is false.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.5-76, p. 779: in the positive sum R=sum_j binom(n,j)*B_(2n-j), the term j=2 divided by the printed truncation B_(2n)+n*B_(2n-1) grows like xi/4. Since 2^n*P(0,n) is at least R, the claimed relative o(1) truncation cannot hold. The full sum must be retained.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

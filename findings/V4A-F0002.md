# V4A-F0002 — Cograph diameter claim needs induced subgraphs

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / answer 7–90, p. 526

The parenthetical claim is false for general subgraphs; the exercise classification remains correct.

K4 is a cograph, since it is the complement of four isolated vertices. Its spanning subgraph with edges 12, 23 and 34 is P4, whose diameter is 3. Thus the printed claim that every connected subgraph of a cograph has diameter at most 2 is false. Adding induced repairs the statement. The original wording, the book’s subgraph definition and all five relevant 4A/F0 errata layers were checked; the known nearby corrections do not fix this claim.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7-90, p. 526: the diameter characterization needs connected induced subgraphs. The cograph K4 contains a noninduced four-vertex path of diameter three. The book explicitly distinguishes subgraphs from induced subgraphs.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

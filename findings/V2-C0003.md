# V2-C0003 — The added probability has a positive instead of negative exponent

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · Public errata all2-prepre, p. 30 · Added answer 3.1–19

The added asymptotic probability has the wrong sign in its exponent.

The answer added in the May 8, 2005 public errata gives Pr(λ=1)=Θ(m^(k/2)). For fixed positive k this eventually exceeds 1, so the exponent must be negative. The cited primary preprint supports Θ(m^(-k/2)). This answer is absent from the original 1997 scan: the defect is in the errata, and original page 542 is related evidence only.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The erratum gives an asymptotic probability growing beyond one; the cited paper has the missing minus sign.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V4A-F0079 — The Eulerian-circuit product omits all factors involving vertex zero

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.3–107, determinant formula, printed 745

Start the product at j=0, retaining the determinant on indices 1,...,2m.

The product starts at j=1 but must include 0<=j<k<=2m. The missing factors invalidate the coefficient formula.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.3-107, p. 745: the product must include pairs incident with vertex 0, so its lower index is 0, not 1. Removing the root row and column applies to the matrix minor only. For a triangle the printed rational expression has total degree -2 instead of the required degree 2; restoring all pairs yields 2(z0^2+z1^2+z2^2) and coefficient 2.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

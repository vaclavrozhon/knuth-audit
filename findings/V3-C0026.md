# V3-C0026 — Two combined multiple-list sorting times are overstated

**Confirmed · low severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Volume3 / §5.2.1, p.101, last paragraph: expected times after the supplementary list-concatenation program

The coefficients for Program M followed by list concatenation should be44.62N and41.89N. Sorting correctness, the linear average-time order and the third coefficient52.74N are unaffected.

Page101 gives44.99N and41.95N for M=N and M=N/2 after exercise35 joins the lists. Every instruction of that program gives4N+7M-2E+2, where E is the number of empty lists. Its expectation is M(1-1/M)^N. Combining this with the independently derived Program M mean gives44.624442... and41.885769.... The printed values match subtracting only E instead of2E. Exact rational Taylor bounds verify the rounding; the original typesetting and all five supplied errata layers were checked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The concatenation code on p. 625 costs 4N+7M-2E+2, where E counts empty lists. Its expected value uses E[E]=M(1-1/M)^N. The combined limiting coefficients on p. 101 should round to 44.62 and 41.89, rather than 44.99 and 41.95. The third coefficient 52.74 remains correct.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

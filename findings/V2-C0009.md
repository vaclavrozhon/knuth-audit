# V2-C0009 — The period search and discrepancy claim need period growth

**Confirmed · high severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · Exercises 3.2.2–11(c),26 · pp. 37,39

The search may not terminate, and the discrepancy conclusion can fail. The intended growing-period conjecture is not resolved.

The recurrence Xₙ=Xₙ₋₁−Xₙ₋₂ has period 6 modulo every 2ᵉ for e≥2, so the suggested search never finds a change. It also has a primitive polynomial modulo 2 and at most six zeroes per cycle for every seed, yet the seed (1,1) gives two-dimensional discrepancy at least 1/4. Thus the stated decaying bound needs a period-growth assumption.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The known constant-period recurrence makes the proposed search nonterminating and violates the stated discrepancy consequence.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V4A-F0011 — Masking decoder inputs loses the prefix condition

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / answer7.1.2-33, p.565

The proposed reduction computes incorrect minterms; its cost bound can be repaired.

In answer33, let n=4,N=3,k=2 and P=NOT(x1) AND NOT(x2). ANDing P into x3,x4 maps both0000 and1000 to00, while the required zero minterm differs. The reduced decoder therefore gives a false match. Keep P as an enable condition on minterms to retain the stated asymptotic bound.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.1.2-33, p. 565: masking the remaining inputs by the leading-zero test loses information needed by the minterms. For n=4,N=3, inputs 0000 and 1000 become identical after this operation although their zero-minterm values differ. Masking the computed outputs repairs the argument.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

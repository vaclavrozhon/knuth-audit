# V2-C0105 — The automaton count depends on whether the extra addend is included

**Candidate · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.3.3 · Exercise 11

Candidate: pure multiplication of two2-bit numbers needs one module, while allowing the additional2-bit q can require two.

The text defines the array for UV+q, but exercise11 asks about the product of n-bit numbers. Both interpretations have complete arguments: floor(n/2)+1 modules are necessary and sufficient for UV+q; pure UV has the exception n=2, where one module suffices. For U=V=q=3, two modules are required to return12. Context may include q implicitly, so this remains a clarification candidate.

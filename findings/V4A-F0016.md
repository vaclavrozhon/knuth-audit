# V4A-F0016 — Flipping the two extreme inputs can preserve decomposability

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / answer7.1.2-75, p.576

The blanket assertion of indecomposability is false; the warning about approximate decomposition remains useful.

For f=OR(x1,x2,x3,x4), XOR with S_{0,4} gives NAND(x1,x2,x3,x4). Both functions are decomposable across Y={x1,x2}, Z={x3,x4}. Thus flipping the two extreme inputs does not always make a decomposable function indecomposable.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.1.2-75, p. 576: flipping the all-zero and all-one inputs of every decomposable function need not make it indecomposable. Four-variable OR becomes NAND. Both have a two-block decomposition. The surrounding exercise does not impose an additional restriction that excludes this example.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# F013 — The variance is missing a factor of K

**Confirmed · high severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer MPR 132(b), p. 393

A simple example gives 1/4 instead of the printed 1/8.

The variance is missing a factor of K. For N=4, K=2, n=1 it should be 1/4; the printed formula gives 1/8. Derived from covariances and checked against 3,306 distributions. Confirmed only in the PDF so far. No matching correction was found in the complete 4B and Fascicle 5 errata; the physical copy has not been checked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer MPR 132(b), p. 393: the variance lacks a factor K. For N=4,K=2,n=1 the draw is a fair bit with variance 1/4, not the printed 1/8.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

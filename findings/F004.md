# F004 — Two factorials are missing

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–196(h), missing factorials

The printed formula gives the wrong value.

Both factorials are missing from the formula for Pi_n; the main text on p.103 includes them correctly. For n=4 there are 12 solutions, while the printed expression gives 6. Confirmed in the PDF and original PostScript.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.2.1-196(h), p. 470: both floor expressions need factorial signs. For n=4 with bounds 2444 the product of available choices is 2*3*2*1=12, not 2*3=6. The main-text expression is correct.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

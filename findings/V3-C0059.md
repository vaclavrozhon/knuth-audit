# V3-C0059 — The 2022 AVL height bound is too small

**Confirmed · low severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Errata all3-pre PDF 8, 1 Jan 2022 replacement for Theorem 6.2.3A on printed 460

The replacement additive constant makes the height theorem false; restore N+2 or use a justified nonempty-tree bound.

A two-node AVL tree has height 2, but 1.4405 lg(2+1.063)-0.3277 is approximately 1.99863.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The January 2022 replacement N+1.063 in the AVL height bound on p. 460 fails for the ordinary two-node tree of height 2. Even with the separately corrected coefficient 1.4405, its right side is about 1.998629. The Binet remainder used in the accompanying argument is about .065248, not at most .063. The original N+2 bound avoids this defect.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V2-C0184 — The BBS proof’s error variable omits the sign of chi

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.5.4 · printed 669

The claimed expectation fails even for a perfect oracle. The signed voting formula itself already handles this sign correctly.

For chi(u)=-1, a correct oracle predicts lambda(-u), not lambda(u). The printed error variable therefore marks correct predictions as errors.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Z_j compares the oracle with lambda(u) even when chi(u) is negative, although the canonical root then requires lambda(-u). The signed vote Y_j already handles this correctly. With a perfect oracle, the printed mean on units is nonpositive; for m=209 it is -2/45, while the repaired mean is 37/90. The known published correction about pairwise independence is a different issue.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V1-C0091 — The printed allocation strategy exceeds its claimed bound

**Confirmed · high severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · Exercise 40 · page 616

The stated strategy does not establish the claimed worst-case guarantee.

For maximum block size 32, a general legal family for the printed strategy has asymptotic factor at least 4709347/783360, which is greater than 6. A previously validated trace has 5,554,555 operations, budget 1,179,680 and final extent 7,088,928, exceeding 6 times the budget by 10,848.

Robson’s original algorithm requires a whole free slot rounded up to a power of two and aligned to that power. The book’s different strategy tests only the requested length at the lower-power alignment. The optimal bound N(32)≤6 is not refuted.

The original pages and all four current/historical errata layers were reviewed on November 5, 2026. This does not establish priority or inspection of a later physical printing.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The general legal counterfamily exceeds factor six for the printed rule; Robson uses a different slot condition.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

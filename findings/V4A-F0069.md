# V4A-F0069 — The multiset example does not apply the stated exchanges

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Exercise 7.2.1.3–48, printed 383: example list

Three terms of the example are wrong; the general construction and its proof remain valid.

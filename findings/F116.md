# F116 — A repeated digit invalidates Behrens’s rectangular example

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / p. 78, figure 35(b); row 6, column 7 (1-based)

Figure 35(b) needs 5 instead of 6 in row 6, column 7, counting from 1.

The printed sixth row is 6971426; its last 6 also duplicates a 6 in the last column and its nine-cell box. Replacing that cell with 5 makes every row distinct and every column and box a permutation of 1–9. It is the only single-cell repair among 504 possibilities. The error is visible in both the PDF and the author’s original PostScript; no matching correction was found in current or historical errata. The 1956 source paper and a physical copy have not been checked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Page 78, figure 35(b): the sixth row 6971426 repeats 6, as do the affected column and box. Replacing its last digit by 5 repairs all three. This is a direct contradiction of the stated rules for the rectangular sudoku.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

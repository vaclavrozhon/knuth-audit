# V3-C0054 — The partial fractions invert the pole locations

**Confirmed · low severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Answer5.4.3-8, printed685: partial-fraction denominators use 1-rho*z instead of 1-z/rho

Local partial-fraction typo; formula(12) in the main text already has the correct denominator.

The summation variable rho is a root of q_4(rho)=rho, so the summand must have its pole at z=rho. The printed expression is analytic near an actual pole of A.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The partial fractions on p. 685 need 1-z/rho, not 1-rho z, because rho is a root of q_4(rho)=rho and hence a pole of the generating function. The smallest positive pole is below 1/2, where the printed expansion is analytic. The main-text coefficient formula has the correct pole convention.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

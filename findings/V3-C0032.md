# V3-C0032 — The Quicksort tail bound can put its threshold below the minimum cost

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Exercise5.2.2-42, printed137/file148; answer636 cites an external theorem.

The stated probability theorem is false. The sorting algorithm and Karp’s general theorem are unaffected.

With63 distinct keys, cutoff1 and c=1/20, every input needs at least320 partition comparisons. The claimed threshold is only317.739..., so its exceedance probability is1, not less than exp(-1/20).

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The Quicksort probability bound on p. 137 is false for N=63, cutoff M=1, and c=.05. The least possible comparison count is 320, while the printed threshold (c+1)(N+1)H_N is about 317.739. Thus every input exceeds the threshold, contradicting the claimed probability below exp(-.05). The minimum follows from the split recurrence with cost n+1 and is attained by balanced splits. The 1996 first-edition addition has the same defective coefficient.

# F7-C0020 — Step N5 is not independent of the number of variables

**Confirmed · low severity.** Published fascicle · Digital release 1 · 2025.

Location: Fascicle 7, printed 103/file 118: Algorithm N complexity paragraph

Replace the independence-from-n claim by a polynomial bound accounting for seeding, full witnesses and contribution checks. The polynomial-time conclusion remains valid.

With Boolean majority, N1 retains at least one tuple per coordinate pair. N5 explicitly scans their projections to seed its dictionary, requiring at least binomial(n,2) work even with a four-key projected dictionary.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

N5 seeds its dictionary from every source tuple, so its cost is not independent of n. For Boolean ternary majority, N1 contributes at least binomial(n,2) distinct tuples, and the first constrained call scans them even though the projected dictionary has only four keys. The overall fixed-arity polynomial-time conclusion survives.

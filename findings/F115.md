# F115 — The Sudoku isomorphism claim also needs symbol relabeling

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–57, p. 427; isomorphism of the Sudoku solutions

Rows and columns alone leave six classes; allowing digit permutations joins them.

The 864 solutions under the weaker normalization form six row/column classes of 144; the literal normalization leaves six classes of 72. An explicit counterexample has row transition (1 2 3)(4 6 5)(7 8 9), absent from all 72 ordered row pairs of the printed grid. Column permutations preserve these transitions, while row permutations only reorder them. A second certificate also excludes transposition. All 864 positive transformations with digit relabeling are directly checked. This is independent of the factor-of-two count issue. PDF and author PS agree; no matching erratum found. Physical copy not checked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

DLX 57: row and column permutations alone leave six classes of the listed sudoku solutions. The stored witness has a row-pair digit permutation absent from every row pair of the displayed grid, ruling out even unrestricted row/column permutations. Allowing digit renaming gives the intended single class.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

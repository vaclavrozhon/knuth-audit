# V2-C0094 — The logarithmic squeeze bound needs a minus sign

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.2.4 · Exercise 18

In the final displayed-in-text upper bound in answer 18, the first
plus sign in

floor(log_10(b^n)) + floor(log_10(a^n)) + 1

should be a minus sign. The preceding line gives the upper bound
p(10^m,10^(m'+1)) = m'-m+1, where m=floor(log_10(a^n)) and
m'=floor(log_10(b^n)). The corrected bound gives the intended squeeze
for n p(a,b) and the limit log_10(b/a).

The printed weaker inequality is itself true, but it does not give
that squeeze. For example, a=10 and b=100 give the intended upper
bound n+1, whereas the printed expression gives 3n+1.

Checked against the September 1997 first printing and the four public
errata layers available in this audit. The March 22, 2002 correction
on this page concerns a different parenthesis. I have not checked the
latest physical printing or whether this has already been reported.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The preceding bound is m'-m+1; substitution must retain its minus sign. The printed plus gives 3n+1 rather than n+1 when a=10,b=100 and fails to establish the required squeeze. Retain as a proof typo; the weaker printed inequality and final theorem remain true.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

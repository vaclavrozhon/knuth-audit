# V3-C0038 — The final Riemann sum is missing its bin-count factor

**Confirmed · low severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Answer5.2.5-18, printed652: the final Riemann sum omits a factor c

A local normalization error; the asymptotic linear-time conclusion survives the correction.

For cN bins of width1/(cN), the sum of the density suprema is asymptotic to cN. The answer states N.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

On p. 652, the sum of the binwise density suprema is cN+o(N), not N, when there are cN bins. Constant density 1 and c=1/8 already contradict the printed statement. The eventual linear expected-time conclusion still follows.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

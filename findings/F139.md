# F139 — The printed Endless Chain inventory is impossible

**Confirmed · high severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / exercise 7.2.2.1–150, p. 143; answers pp. 458–459, Endless Chain

The exercise has no solution as drawn. All four answer diagrams use a different inventory of small pieces.

Only strips 3, 14 and 17 have an odd number of horizontal path ends. Rotation preserves this parity on every strip, so the total remains odd; a closed chain must pair all such ends. Replace one of the two S-dominoes by a second L-domino. This is the inventory used by all four printed answers. One bounded census of the corrected set produced 43 valid solutions, eight paths within the frame, and the printed multiplicities 1, 15, 9, 3. The faulty inventory is present in our PDF and the author draft; no matching correction was found in six public errata layers.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Exercise 150: the pictured Endless Chain inventory has odd endpoint parity, so it cannot form a closed chain. It has one L piece and two S pieces where the answer uses two L and one S. The parity obstruction is independent of enumeration and agrees with the original piece crop.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V2-C0075 — The expected count omits the probability of output

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.6 · Exercise 14

The displayed expected number of ones omits the probability that
an output is produced. With p_k defined immediately above, put

B=(26p_2+11p_3+26p_4+11p_6+11p_9+4p_12+4p_20
       +3p_28+p_47+p_74+p_99+1/2)/100.

Exact evaluation gives B=0.49369628238894736..., so the printed
expression 10^6 B equals493696.282388947..., not approximately14043.
The intended factor is

q=2^-100 sum_{i=0}^{40} binom(100,i)
  =0.0284439668204903958....

Then 10^6 q B=14042.6806756707..., consistent with14043 and with the
following approximations28444 total outputs and14401 zeros.

This repairs the displayed arithmetic in its approximation model.
It does not by itself prove an exact expectation for the sequential
adaptive experiment: the100 previous bits are linearly dependent at
some phases (rank99 with even parity at phase37 for blocks of200),
and adaptive advancement can condition subsequent state distributions.
The audit distinguishes these qualifications from the missing factor.

The original page was visually checked and the combinatorial sums
were evaluated exactly. No correction was found in all2-prepre,
all2-pre, all2, err2, or saved current TeX. Later physical printings
and priority are unverified; not sent.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The displayed weighted sum is 0.49369628238894736. Its quoted count 14043 needs the additional factor 2^-100 times the sum of binomial(100,i), i=0,...,40, which is 0.0284439668204904. Their product times one million is 14042.6806757. This identifies a missing factor in the stated approximation, not an exact expectation under every adaptive sampling phase.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

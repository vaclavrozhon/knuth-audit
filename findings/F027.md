# F027 — The expected-value expression diverges incorrectly

**Confirmed · high severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer SAT 307(a), p. 609

The sum is missing the previous value of α; the example’s true mean is 2.

The calculation of E X is missing the old value of α at E_Nj. For p1=p∞=1/2 and Nj=1, the printed sum diverges, while the correct expectation is 2. Confirmed in the PDF and the author’s PostScript; no matching correction was found in the errata.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 307(a), p. 609: multiply E_(N_j) by the probability of reaching run j, namely the old alpha. For p_1=p_infinity=1/2 and all cutoffs one, the expectation is two; the printed partial sums 1,7/4,19/8,47/16 diverge instead.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

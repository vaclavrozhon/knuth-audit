# F001 — The upper bound uses the wrong index

**Confirmed · high severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / 7.2.2.1–197(a,b)

The construction permits permutations that violate the specified bounds.

Replace a_n with a_j. A counterexample exists for n=2; the correction was checked for 625 bound vectors with n≤7. Confirmed in the PDF and the author’s PostScript. The physical copy has not been checked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Exercise 7.2.2.1-197(a,b), p. 149: use the individual bound a_j instead of a_n. At n=2 with bounds (1,2), the printed conditions permit t_1=t_2=2 and produce the forbidden permutation 21.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

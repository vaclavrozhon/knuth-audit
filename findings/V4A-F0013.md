# V4A-F0013 — Sorted threshold outputs are listed in reverse order

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / answer7.1.2-38, p.567

The output functions are assigned to the wrong wires; reversing their indices preserves the cost and depth bounds.

Ascending sorting gives f_k=S_{>=n+1-k}, not S_{>=k}, from top to bottom. For input(1,0), the sorted result is(0,1), while the listed threshold sequence is(1,0). Costs and depths are unchanged.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.1.2-38(a), p. 567: the listed thresholds put the sorted outputs in reverse order. The exercise explicitly specifies ascending order. For input (1,0), the printed top-to-bottom order gives (1,0), whereas ascending order is (0,1). The kth output needs threshold n+1-k.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# F148 — Population summaries disagree with the source data

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / main text 7.2.2.1, p. 116, population summaries

Three rounded population figures on page 116 are inaccurate; the NY obstruction remains valid.

New England totals 14,445,438 and NY plus New England totals 33,823,525. These round to 14.4 and 33.8 million, not 14.3 and 33.7. The smallest non-NY group in the left figure 116 has 37,177,055 people, about 37.2 million rather than the stated lower endpoint 37.3. All relevant inputs agree between Knuth’s SGB file and the official 2015 census baseline. Both book and author draft have the same text; no matching erratum was found.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Page 116: the stated historical population data give New England 14,445,438 (14.4 million), with New York 33,823,525 (33.8), and the smallest displayed non-New-York cluster 37,177,055 (37.2). The printed 14.3, 33.7 and 37.3 are inconsistent with these inputs; no new optimization is required.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

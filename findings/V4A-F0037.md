# V4A-F0037 — The covering-function asymptotic loses a leading term

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Exercise 7.1.4-131(a), printed page 267: fixed-q asymptotic

Correct the asymptotic in the question; the exact answer remains valid.

The exact answer has leading term p*((q+2)*2^(q-1)-1), not p*q*2^(q-1) for fixed q. With q=2 the exact size is 7*p-2, so its ratio to the claimed 4*p tends to 7/4.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Exercise 131(a), p. 267: with fixed q=2, the exact expression in answer 131, p. 642, is 7p-2 rather than asymptotic to 4p. Their ratio tends to 7/4. The leading coefficient for fixed q is (q+2)*2^(q-1)-1.

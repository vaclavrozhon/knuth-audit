# F064 — The sudoku-pattern condition needs two boxes

**Confirmed · high severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / 7.2.2.2–515(vi), p. 367 and answer p. 651–652

The model does not enforce the more general restriction as printed.

A model with exactly 58,212 clauses, 351,432 literals, and 2,471 variables satisfies (i)–(v), yet has the forbidden four-box rectangle 4,7/7,4. Condition (vi) needs to specify two boxes. Visually confirmed in the PDF and PostScript; no matching erratum was found.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

SAT 515(vi): the statement must restrict the alternating 2 by 2 rectangle to two boxes. The printed clauses impose precisely that restriction, while a valid encoded puzzle can have a blank alternating rectangle occupying four boxes. The stored full grid and clause certificate establish this mismatch; no uniqueness assumption is added to the stated problem.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V4A-F0050 — Postorder changes the forest ZDD

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer7.1.4-224, printed667: postorder preservation claim

Remove postorder from the stated order-preserving examples. The correspondence remains valid for suitable topological orders.

Postorder does not preserve the stated child/sibling ZDD of a forest. The path family{{a,b},{a,c}} has a tree of nonsink nodes in preorder a,b,c but a shared nonsink in postorder b,c,a.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 224, p. 667: the path family {{a,b},{a,c}} has a tree-shaped ZDD in preorder a,b,c, but in postorder b,c,a the nonsink a is shared. The assertion that postorder preserves the diagram is false; preorder and level order retain the intended correspondence.

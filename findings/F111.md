# F111 — The greedy-queens matrix indices are reversed

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–37(a), p. 423

The identity should read a[q_i,i]=1; the permutation result remains valid.

The question gives q_2=3, but the answer recurrence gives a[2,3]=5 and a[3,2]=1. Thus the printed a[i,q_i]=1 is false already at i=2. Two independent constructions agree on all 12,720 checked matrix cells, and the corrected orientation follows from the greedy definition. The PDF and original author PS contain the same transposition; current and historical errata do not correct it. Physical copy not yet checked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

DLX 37(a): the displayed recurrence gives a[2,3]=5 and a[3,2]=1, while q_2=3. Thus the stated unit entry is a[q_i,i], with the indices interchanged.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

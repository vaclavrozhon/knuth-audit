# V4A-F0123 — Three intermediate binomial-moment formulas have incorrect factors

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.6–57, first paragraph, printed 796

Correct these three intermediate identities; the final even and odd worm-depth formulas remain correct.

The weighted tail sum omits 2(a+b) in the denominator, the recurrence omits 2b(2b-1), and the third-moment denominator should be (a+b)(a+b-1).

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.6-57, p. 796: at a=b=1 the truncated first-moment sum is one, not four; its expression needs division by 2(a+b). The moment recurrence needs multiplier 2b(2b-1). The third-moment denominator is (a+b)(a+b-1), not (a+b)^2. Each follows termwise or by telescoping; the final worm-depth formula survives.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

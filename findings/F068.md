# F068 — The supposedly optimal rectangle can be smaller

**Confirmed · high severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–158, p. 461

A verified 28 × 16 placement has area 448 rather than 450.

For n=6, the printed rectangle claimed to be optimal is 30×15, with area 450. An explicit validated witness fits 28×16, area 448, using 21 pieces and seven empty cells. Confirmed on PDF p.461 and PostScript p.165, with no matching erratum. The finding does not depend on negative optimality computations.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

DLX 158: an explicit 28 by 16 packing of one unit square, two side-two squares, through six side-six squares has area 448, below the claimed optimal 30 by 15 area 450. Nonoverlap and multiplicities are certified directly. Establishing the global optimum is unnecessary for this refutation.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

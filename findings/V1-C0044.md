# V1-C0044 — Incorrect constant in the running time of COPY

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · §2.3.2 · exercise 14 · printed 573

The running-time formula overstates the constant by 5u.

The ten printed instruction groups sum to 36n+14 for lines 064–103. Entry STJ and the final JMP add 3u, so complete COPY takes (36n+17)u. A single leaf takes 53u rather than 58u. The caller jump and the special COPYP1/COPYP2 entrances do not explain the printed constant 22. The copied tree and the register contract are correct. The original listing, general sum and accepted literal executions agree; all four refreshed errata layers contain no repair. Physical printing and priority remain unverified.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The displayed instruction groups sum to 36n+17 including entry and return, not 36n+22.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

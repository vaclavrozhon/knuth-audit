# V2-C0173 — The factor-base logarithm formula uses the wrong coefficients

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.5.4 · printed 671

The formula can return an incorrect discrete logarithm. The corrected general algorithm has a recorded proof.

With p=7,a=3 and factor base{2}, the printed sum returns logarithm1 although3^2=2. The sum needs the original power exponents, with one common combination of relation rows.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The discrete-log reconstruction multiplies relation coefficients by e_jk instead of the original exponents n_i. The single relation 3 squared equals 2 modulo 7 gives the printed logarithm one instead of two. The coefficients must also be shared across coordinates: sum_i c_ik*e_ij+(p-1)t_jk=[j=k], followed by N_k=sum_i c_ik*n_i modulo p-1. The 1998 correction to the final modulus does not fix either issue.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

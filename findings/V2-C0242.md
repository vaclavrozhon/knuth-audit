# V2-C0242 — Two bounds omit the unit-modulus exception

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.3.3 · Printed 80, 82 and 87

Low-impact boundary omissions. The main formulas and ordinary nontrivial generators remain valid.

The definitions allow m=1,a=c=X0=0, a full-period sequence of length 1. Theorem P then gives |r|=d/(2m)=1/2, contradicting its strict bound; equation (11) also drops a nonzero delta term. Separately, the explicitly allowed h=k=1 tableau takes one step, whereas the prose on printed 87 bounds it by log_phi(1)=0. Add m>1 to the first bound/proof and handle k=1 separately in the step estimate. The Dedekind formula itself returns 0 correctly. Original pages and all errata layers rechecked on 6 November 2026; latest physical printing and priority remain unverified.

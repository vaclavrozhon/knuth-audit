# F066 — The hitori construction exceeds its bound

**Confirmed · high severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / addendum to answer 7.2.2.1–443, p. 547

On a 7 × 7 grid it needs 13 black cells.

The modulo-5 construction, when only adding black cells, exceeds mn/5+2. On a 7×7 grid, every shift has minimum 13; two forced additions to the base pattern already give 12>11.8. Checked by a direct proof and two enumerators, and confirmed on PDF p.547 and PostScript p.251. A different construction with 11 black cells exists; this finding does not disprove the general bound.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

DLX 443: the printed mod-five construction plus added black cells does not prove the mn/5+2 bound. On a 7 by 7 board its ten initial black cells require at least two additional cells, exceeding 11.8; the completed finite check actually needs thirteen black cells. This refutes the proposed construction, not the general existence bound: a different eleven-black cover exists.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

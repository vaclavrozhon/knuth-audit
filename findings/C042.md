# C042 — The signature-length condition may need a primary-item exception

**Candidate · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–254(b), p. 480

Applying LEN!=0 to primary items could turn a dead end into success. The main definition and native DLX6 are correct.

Answer 254(b) says each item contributes when LEN is nonzero. That filter is appropriate for secondary items. An uncovered primary item must retain its bit even if its option list is empty. The surrounding discussion may implicitly restrict the condition to secondary items, so this remains a wording candidate.

This earlier candidate or dismissed entry was outside the 806 confirmed-card publication review.

# F260 — Newton's inequalities were proved before 1865

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / Mathematical Preliminaries Redux, answer26, p.374: history of Newton's inequalities

The historical attribution confuses coefficient inequalities with a stronger rule for counting nonreal roots. The inequality and printed mathematical proof remain valid.

The answer credits the eventual proof to Sylvester in 1865, although Maclaurin's 1729 paper already proves the coefficient inequality used here.

## Publication review, 17 November 2026

Compare Maclaurin1729, Proposition IX and its corollary on pp.69–70, with the same-sign hypothesis on p.68. Normalize the coefficients by binomial coefficients. Sylvester's reprint pp.498–499 and 508 distinguish the root-counting rule from the earlier coefficient criterion.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

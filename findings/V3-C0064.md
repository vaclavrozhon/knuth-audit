# V3-C0064 — The variance paragraph names the wrong quantity

**Confirmed · low severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Answer 6.2.4-8, printed 721: variance of storage utilization versus number of pages

Rename the quantity as the number of pages (space occupied); the utilization fraction has bounded variance.

The utilization fraction lies in [0,1], so its variance cannot grow as Θ(N); the cited phase transition concerns the number of pages.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

On p. 721, the utilization fraction U=N/(M S), where S counts pages, lies in [0,1] and has variance at most 1/4. It cannot have the stated linear or superlinear variance. Mahmoud and Pittel's cited phase transition concerns occupied space, not that fraction. This is a mismatch of the named random variable, not a new variance theorem.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

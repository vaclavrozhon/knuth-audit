# V1-C0050 — The published height asymptotic replacement is wrong

**Confirmed · high severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · §2.3.1 · Erratum dated 13 November 1999 · answer 11, printed 565

Applying this erratum would change a correct original formula into an incorrect one.

The first-printing term +11/24·sqrt(π/n) is correct. The 1999 erratum replaces it by −13/24·sqrt(π/n)+1/(2n). The exact Lambert-series formula, singularity analysis and Catalan normalization retain +11/24 and cancel the 1/n term. Accepted exact coefficients support this proof. This finding concerns the erratum; printed 565 is the correct comparison page.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The exact generating function and coefficient transfer retain the original 11/24 coefficient and cancel the added 1/n term.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

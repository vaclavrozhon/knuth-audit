# F007 — The mixture weights are reversed

**Confirmed · high severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / MPR 123, p. 25, mixture weights

The formula assigns the opposite probability to each component.

For Z=A?Y:X and Pr(A)=p_k, the mixture density has its weights reversed. It should be p_k y(t)+(1−p_k)x(t). An exact example uses positive probabilities. Confirmed in the PDF and the author’s PostScript.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

MPR 123, p. 25: if Z=A?Y:X and Pr(A)=p_k, its mass function is p_k*y(t)+(1-p_k)*x(t). The displayed mixture interchanges these weights. The later Bayesian update uses the intended weights.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V2-C0035 — Continuous run formulas require a multiplier greater than one

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.3.3 · Exercises 22–24 · Printed 92, 575 and 576

A missing hypothesis affects the unit multiplier in exercises 22–24; the preceding correlation formula remains valid.

For a=1 and uniform x in [0,1), the map s(x)={x+theta} satisfies Pr(s(x)<x)=theta, not 1/2. At theta=0 the map is the identity, so any strict descent has probability 0; exercise 24 nevertheless gives 1/2 for length 2. Require a>1 or give separate answers for a=1. The 2005 clarification that x is uniform does not address this case. Rechecked on 6 November 2026; latest physical printing and priority remain unverified.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The stated multiplier domain includes one, where the decreasing-step probability is theta, not one half; retain as a minor missing hypothesis.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

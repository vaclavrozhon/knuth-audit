# V2-C0125 — The claimed exact residue sum omits an exponential remainder

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.5.2 · Exercise 28

The stated asymptotic order and periodic leading term remain correct.

The exact function tau obeys4tau(2n)−tau(n)=1/(exp(n)−1)>0. A pure n^−2 periodic term would make that difference zero. The missing term is minus the positive sum of4^r/(exp(2^r n)−1), r≥0. Its exponential decay preserves the requested asymptotic result.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The definition gives 4*tau(2n)-tau(n)=1/(exp(n)-1)>0. An exact expression n^-2 times a function periodic in log2(n), as asserted in the next paragraph, would give zero. The missing remainder is -sum over r>=0 of 4^r/(exp(2^r*n)-1), which is O(exp(-n)). Retain as an exact-versus-asymptotic error, distinct from Brent's already public missing-periodic-term correction.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

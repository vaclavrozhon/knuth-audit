# V3-C0053 — The reverse-generation argument omits simultaneous empty tapes

**Confirmed · low severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Answer5.4.2-28, printed684: full-addition rule does not generate every sortable distribution

The phase bound survives; the proof needs partial additions or componentwise domination, not exact generation.

The positive distribution(2,2,1,1,1) sorts in two phases, but its total7 cannot be generated from total1 by operations increasing the total by4x.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The exact reverse-generation claim on p. 684 excludes simultaneous tape exhaustion. Initial counts (2,2,1,1,1) sort in two phases, but every distribution generated from (1,0,0,0,0) by adding one entry to all four others has total congruent to 1 modulo 4. Seven runs are impossible under that rule. Componentwise domination repairs the upper-bound argument; the optimum bound itself is not refuted.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

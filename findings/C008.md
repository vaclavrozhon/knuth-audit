# C008 — Stable assignments are incorrectly called covering

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / SAT361, p.619: stable assignments are incorrectly called covering

This misstates the relationship between binary fixed points and covering assignments. The survey equations themselves are unaffected.

Answer361 identifies two classes that exercise364 explicitly distinguishes. A stable assignment need not have each assigned variable constrained.

## Publication review, 17 November 2026

Take the single clause x OR y and assign x=y=1. Both variables are unconstrained, so the assignment is not covering. Reinforcement messages1 on x,y and0 on their negations, with both clause messages0, form a fully defined binary fixed point. Replace covering by stable and refer to exercise363.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

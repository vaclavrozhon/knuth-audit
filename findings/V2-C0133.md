# V2-C0133 — The replacement proof of Lemma M uses incorrect limits

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · Section 4.5.3 · Printed page 367

The 2011 replacement needs the remainder K_N, not the original null set K.

The 2011 replacement needs the remainder K_N, not the original null set K. With N fixed, the two auxiliary limits need not equal the measure of I; explicit geometric intervals give different limits. The lemma is true and an epsilon argument repairs the proof. This is a defect in replacement text, not a previously corrected finding.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The 2011 replacement visibly omits the subscript in I_N union K_N. Its final limits also fix epsilon and N, so they equal mu(I_N) and 1-mu(J_N), not generally mu(I). Alternating dyadic intervals give the explicit distinct limits 2/3-(2/3)*4^-N and 2/3+(1/3)*4^-N. The intended squeeze proves Lemma M after letting epsilon go to zero; the lemma is correct.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

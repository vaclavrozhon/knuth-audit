# F119 — Bitland has 1,647 six-B placements, not 1,467

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / p. 431, answer 7.2.2.1–69; N6 placement count

Two digits are transposed in the N6 option count. The final 60 districtings remain correct.

All three original Bitland grids agree. Polyomino growth, the complete Algorithm R archive, and a separate examination of all 11,440 nine-cell subsets of a 4×4 square yield the same 12,097 legal placements. Exactly 1,647 contain six B cells; every witness is saved. The other counts and both final solution sets are correct. Our PDF and the author’s older PostScript both print 1,467. No matching correction was found in the checked current or historical errata; a physical copy remains unchecked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

DLX 69: the number of admissible convex nonomino placements containing six B cells is 1647, not 1467. The stored 1647 distinct witnesses, full 12097-placement histogram and independent subset construction agree. This count concerns individual placements; the final counts of complete districtings are unaffected.

# F021 — The constant and sign are incorrect

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / p. 469, answer 189(c)

Two algebraic errors occur in the paragraph; the absolute-value estimates remain valid.

The paragraph contains two errors: g/3 should be g/e, and the maximized modulus exp(exp(uz)) is missing a minus sign inside the outer exponential. Substitution and z=0 provide direct proofs. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript. No matching correction was found in the complete 4B and Fascicle 5 errata; the physical copy has not been checked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 189(c), p. 469: substitution x=exp(t)-1 gives the leading term g/e, not g/3. The following maximum-modulus integrand also needs exp(-exp(uz)); its printed positive exponent already disagrees at z=0. These are errors in one displayed analytic decomposition.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V4A-F0040 — The profile inequalities are not sufficient

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Exercise7.1.4-146(b), printed page268, and answer647

The converse needs additional reachability conditions; the proposed construction can leave level-2 nodes unused.

The positive tuple (b0,b1,q0,q2)=(1,2,1,5) satisfies all printed inequalities but is impossible: a single QDD root reaches at most four nodes after two decisions.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Exercise 146(b) and answer, pp. 268 and 647: b0=1,b1=2,q0=1,q2=5 meets the displayed inequalities, but one root after two binary tests has at most four distinct nodes. The converse needs an additional reachability bound, such as q2<=q0+b0+b1; this necessary condition is not asserted to be sufficient.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V2-C0249 — The harmonic remainder loses an unbounded factor

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · Section 3.5 · Answer 20(b) · Printed page 594

A local asymptotic equality is false for admissible sequences; the optimal lower bound remains valid.

Section 3.5 answer 20(b) multiplies a harmonic-sum expansion by p_n but retains an O(1/n) remainder. For the distinct points U_j=1−2^(−j), p_n=(2n−1)/2 and the displayed error is at least (2n−1)/(8n), so it does not tend to zero. Use O(p_n/n), or derive the final bound directly from p_n≥1/S_n.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The harmonic remainder on p. 594 is O(p_n/n), not generally O(1/n). The permitted distinct points U_j=1−2^(−j) give p_n=(2n−1)/2 and an error at least (2n−1)/(8n). The main lower bound follows directly by dividing by the harmonic sum; alternatively separate infinite limsup first.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

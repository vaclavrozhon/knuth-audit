# F070 — The binomial coefficient needs upper index t

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–177(b), p. 467

The recurrence requires binom(t,k) rather than binom(n,k).

The upper index should be t rather than n. A general proof and 72 coefficient checks agree with the book’s numerical data. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript; no matching correction was found.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

DLX 177(b): the recurrence needs binomial(t,k), not binomial(n,k); n is not its parameter. The reported Q(5,5) remains correct.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

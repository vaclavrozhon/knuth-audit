# V4A-F0100 — The Bell expansion remainder needs logarithmic factors

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Exercise 7.2.1.5–45, displayed remainder, printed 437; answer 52, final note, printed 774

Use the same logarithmic remainder scale as in equation (26).

The Bell expansion coefficients grow with xi. The exercise-45 remainder needs logarithmic factors; the Gaussian probability statement in answer 52 likewise has relative error of order (log n)/n, not O(1/n).

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Exercise 7.2.1.5-45, p. 437, and answer 52, p. 774: b2prime=xi^2/288+O(xi), so after the first correction the remainder is not O(n^-2). The scale is O((log(n)/n)^(m+1)). In answer 52, b1prime asymptotic to -xi/12 likewise makes the leading Gaussian relative error order log(n)/n, not O(1/n).

# V1-C0033 — Potential CONTROL indicator issue depends on the calling convention

**Candidate · high severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · §1.4.4 · Answer 12 · printed page 533

The behavior difference is established; its classification as a book error depends on an unproven preservation requirement.

The added CMPA changes the comparison indicator, while the original CONTROL preserves it. The recorded two-card trace correctly demonstrates that difference.

The November 12 publication review found a missing contractual step in the error claim. Page 188 allows subroutines to change the comparison indicator without listing it in their exit conditions, and pages 197–198 describe coroutines as mutually callable routines whose conditions must fit. Pages 222–223 do not explicitly promise indicator preservation across this amendment.

Therefore inserting JRED CONTROL between a comparison and its conditional branch is not clearly a permitted use under the general convention. A difference from the old implementation alone does not prove a book error. The case is now a candidate for clarification, pending an explicit preservation requirement or a failing caller that respects the calling convention. The saved trace and suggested preserving variant remain useful, but the finding is excluded from confirmed errors.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The trace changes CI, but the book permits unmentioned CI changes and does not clearly promise preservation across CONTROL; the alleged caller may violate the calling convention.

Decision: `candidate`. This supersedes the earlier confirmed assessment.

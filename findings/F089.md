# F089 — The conditioning prefix is too long

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2–54, p. 407

The final term should end before the current variable.

The final conditioning prefix in Π should end at x_(l−1), not x_l. The reweighting itself is correct and has been verified generally. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 54: the last factor in the path probability must condition on the parent prefix ending at x_(ell−1), not the resulting prefix ending at x_ell. The printed conditional distribution is undefined at a leaf; the weighting rule itself is correct.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V2-C0060 — The upper-half frequency at even positions is reversed

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.5 · Exercise 28

The proposed sequence fails the frequency required by the exercise. Complementing the binary period fixes the answer.

The printed answer gives an upper-half frequency of 1/4 at even positions, but the exercise requires 3/4. The period 0001000111011101 has only two ones in its eight even-indexed bits. Since (X_n+U_n)/2 lies in the upper half exactly when X_n=1, this is a direct contradiction. Replacing X_n by 1-X_n repairs the frequency and preserves 3-distribution. Original pages 155, 182 and 596 and all relevant errata layers were checked. The newest physical printing and priority have not been verified.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The explicit period on p. 155 has upper-half bit frequency 1/4 at even positions. The answer's `(X_n+U_n)/2` therefore gives 1/4, whereas the exercise asks for 3/4. Complementing `X` repairs the direction and preserves three-distribution. The 2024 correction changes different pair indices.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

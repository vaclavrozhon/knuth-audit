# C002 — The asymptotic estimate may assume even n

**Candidate · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–199, asymptotics

The stated order differs along the odd subsequence.

For n=2q+1 and m=q+1, N~2(n−1)!, whereas the stated Θ((n−2)!) holds for even n=2q and m=q. The even subsequence may be implicit, so this is not classified as an unambiguous correction.

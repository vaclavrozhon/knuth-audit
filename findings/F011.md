# F011 — The integral is missing a factor of π

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer MPR 130(e), p. 393

Direct integration gives 2π; the printed result gives 2.

The integral’s result is missing π. At p=q=1/2 and z=0, the integral is 2π, while the printed result is 2. Checked independently by residues and direct integration, and confirmed in the PDF and PostScript. No matching correction was found in the complete 4B and Fascicle 5 errata; the physical copy has not been checked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer MPR 130(e), p. 393: the integral's value needs a factor pi. At p=q=1/2,z=0 it is four times the integral of (1+y^2)^(-2), hence 2*pi rather than two. The final Cauchy law is correct.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

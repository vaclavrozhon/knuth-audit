# V3-C0075 — The replacement carry formula is shifted

**Confirmed · low severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Errata all3-prepre PDF 38: replacement for answer 6.4-32

End the prefix sum at j-1, or change c_j to c_(j+1).

With M=3 and b=(2,0,0), the replacement produces (0,0,1) instead of the required (1,0,0).

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The January 2002 replacement for answer 6.4-32 is shifted by one index. For M=3 and b=(2,0,0), the carry recurrence gives c=(1,0,0), while the replacement gives (0,0,1). Sum up to j−1 in the prefix sum, or relabel its stated carry as c_(j+1). This finding concerns the replacement, not the superseded proof.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

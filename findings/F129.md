# F129 — Three forced edges use undefined zero-based indices

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–109, p. 442

The example names edges outside the declared coordinate range.

Answer 109 defines coordinates starting at1, but its connected-search example names H00, H20, V30. With ZERO in cells 11, 21, 31, 41 and cell 51 empty, the forced edges are H11, H31, V41. The same mismatch occurs in the author’s PostScript; no matching correction was found in the checked errata.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 109: under the stated one-based grid convention the forced items are H11, H31, V41; the printed H00, H20, V30 are undefined or displaced.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

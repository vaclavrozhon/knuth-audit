# V2-C0070 — The research-status note on R6 counterexamples is outdated

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.5 · Exercise 31

The statement that no R6 counterexample is known needs an updated reference. This is not a new solution of the stronger betting-based Kolmogorov–Loveland randomness problem.

Research-status update based on prior published work. Shen, Uspensky and Vereshchagin (2017), Theorem 203(b), explicitly give the binary every-prefix property. The archived independent-coordinate proof verifies it for the exact real R6 definition and all bases. No explicit prior report correcting this TAOCP sentence was found; the theorem and its general method are not claimed as new.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Retain explicitly as a research-status update, not a new theorem. Shen, Uspensky and Vereshchagin's 2017 Theorem 203(b) states the binary every-prefix property. The audit's independent-coordinate construction and bounded martingale-difference estimate verify it directly for all bases in the book's real R6 definition. The probability of all positive prefix excesses is positive after 4096 initial lower-half variables. This concerns frequency selection, not the stronger modern betting notion of Kolmogorov–Loveland randomness.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

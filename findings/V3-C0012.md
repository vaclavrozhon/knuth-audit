# V3-C0012 — The partitioning formula takes the reciprocal twice

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Volume 3 / answer 5.2.2-29, p. 633

For ordinary random-pivot quicksort, the stated leading term is too small by a factor of four.

For f(x)=1 the displayed integral is -1/2 and the defined alpha is 2. The claimed alpha^-1 n ln n is therefore (1/2)n ln n, whereas the quicksort recurrence gives 2n ln n+O(n). With the printed definition of alpha, the result must be alpha n ln n. Original typesetting and all relevant archived errata layers checked; no matching correction found.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

For uniform pivot density f=1, the integral on p. 633 gives alpha=2; the printed alpha^(-1) n ln n then gives half n ln n, whereas the ordinary quicksort recurrence gives 2 n ln n. One of the two reciprocal conventions must be changed.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

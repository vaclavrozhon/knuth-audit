# F014 — The probability exceeds the claimed bound

**Confirmed · critical severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / MPR 134(b), p. 27 and answer p. 394

Two fair bits disprove the stated two-sample inequality.

The two-sample bound is false: two fair bits with m=1 and ε=9/10 give probability 1/2 against the claimed bound of about 0.3958. A factor of 1/2 is missing from the hypergeometric tail threshold. An exact proof and the PDF wording were checked; part (c) works after the correction. No matching correction was found in the complete 4B and Fascicle 5 errata; the physical copy has not been checked. On November 1, 2026, the same incorrect wording was also visually confirmed on p.27 of the April 2026 fourth digital version preview.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

MPR 134(b) and answer, pp. 27,394: two independent fair bits with m=1 and epsilon=9/10 differ with probability 1/2, exceeding 2*exp(-81/50). The pooled-sample threshold is epsilon*m/2, not epsilon*m; the repaired exponent -epsilon^2*m/2 suffices for part (c). The previously inspected April 2026 publisher preview still has the defective statement.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

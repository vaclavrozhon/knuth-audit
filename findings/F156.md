# F156 — Four entries do not match the illustrated plane partition

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–262(b), p.483

The printed array represents a different tiling from the middle illustration it refers to.

In the first column, rows 4–7 should read 3,5,5,6 instead of 2,4,4,5. The other 60 entries agree. Both arrays are valid reverse plane partitions; only the claimed correspondence is wrong. All 256 lozenges of the middle picture and its 64 vertical lozenges were decoded in both source versions.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 262(b): the first-column entries in rows 4–7 are one too small for the displayed middle tiling. The projection from the rhombus coordinates gives 3,5,5,6, not 2,4,4,5. Both arrays separately satisfy the plane-partition inequalities; only their claimed correspondence is wrong.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

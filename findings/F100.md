# F100 — There are eight nearly full organ sounds

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / 7.2.2–79(g,h), p. 415

Q₈₁₁ = 8, not 12; the range of excluded pedals is too wide.

Q811 should be 8 rather than 12, and the range for the switched-off pedal should be 12≤j<20 rather than 12≤j<24. Pedal p20 switches off three pipes. A general proof, all 812 target sets, and independent complete polynomials agree. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript; no matching erratum was found.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 79(g,h): exactly eight sounds omit one of the 812 pipes. Missing pedal indices range from 12 through 19, rather than through 23. At index 20 the printed construction silences three pipes; the same problem occurs at 21,22,23. The pedal polynomial's own coefficient eight confirms the correction.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

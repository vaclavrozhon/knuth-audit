# F039 — The final inequality points the wrong way

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / SAT 355, p. 618

A single-edge example disproves the printed > sign.

The final E_j>1/δ has the wrong direction: a single edge with p_i=1/4 gives E=1/2 and δ=1. Non-strict inequalities are needed for zero coordinates. A complete corrected proof is available. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 355: the concluding inequality for E_j is reversed. A single edge at p1=p2=1/4 has slack delta=1 and E_j=1/2, contradicting E_j>1/delta. A proof covering zero coordinates uses non-strict inequalities and gives E_j<=1/delta.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

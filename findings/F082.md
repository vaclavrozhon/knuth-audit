# F082 — The Langford modification can loop forever

**Confirmed · critical severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2–22, p. 400

For n = 10 it does not terminate; for n = 6 it also duplicates results.

The prescribed modification for loose Langford pairings is incomplete: n=6 outputs 13 rather than 10 representatives, and n=10 enters an exactly documented cycle. Two returns are missing during forced choices. The correction and complete models were verified. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript; no matching erratum was found.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 22: the two forced choices can disagree at n=6; the literal modification then outputs thirteen representatives instead of ten. At n=10 the reachable prefix (1,−1,9) leads to a repeated scan between labels 8 and 10 when the forced second slot is occupied. The recorded state cycle proves nontermination. Backtracking on conflicting requirements and on failed forced placement repairs these cases; the count table remains correct.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

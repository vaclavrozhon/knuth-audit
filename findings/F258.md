# F258 — Two sign claims in the overlap-ratio proof are incorrect

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / 7.2.2.2–203(e), p.587: signs in the overlap-ratio proof

Correct the intermediate signs and the associated root-location discussion. This finding does not contradict the final satisfiability-threshold conclusion.

Answer 203(e) asserts f(1/2)<0, but the allowed choice p=1/2, r=7/5 gives f(1/2)=3/25>0. The discrepancy persists for arbitrarily small positive epsilon. The following approximation near 1/2 also uses the opposite paired sign: g(1/2 +/- 1/N) has leading term +/- f(1/2)N/4. Both defects occur in one proof paragraph.

## Publication review, 17 November 2026

Substitute p=1/2 and r=7/5 into f(sigma)=2pr sigma/(1-p+p sigma^2)-1; then expand ln((1/2+h)/(1/2-h))=4h+O(h^3).

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

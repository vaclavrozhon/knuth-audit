# V4A-F0108 — The block-count variance error is not uniform in block size

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.5–65, final asymptotic estimate, printed 778

Restrict the rate to fixed k, or retain the covariance correction when k grows.

The claimed relative error O(n^(4*epsilon-1)) fails for k near xi, even though k<=n^epsilon. The exact variance formula is correct.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.5-65, p. 778: let X_k count blocks of size k and lambda=xi^k/k!, where xi*exp(xi)=n. At k=floor(xi), the exact factorial moments imply Var(X_k)/lambda=1-1/sqrt(2*pi*xi)+o(xi^-1/2). Taking epsilon=1/8 contradicts the printed uniform relative O(n^-1/2) error. The exact moment formula and leading equivalence remain valid.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

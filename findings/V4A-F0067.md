# V4A-F0067 — Wrong sign in the falling-factorial root expansion

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.3–17, printed 727: forward falling-factorial root expansion

The displayed intermediate expansion is wrong; the exact unranking algorithm and final inverse approximation remain valid.

The coefficient of 1/x in (x falling t)^(1/t) is -(t^2-1)/24, not +(t^2-1)/24. The t=2 square root gives an immediate counterexample; the subsequent inverse expansion is correct.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.3-17, p. 727: sqrt(x(x-1))=x-1/2-1/(8x)+O(x^-2) already disproves the printed positive 1/x term. The general coefficient is -(t^2-1)/24. The following inverse expansion is correct.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

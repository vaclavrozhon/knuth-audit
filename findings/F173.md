# F173 — Sketch values belong to the permuted union

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / Mathematical Preliminaries Redux, answer111(b), p.387

Use the image of the union under the permutation, or the inverse images of the sketch values. The collision probabilities and the main results remain valid.

Answer111(b) places the sketch values in the original union. For A=B={1} and the permutation21, both sketches equal2, outside that union. The two-permutation family is exactly minwise independent.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

MPR111(b): sketches are permutation images, which need not belong to A union B. With A=B={1} and permutation 21, both sketches are 2. Use the image of the union, or pull back the sketches. The Jaccard collision probability is unchanged.

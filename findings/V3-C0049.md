# V3-C0049 — The 2005 Dedekind-number erratum has a false leading term

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Errata all3-prepre PDF34, 1 Nov2005 replacement for answer5.3.4-31 on printed669

The replacement asymptotic is false at leading order. The original 1998 answer is correct at that order.

The replacement predicts ln(delta_(2m))=(1/2)binom(2m,m)ln2+o(binom(2m,m)), but independently choosing middle-layer sets gives delta_(2m)>=2^binom(2m,m)-1.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The 2005 erratum replacing the Dedekind-number answer on p. 669 has half the necessary leading binomial coefficient in its exponential. Arbitrary subsets of the middle layer already give at least 2^binom(2m,m)-1 relevant monotone functions; the replacement's leading logarithm is binom(2m-1,m)ln 2, exactly half as large. The smaller displayed terms cannot repair this. The original 1998 limiting statement is sound. This card concerns the replacement, and does not endorse all its other terms.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

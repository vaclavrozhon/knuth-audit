# F072 — The level profile is off by one

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–215(d), p. 473

The correct term is 2(q−l+1).

The level profile needs 2(q−l+1), not 2(q−l). Checked by a direct counterexample at q=1 and a general proof; DLX2 confirms 40 profiles. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

DLX 215(d): level ell has 2(q−ell+1) nodes, not 2(q−ell). The root's two children at q=1 already expose the missing one.

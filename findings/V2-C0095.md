# V2-C0095 — MIX running-time constants need correction

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.3.1 · Exercise 3/8

Two constant terms in the MIX running times appear to be one cycle low.

Two constant terms in the MIX running times appear to be one cycle low.

Answer 3: The exact time is 5MN + 7N + K + 5. The initial ENN1, JOV,
ENTX cost three cycles, and the final STX costs two. Thus, under the
stated assumption K = MN/2, the time is 5.5MN + 7N + 5, rather than +4.
For M=2, N=1, and input words b-1 and 1, K=1=MN/2 and the program
uses 23 cycles, whereas the printed formula gives 22.

Answer 8: The initial ENT1, JOV, STZ cost four cycles. Hence the exact
time is 9N + L + 7K + 4, and the displayed approximation becomes 13N+4.
For N=1 and input words b-1 and 1, L=K=1 and the time is 21 cycles,
rather than the printed 20.

Here b denotes the size of a MIX word, and overflow is initially clear.
The programs themselves produce the correct sums.

I checked the original pages and all four available errata layers
(all2-prepre, all2-pre, all2, err2). I have not verified the latest
physical printing or whether these corrections have already been reported.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The three initial instructions and final store in answer 3 cost five units, not four. Answer 8's initial ENT1,JOV,STZ cost four, not three. Their displayed frequency columns give exact times 5MN+7N+K+5 and 9N+L+7K+4. The concrete cases in the reports meet the assumptions on the carry counts. Both programs compute correct results.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

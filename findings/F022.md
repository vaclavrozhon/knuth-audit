# F022 — The denominator should be (k−1)!

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / p. 469, after answer 189(d)

Integration by parts reveals a missing factor of k.

The first integral for g_k needs (k−1)! instead of k!. Checked by integration by parts, 12 numerical pairs of integrals, and the original paper, PDF, and PostScript. No matching correction was found in the complete 4B and Fascicle 5 errata; the physical copy has not been checked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 189, p. 469: the first integral for g_k requires denominator (k-1)!, not k!. Integration by parts supplies the factor k needed to match the second integral. The cited Asakly et al. paper, Corollary 4, has the correct factor.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

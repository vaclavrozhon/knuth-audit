# F268 — Clause-signature argument requires one extra changed level

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / main text p.257: changed-level bound in the clause-signature argument

A local error in the rationale for selecting learned clauses. The corrected bound still supports the qualitative heuristic; the algorithm and empirical table are not refuted.

The p+1 bound on p.257 is too large: a clause with signature (2,0) can force a literal after just one old level changes sign.

## Publication review, 17 November 2026

Use C=x OR NOT y and C'=y OR z. On x=true@1,y=true@2,z=true@3 their signatures are (1,1) and (2,0). Keep x, backtrack and set y=false: C' forces z=true. Only old level2 changed sign, although p+1=2. The general necessary bound is p'-1, hence at least p.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

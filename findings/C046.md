# C046 — The one-coordinate boundary may need an explicit scope qualifier

**Candidate · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / Mathematical Preliminaries Redux, answer8, p.372

If n≥2 is implicit, dismiss this as a context note. Otherwise add that restriction or the n=1 exception. No confirmed erratum is asserted.

Candidate only. For n=1 the stated parity-biased law is P(0)=3/4 and P(1)=1/4, so the single variable is independent and the final claim of non-n-wise independence does not apply literally. The complete theorem is correct for n≥2. It remains open whether the earlier n≥2 restriction is intended to carry into this exercise.

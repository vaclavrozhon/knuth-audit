An arbitrary nonzero coefficient need not give sum d_j<=d: f=x+y with coefficient x⁰=y gives d_1=d_2=1 but d=1. Choosing the leading coefficient at each step proves the correct conclusion. An existential reading is valid; the Schwartz–Zippel bound is not refuted.

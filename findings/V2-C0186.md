# V2-C0186 — Mersenne’s time estimate means a whole century

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume2 · printed 407

A historical quotation overstates the original claim; the mathematical tests are unaffected.

The original1644 phrase saeculum integrum means a whole century. The book translates it as all time.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The original 1644 Latin visibly says saeculum integrum, an entire century or age, rather than all time. Replacing the book's translation by an entire century follows the original and the earlier Kravitz translation. This is a translation correction, not a new primality result or a claim to have reconstructed every historical detail in the paragraph.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

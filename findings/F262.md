# F262 — The minimality proof deletes the wrong clause

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / SAT answer 206, p.588: wrong clause deletion in the minimality proof

The displayed deletion may remove nothing, so this last proof step does not contradict minimality. The theorem is correct and the repair is local.

Answer 206 conditions C on the fresh variable instead of removing the clause that C became in the joined formula.

## Publication review, 17 November 2026

Use F={a, NOT a}, F'={b, NOT b}, and join them by adding x to the first two clauses and NOT x to the other two. For C=a, conditioning on NOT x leaves a, which is absent from the join. Deleting x OR a instead gives the satisfying assignment x=0, a=0.

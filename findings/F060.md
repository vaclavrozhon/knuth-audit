# F060 — The hitori upper bound is false

**Confirmed · high severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / 7.2.2.1–443, p. 183 and 547

The four black corners of a 3 × 3 grid give a counterexample.

The bound (mn+2)/3 is false: counterexamples include four black corners of a 3×3 grid and an infinite family of 3×odd-n grids. The proof’s −[mn odd] term is wrong; the correction is 3s≤mn+3−[m even]−[n even]. Checked using the uniquely solvable puzzle AAA/BCD/EEE, a general proof, and 4,950 boundary checks. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

DLX 443: the 3 by 3 cover with four black corners and a connected white cross contradicts the claimed (mn+2)/3 bound. It can be the unique solution of the puzzle AAA/BCD/EEE. The corrected boundary argument gives 3s≤mn+3−[m even]−[n even], where s is the number of black cells. The failure extends to an infinite family of odd-width, three-row covers.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

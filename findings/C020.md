# C020 — The frontier bound needs a specified measurement point

**Candidate · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–40, p. 424; database-size bound

The database may temporarily exceed 2^ν while new entries are inserted.

One primary item with one option has frontier size ν=0. The database grows from {(0,1)} to {(0,1),(1,1)} in N3, so its size is 2 rather than at most 1; N4 compresses it back to one entry. The bound 2^ν holds before insertion and after compression. A bound of 2^(ν+1) covers the whole iteration. The counting algorithm and its asymptotic rate remain valid. The author may intend only completed iterations, so the wording is tracked as a low-priority candidate rather than a confirmed error. General proofs, complete small-instance checks, PDF, and author PS support the distinction; checked errata contain no clarification.

# V2-C0030 — The forbidden-pair generating function has an extra numerator

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.3.2 · Printed 571

One table entry counts too many words; its asymptotic columns and numerical calibration are correct.

For words excluding ab and bc, the generating function is 1/(1−mz+2z²−z³). The printed numerator 1+z gives m+1 permitted words of length 1, although only m exist. The exact moment formulas and all six reported experiments remain valid. Rechecked on 6 November 2026; latest physical printing and priority remain unverified.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The printed numerator counts m+1 one-letter words over an m-letter alphabet.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

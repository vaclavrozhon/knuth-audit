# V4A-F0122 — The final ballot-area formula omits the diagonal case

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.6–55, final displayed equality, printed 795

State r>=1 for the last expression and use answer 54 on the diagonal, or add half the central binomial coefficient when r=0.

The final expression for C prime at p,p+r is valid for r>=1 but fails at r=0, which is included in the exercise. The preceding generating-function expressions remain valid there.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.6-55, p. 795: the last simplification is invalid on the principal diagonal r=0. At p=2 it gives -2, while the area polynomial 1+x has derivative one. Add [r=0]*binom(2p,p)/2 or restrict this last equality to r>=1. The preceding coefficient expressions remain valid.

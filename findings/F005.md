# F005 — The initial coefficient should be 2

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–196(h), series constant

The first term of the numerical formula is incorrect.

The series 6+4/1!+5/2!+… sums to 4e+3, not 4e−1. Replace 6 with 2, or omit 4/1!. This also follows from the exact formula in 196(e). Confirmed in the PDF and original PostScript.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.2.1-196(h), p. 470: the printed series sums to 4e+3. To obtain the stated 4e-1, replace the initial six by two, or omit the term 4/1!. The exact preceding update formula supports this correction.

# V2-C0053 — The logistic-map density has the wrong normalization

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.4.1 · Exercise 24 · page 140

The printed expression cannot be a probability density. A local normalization correction fixes it without changing the recurrence.

The printed density 1/sqrt(2*pi*x*(1-x)) integrates to sqrt(pi/2), which exceeds 1. The correct density is 1/(pi*sqrt(x*(1-x))), with CDF (2/pi)*arcsin(sqrt(x)). The substitution x=sin^2(theta) proves the normalization exactly. The logistic recurrence and its angle-doubling identity are unchanged.

# F098 — The algorithm outputs an underweight singleton

**Confirmed · high severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / 7.2.2–78, answer p. 414

The initial visit does not check the lower weight bound L.

The initial singleton requires WT(v)≥L. Otherwise, weights 1,1 with L=2, U=3 produce an invalid singleton. A literal transcription and 112,974 weighted cases confirm the correction. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript, separately from the known R6 and R2prime corrections.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 78: the initial singleton visit omits the lower weight bound. An edge with both weights one and bounds L=2,U=3 makes it output invalid singletons. Search should continue below L but the singleton should be visited only when its weight is at least L.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

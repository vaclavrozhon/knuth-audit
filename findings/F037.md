# F037 — The proof reverses the direction of change

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / SAT 346(a), p. 616

The word should be “increase” rather than “decrease.”

The proof should say increase rather than decrease: reducing a probability does not increase the trace weight. A single edge gives K=2→4/3 when p_b=1/2→1/4. The exercise and algorithm have the correct direction. Confirmed in the PDF and the author’s PostScript; no matching erratum was found.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 346(a): decreasing a nonnegative probability cannot increase a trace's weight. The final word in the sentence must be increase. For a single edge, K_a=1/(1-p_b) falls from two to 4/3 when p_b falls from 1/2 to 1/4, with p_a=1/8 keeping both points in the region.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

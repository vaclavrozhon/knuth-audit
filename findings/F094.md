# F094 — The rejected intermediate sum is possible

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

9 + 7 + 4 + 4 = 24; the puzzle’s final solution remains correct.

9+7+4+4=24 refutes the claim “never 24.” Two explicit rotations satisfy N+S=24; they are excluded only by NE+SW=19 or 20. The book’s final solution is unique and correct. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript; the known erratum only corrects the bibliography.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 66: 9+7+4+4=24 contradicts the stated exclusion. The two compatible rotations must instead be rejected using their northeast–southwest sums, 19 and 20. The final puzzle solution remains correct.

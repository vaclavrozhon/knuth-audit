# V2-C0026 — The 2004 erratum makes the coefficient estimate false

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.3.2 · Erratum dated 7 January 2004, referring to printed 569

This defect is in the published correction; the original threshold 8 is consistent with the estimate.

The 2004 change replaces j≥8 by j≥3 in c_j=O(n^−3). But c_3=1/(3sqrt(n))+O(n^−9/2). For fixed j, the leading size 1/[j n^(j/2−1)] supports the original threshold 8. The main partition asymptotics remain correct. Rechecked against the first printing and all four errata layers on 6 November 2026. A recent physical printing and priority remain unverified.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The 2004 erratum incorrectly includes the cubic coefficient in an O(n^-3) bound; that coefficient has order n^-1/2.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

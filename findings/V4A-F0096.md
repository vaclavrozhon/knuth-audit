# V4A-F0096 — The simplified Bell estimates omit terms larger than their remainders

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Equations 7.2.1.5–(29) and (30), printed 423

Retain the exact rational first corrections, or weaken both displayed remainders to O(1/n).

Replacing the exact first correction by -xi/(12n) loses nonzero terms of order 1/n, exceeding the printed O((log n/n)^2) error.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Equations (29)–(30), p. 423: replacing the exact first corrections by -xi/(12n) discards terms asymptotic to 11/(24n) and -1/(24n). Neither fits the printed O((log(n)/n)^2) remainder. Retain the exact coefficients or weaken the remainder to O(1/n).

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

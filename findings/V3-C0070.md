# V3-C0070 — A trie quantity is misidentified after the product expansion

**Confirmed · low severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: 6.3 printed 505, paragraph after (14): n=0 term misidentified as trie inspection total

Refer to U_N, as the subsequent algebra already does.

For N=2 the term is 2, while N-1 plus the trie inspection total is 5; the term is actually N-1+U_N.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The n=0 term of (14) on p. 505 is N−1+U_N, not N−1 plus the trie bit-inspection total. At N=2 the term sums to 2, whereas the claimed trie expression is 5.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# F056 — The reduction produces an unsatisfiable output

**Confirmed · critical severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / SAT 212(d), answer p. 589

A solvable one-element input has no solution after the printed transformation.

The construction needs k=K′ instead of k=(I,J,K′). For N=1 and L11={1}, the printed r=c=I2 and off-diagonal p have no solution even though the input does. The example satisfies the equalities in (b), independently of F055. The correction preserves the number of solutions in general; 614 inputs and 659 solutions were checked. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript, separately from the known 2017 change.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 212(d): the condition must be k=K′, not k=(I,J,K′). With N=1 and L11={1}, the printed construction gives incompatible row, column, and symbol margins. The corrected row and column matrices are both [[1,0],[1,0]].

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# F7-C0003 — Answer20 calls the slot/type items “options”

**Confirmed · low severity.** Published fascicle · Digital release 1 · 2025.

Location: Fascicle7 / 7.2.2.3, answer20, p161 first sentence

Replace “option” with “item” in that sentence. The intended MCC encoding is unaffected.

The first sentence on p161 says there is one option for each slot and model type. The surrounding construction defines primary items for those entities; its options are slot-model pairs. Two directly verified valid sequences need different options sharing slot2. Original typesetting and all relevant public errata layers were checked; the coordinator independently confirmed this local terminology error.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The first sentence of answer 20 calls the slot/type items options. The construction has one item per slot and per model type, and options for allowed slot/model pairs. The September and October 2026 amendments affect different sentences.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

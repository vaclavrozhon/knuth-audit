# V2-C0108 — The necessary bound on the conversion constant is too strong

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.4 · Exercise 5

The printed bound is sufficient but excludes constants that work. The original conversion program remains correct.

For D=10^n and a nonnegative MIX word constant c, the exact condition is D−gcd(w,D) ≤ c < w. Writing wu=Da+r shows why the smallest positive remainder, gcd(w,D), determines the bound. For w=64^5 and n=1, c=8 works for every input0–9, while the answer incorrectly requires c≥9. The general necessity and sufficiency proof is recorded in the evidence.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

For word capacity w and decimal divisor D=10^n, the exact sufficient and necessary word-constant condition is D-gcd(w,D)<=c<w. This follows by considering the smallest positive remainder of wu modulo D. Binary MIX with w=64^5,n=1,c=8 works for all ten inputs although the printed lower bound is nine. The printed condition is sufficient, but not necessary.

# V4A-F0019 — The byte-flag characterization fails for an arbitrary constant

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / answer 7.1.3-94, p. 598

The claimed extension of the zero-byte test gives incorrect flag predictions. The original all-ones constant is unaffected.

For x=0 and l=#ff, the printed conditions predict a nonzero least significant flag byte, but (x-l) mod 256=1 and the flag is zero. The characterization omits an upper bound on the size of the byte borrow.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 94, p. 598: x=0 and constant l=255 meet the two asserted low-byte conditions, but subtraction gives byte 1 and therefore no high-bit flag. The arbitrary-constant characterization lacks the upper bound 128 on the borrow magnitude. The ordinary zero-byte test remains correct.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# C044 — Are incoming arcs at the stated sources implicitly excluded?

**Candidate · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–261(c), p.482

Literal boundary arcs change the path family and its counts.

The arc description in 261(c) permits edges into vertices in S, such as (0,2)→(0,1). Treating S as genuine sources excludes those arcs. The literal interpretation has 10 and 64 families at n=3 and n=4, versus the intended 5 and 16. Because “sources” may imply the exclusion, this remains a candidate. F154 is independent of this ambiguity.

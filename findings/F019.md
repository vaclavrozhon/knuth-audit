# F019 — The differential equation is missing a factor

**Confirmed · high severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / p. 468, answer 187(b)

General indices require exp(sz).

The general differential equation for T_(r,s) is missing exp(sz); alternatively, the indices must be restricted to s=0. Verified by direct differentiation and in the PDF and PostScript. No matching correction was found in the complete 4B and Fascicle 5 errata; the physical copy has not been checked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 187(b), p. 468: differentiating the defined integral gives T_(r,s)'=exp(z)T_(r,s)+z^r exp(sz). The displayed general identity lacks exp(sz); specializing explicitly to s=0 also repairs the line. The requested special case is unaffected.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

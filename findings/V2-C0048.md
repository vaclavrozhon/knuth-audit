# V2-C0048 — The stride-four basis needs a small-modulus condition

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.3.4 · Answer 21 · p. 581

The stated basis fails for modulus 2; its intended use for modulus at least 4 is correct.

A full-period generator sampled every four steps has first lattice vector gcd(4,m)*(1,a²,a³)/m. The printed factor 4 is valid for m=2^e with e≥2. For m=2,a=c=1, the sampled triple is the single point (0,0,1/2) modulo Z³. The printed basis has determinant 2, whereas the correct periodic lattice has determinant 1. Original pages and all errata layers rechecked on 6 November 2026. Later physical printing and priority remain unverified.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The allowed case `m=2,a=c=1` yields the constant sampled triple `(0,0,1/2)`. Its periodic difference lattice is `Z^3`, whereas the displayed basis has determinant 2. Replace 4 by `gcd(4,m)` or require `m>=4`. Retain as a small-modulus exception.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

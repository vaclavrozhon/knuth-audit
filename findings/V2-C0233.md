# V2-C0233 — Saidan's translation appeared in 1978, not 1975

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · See attached source pages

Two references give 1975; the publisher front matter identifies the original edition as 1978.

Both references to A. S. Saidan, The Arithmetic of al-Uqlidisi
(Dordrecht: D. Reidel), give the year 1975. It should be 1978.

The publisher's front matter explicitly identifies the original
hardcover first edition as 1978. The publisher's bibliographic
record agrees, giving 31 March 1978 for that edition.

https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-94-009-9772-1
https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/bfm:978-94-009-9772-1/1
The copyright page is PDF page 3; it was visually checked.

This correction concerns the original edition, not the dates of
the later softcover or electronic reprints. Both occurrences in
TAOCP were checked in page images. No correction was found in the
six archived errata layers. No later physical printing or priority
of this observation is claimed.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The Said(an) translation cited on pp. 198 and 461 was published in 1978, not 1975. The title/copyright pages and the publisher's book record agree.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The 2005 addition visibly assigns a logarithmic variance to tau_n, but equation (48) defines tau_n as a deterministic average for fixed n. Morris's precise probability model and moment formulation confirm the intended random division count over pairs with both entries bounded. Retain the notation/model correction; Hensley's full original article remains unavailable.

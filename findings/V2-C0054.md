# V2-C0054 — The outer-area formula omits a second region

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.4.1 · Answer 21 · p. 588

The general area formula fails for some parameters; the implemented generator and its tabulated practical case are unaffected.

Even after the published 2005 factor-of-two correction, the general exterior-area formula can omit a second horizontal region. At parameter x=1 the boundary is v²=4u(1−u), symmetric under u→1−u; the formula gives about 0.12184789 while the actual area is twice that. The practical parameter x=−0.35 has only the first region, so Algorithm R and its printed operation-count table remain valid. Rechecked November 6, 2026; no matching further erratum found. Novelty and the latest physical printing remain unverified.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Even after the 2005 factor-of-two correction, the ratio-of-uniforms exterior-area formula omits a second horizontal interval for positive `x`. At `x=1`, the intersections occur at `r` and `1-r`, where `r=(1-sqrt(1-2/e))/2`; the true area is twice the printed result. An integral of the positive part over the whole unit interval repairs it, or the domain can be restricted. The actual negative-parameter timing example is unaffected; no new global optimizer is asserted.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

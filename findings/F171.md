# F171 — The coupling proof removes arcs with the wrong target vertex

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / Mathematical Preliminaries Redux, answer109(c), p.386

Replace the target xj by yj. This repairs the intended restriction of the network; the main coupling theorem remains valid.

Answer109(c) removes xi→xj, although the middle arcs of the stated network are xi→yj. The unchanged network can carry a full flow that violates the required order.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer MPR109(c) deletes x_i to x_j arcs from a network whose middle arcs are x_i to y_j. The target must be y_j; otherwise a full crossed flow on a two-element chain need not respect the required order.

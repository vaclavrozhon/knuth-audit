# V3-C0044 — Two clause-output indices omit a factor n

**Confirmed · low severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Answer 5.3.4-52, printed672: concluding clause-wire indices omit n

Wrong explanatory wire references; the comparator construction itself already has the correct offset.

The two concluding clause-output indices use 2m+2n instead of the construction's 2mn+2n.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

On p. 672 the output-wire indices in the explanatory clause omit a factor n: they need 2mn+2n+j and 2mn+2n+k. For m=3, n=5 the affected output wires are 41,42,43, not 17,18,19. The displayed comparator construction already uses the correct indices.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

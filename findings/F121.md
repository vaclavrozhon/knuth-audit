# F121 — The semisymmetry condition needs XOR, not addition

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / p. 432, answer 7.2.2.1–75(b)

A wrong operator appears in the justification of two valid multiplication tables.

Answer 75(b) prints f(t+f(t))=t. The construction in the question uses XOR, and direct substitution requires f(t XOR f(t))=t. For the fourth table f=(0,2,3,1): ordinary addition at t=2 asks for undefined f(5). Even addition modulo 4 fails at t=3. Both tables, all 18 tables of order 4, and their class sizes 4, 12, 2 remain correct. The PDF and author PS agree; no matching correction was found in the checked errata. A physical copy remains unchecked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

DLX 75(b): the required identity is f(t XOR f(t))=t. With f=(0,2,3,1), ordinary addition asks for undefined f(5) at t=2; addition modulo four also fails at t=3. The five multiplication tables themselves remain correct.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

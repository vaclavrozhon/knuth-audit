# V4A-F0114 — The subtree removal vectors have the wrong sign

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.6–37, definition of f_j on 791 and C(n+e_j;j) identities on 792

Use f_j=e_j+(j-1)e_0; in the C(n;j) identities use C(n+f_j;j).

Removing a degree-j corolla removes j-1 leaves as well as one internal node. The printed minus sign in f_j, and the later use of e_j in its place, contradict the counts.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.6-37, pp. 791–792: removing a degree-j node changes the composition by f_j=e_j+(j-1)e_0, not by subtracting the leaf contribution. At n_0=3,n_2=2, C-E is one rather than the printed three. The later C(n+e_j;j) also requires n+f_j; the same composition gives C+D=5 instead of zero.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V2-C0254 — Motzkin’s coefficient formula omits a constant term

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · Section 4.6.4 · Answer 39 · Printed page 705

Set b=beta1+alpha_m. This repairs the local coefficient calculation and leaves the representation theorem intact.

The quadratic factor contains beta1+alpha_m, but the answer sets its constant b to alpha_m. For w1=x²+1 and w2=(x²+1)², the resulting formula gives the constant coefficient of w1 as 2 instead of 1.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 39 on p. 705 omits beta1 from the quadratic constant: b=beta1+alpha_m. For w1=x²+1 and w2=(x²+1)², the printed formula produces constant coefficient 2 instead of 1. The representation theorem is unaffected.

# F117 — An extra S is painted over the correct G in our PDF

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / p. 429, answer 7.2.2.1–65; SOLVING, row 7, column 7

The lower-right SOLVING cell is visually corrupted in the local PDF; the author’s PostScript is correct.

The PDF first paints G and then S at almost identical coordinates in row 7, column 7. The puzzle explicitly gives G there, and two independent solvers agree on the unique solution ending OLNISVG. The older author PostScript contains a clean G. This finding establishes a local file defect only; it does not establish an error in the printed book or an error made by Knuth. No matching public erratum was found.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 65: retain only as a local PDF defect. The lower-right SOLVING cell paints S over the given and correct G. The author PostScript has a clean G. This does not establish an error in the printed book or attribute the corruption to the author.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

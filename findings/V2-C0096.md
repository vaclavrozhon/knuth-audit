# V2-C0096 — A small digit count is incorrect

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.3.1 · Exercise 15

The strict error bound (n-2)b^(-n-1) needs the condition n>=3. For n=1 or n=2, no product u_i v_j has i+j>n+2, so the omission error is zero. The printed bound is negative for n=1 and zero for n=2.

The strict error bound (n-2)b^(-n-1) needs the condition n>=3.
For n=1 or n=2, no product u_i v_j has i+j>n+2, so the omission error
is zero. The printed bound is negative for n=1 and zero for n=2.

The exact maximum omission error, attained with all digits equal to b-1,
is
  (n-2)b^(-n-1) - (n-1)b^(-n-2) + b^(-2n).
It is strictly below the printed bound for n>=3 and equals zero for
n=1,2. The parenthetical bound for omitting i+j>n+3 has the analogous
zero-error cases n<=3 (and a negative printed bound for n<3).

This concerns omitted products before final rounding; the asymptotic
point of the exercise is unaffected. A short restriction or explicit
zero-error cases would resolve the issue.

I checked the original pages and all four available errata layers
(all2-prepre, all2-pre, all2, err2). I have not verified the latest
physical printing or priority of this observation.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

For n=1 or 2 no digit products satisfy i+j>n+2, so omission error is zero. The claimed strict bound is negative or zero. Restrict that bound to n>=3, and similarly separate the zero-error cases in the parenthetical next bound. Retain as a minor missing small-length qualification.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

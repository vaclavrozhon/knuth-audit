# V3-C0055 — The radix-tree optimum argument shifts every level by one

**Confirmed · low severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Answer 5.4.7-4, printed 694: tree levels 2, 3, 4 should be 1, 2, 3

Local level-label error in the optimum proof sketch; the 2.8-pass schedule and optimum are not refuted.

The root has level zero. The Ashenhurst tree on p.344 already has three level-2 leaves and attains 2.8 passes, contradicting the answer's opening exclusion of a level-2 leaf. All three level labels are one too high.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The three level labels in answer 4 on p. 694 should be 1,2,3, not 2,3,4. The root has level zero, and the displayed Ashenhurst tree already has three level-2 leaves while achieving the stated 2.8 passes. This corrects the proof sketch, not the optimum.

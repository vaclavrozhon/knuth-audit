# V4A-F0077 — Two shadow-minimization tasks omit the comparison shadow

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Exercises 7.2.1.3–103 and 104, concluding inequalities, printed 388

Insert the shadow operator before P in both concluding inequalities.

Both exercises compare |partial A| with |P|, although their solutions choose P to contain N objects. They require |partial P|.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Exercises 7.2.1.3-103–104, p. 388: the extremal comparison needs the size of the shadow of P, not the size of P itself. All twelve edges of a three-dimensional cube have only eight shadow vertices; all four two-bit strings have only two one-bit shadows. The proposed extremal families in the answers have the intended original size N.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# F105 — The expanded ménage identity uses the wrong subscripts

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / section 7.2.2.1, answer 14, p. 418, finite ménage identity

The finite expansion can produce a negative value for a positive seating count.

On page 418, replace T_(n−1), T_(n−2), T_(n−3) in the explicit expansion by T_(n−2), T_(n−4), T_(n−6). The compact sum immediately to the left already has the correct n−2j subscript. For n=3, the printed expansion is −5/6, while the correct value u_3/3! is 1/6. Independent seating counts and exact rational arithmetic confirm the correction. Both the PDF and original PostScript contain the error; no matching erratum was found. The physical copy has not been checked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

DLX 14: expanding the preceding correct sum requires numerator indices n−2, n−4, n−6. The printed n−1,n−2,n−3 gives −5/6 at n=3, whereas the sole permissible seating gives 1/6. The subsequent asymptotic claim is outside this local correction.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

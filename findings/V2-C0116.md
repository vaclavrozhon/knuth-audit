# V2-C0116 — The coprime-pair sum needs positive-coordinate bounds

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.5.1 · Exercise 14

The count may be wrong if an interval contains zero. Its use for positive numerators in the preceding exercise remains valid.

For M1=−1,M2=1,N1=0,N2=2 the coprime pairs are(0,1),(1,1),(1,2): three pairs. The printed sum stops at min(M2,N2)=1 and returns four. Require nonnegative lower bounds so the included coordinates are positive, or extend the divisor limit and remove(0,0) as in the recorded general correction.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The exercise allows integer rectangle bounds without a positivity condition. For (-1,1] by (0,2], three pairs are coprime, but the displayed sum gives four because it counts (0,2). Nonnegative lower bounds repair the intended positive-input formula.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

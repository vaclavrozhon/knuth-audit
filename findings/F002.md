# F002 — The fixed-point cases are split incorrectly

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–201(b), p. 471

The total is correct, but the breakdown for even n needs correction.

For even n≥4 there are four seatings without a fixed point, not two; the remainder is L_n−2 rather than L_n. Counterexample: n=4. The proof uses permutation cycles and matchings of a cycle. The total L_n+2 is correct. Confirmed in the PDF and original PostScript; no matching correction was found in the downloaded errata.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.2.1-201(b), p. 471: for even n there are four permutations moving everybody, including the two perfect adjacent-swap matchings, and L_n-2 with fixed points. At n=4 these derangements are 2143,2341,4123,4321. The total L_n+2 is correct.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

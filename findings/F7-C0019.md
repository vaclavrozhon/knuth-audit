# F7-C0019 — The projection-table relation need not be invariant

**Confirmed · low severity.** Published fascicle · Digital release 1 · 2025.

Location: Fascicle 7, answer 429, printed 252/file 267: definition of P in the second paragraph

Delete the asserted membership of P in Inv(O). The proof only needs P contained in the invariant relation R, so its conclusion remains valid.

For the Boolean OR clone and binary projections, P={0011,0101}, but their coordinatewise OR is 0111 outside P. Thus P need not belong to Inv(O).

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The projection-table subset P in answer 429 need not be invariant under the clone: for Boolean OR, 0011 OR 0101 = 0111 is outside P. The September amendment P subset R is correct but does not remove the preceding false membership claim. The proof is repaired by using the invariance of R, already established.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

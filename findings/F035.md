# F035 — A boundary value need not become positive

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer SAT 345, p. 615

The proof needs nonnegativity and a limiting argument.

On the boundary, reducing one probability need not make M_G positive. Two edges with p′=(1/2,…,1/2) still give zero after p1′ is reduced to 1/4. Nonnegativity and a limiting construction suffice, so the main conclusion remains valid. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 345: decreasing one coordinate at a boundary point need not make the polynomial strictly positive. Two disjoint edges at probabilities 1/2 retain a zero factor after just p1 is decreased. Nonnegativity and continuity suffice for the intended construction.

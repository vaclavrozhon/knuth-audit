The first formula has (sx+t)^((p-1)/2), but the following count of
values s and the algorithm concern shifts x+s. As written, for p=5,
u=1,t=0 no s yields a nontrivial gcd. Even with nonzero t, p=7,u=1,t=3
gives only s=2,5, fewer than the asserted minimum (p-1)/2=3.

Replacing sx+t by x+s restores the statement: if a^2=u and chi is
the quadratic character, the exact count is
(p+1+chi(2a)+chi(-2a))/2 = (p-1)/2 plus 0, 1, or 2.
The shifted algorithm itself is correct.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The August 2001 amendment mixes sx+t with a count over s that belongs to x+s. For p=5,u=1,t=0 there are no successful s; even nonzero t=3 at p=7,u=1 yields only two. The subsequent shift algorithm is the intended correct one.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V2-C0220 — Cantor's last sentence may need editorial clarification

**Candidate · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.6.2

Consider replacing "t(x) would divide u(x)" with "u(x) would divide t(x)". If alpha is a root of the irreducible u and t(alpha)=0, the minimal-polynomial argument gives u | t, contradicting nonzero t and deg(t)<deg(u).

Candidate only: the recorded evidence does not yet establish a book error.

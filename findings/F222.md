# F222 — Reflection ranks free literals as a conflict

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / SAT answer272, printed600/file620: Reflection ranks free literals as a conflict

Rank free literals above the current level and rank false ones by their actual level.

At the stated insertion point d already equals d-prime, so two free reflected literals pass the conflict test.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

At the stated insertion point in SAT272, d already denotes the backjumped level. Giving free literals rank d therefore misclassifies a reflected clause with two free literals as conflicting. The symmetric four-clause witness has a legitimate free reflected clause. Free and satisfied literals need a rank above all false-literal levels.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

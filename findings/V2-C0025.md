# V2-C0025 — Euler differentiation has a factorial and sign error

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.3.2 · Answer 30; printed 569

The displayed auxiliary derivative is false; the final partition asymptotics are correct.

Replace j! by (j−1)! and reverse the sign of the regular Bernoulli sum. For n=1,j=2 the true expansion is rho^−2−1/12+rho²/240+…, compared with 2rho^−2+1/12−rho²/240+…. The discrepancy remains after the global 2021 Bernoulli convention changes. Rechecked against the first printing and all four errata layers on 6 November 2026. A recent physical printing and priority remain unverified.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The Euler derivative has the wrong factorial and regular-part sign even after applying the 2021 Bernoulli corrections.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

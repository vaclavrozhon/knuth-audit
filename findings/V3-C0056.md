# V3-C0056 — The striped-merge buffer formula has the wrong gcd term

**Confirmed · low severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Answer 5.4.9-24, printed 700: final optimal input-buffer count has +gcd(P,D)/2-1 instead of -gcd(P,D)/2

The purported optimal memory count is too large by gcd(P,D)-1 buffers when P and D are not coprime; the preceding construction is unaffected.

Substitution of the preceding optimal residue sum gives PD/2+3D/2+P/2-gcd(P,D)/2. The printed count is larger by gcd(P,D)-1; for P=D=2 it says six buffers instead of five.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The final buffer count on p. 700 needs -gcd(P,D)/2, not +gcd(P,D)/2-1. Direct substitution into the preceding formula gives PD/2+3D/2+P/2-gcd(P,D)/2. For P=D=2, the stated offsets require five buffers under the answer's own accounting, not six.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

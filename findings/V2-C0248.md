# V2-C0248 — The composed selection rule omits the first rule’s decision

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · Section 3.5 · Answer 32 · Printed page 597

A local construction used to prove closure under selection is incorrect as printed; the theorem has a direct corrected proof.

In Section3.5 answer32, the printed predicate only tests the second rule on the previously selected symbols. Let the first rule select even indices and the second select everything. At index1 the printed predicate returns1, while the actual composition must return0. Multiplying by the first rule’s current decision repairs the formula; the R5 closure theorem remains valid.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The composed selection rule on p. 597 omits the first rule's test on the current position. Taking R to select even indices and R-prime to select everything makes the printed rule select odd positions too. Multiply the two Boolean tests. The closure theorem remains true.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# F038 — The series coefficient needs index m

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / SAT 348, p. 617

The wrong index changes the derivative’s value.

The differentiated series should use a_m rather than a_n. For f(z)=1/(1−2z), n=0, r=1/4, the correct value is 2 while the printed expression gives 4/3. The theorem and proof are correct after this change. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 348: the derivative sum indexed by m requires coefficient a_m, not a_n. For f(z)=1/(1-2z), n=0, r=1/4 the printed sum is 4/3 instead of two.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

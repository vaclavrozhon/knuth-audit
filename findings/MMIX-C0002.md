# MMIX-C0002 — An extra t appears in “arent’t”

**Confirmed · low severity.** Published fascicle · Digital release 3 · 2023.

Location: MMIX / Preface, p. v, first paragraph

A minor typographical correction; no mathematical or program behavior changes.

The first paragraph of the preface prints “arent’t” where “aren’t” is intended. The extra letter is visible in the original PDF. No matching correction occurs in the repository fascicle errata; the physical printing was not checked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The preface contains the literal typo arent’t.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

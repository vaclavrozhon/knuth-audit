# F069 — The symmetry-class counts do not agree

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–162(e,h), p. 462

Two independent enumerations give 19 and 116 instead of 4 and 28.

Two independent complete enumerations give 19 and 116 D4 classes. A broader identification of patterns gives 5 and 30, so a convention change alone does not explain the discrepancy; five explicit inequivalent examples are available. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript; the known 2023 correction does not change the counts.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

DLX 162(e,h): the full geometric counts are 19 and 116 classes under the eight symmetries of the square, not 4 and 28. The labeled counts split as 144=17·8+2·4 and 896=108·8+8·4. Even merging inner patterns according to the suggested exact-cover encoding gives 5 and 30, so that alternative interpretation does not rescue the two claims.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

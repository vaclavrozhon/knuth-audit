# V4A-F0133 — Subset and superset subfamilies need an existential condition

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Appendix B, printed 826, subset and superset family entries

Use “for some T in G” in both set-builders, as in the correct definitions on page 669.

The two set-builders use universal implications instead of existential conditions. For F={{1}} and G={{1},{2}}, they incorrectly give the empty family although both intended operations and the adjacent complement identities give F.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Appendix B, p. 826: the subset/superset family entries need an existential condition, not the printed implication for all members of G. For F={{1}}, G={{1},{2}}, both intended outputs are F but both printed set-builders are empty. The adjacent complement equalities and answer 7.1.4-236(a) already give the correct operations.

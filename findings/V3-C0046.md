# V3-C0046 — The shuffle answer changes t to an undefined r

**Confirmed · low severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Answer 5.3.4-13, printed667: three shuffle-mask exponents use undefined r

Undefined parameter in an otherwise valid shuffle description.

For a 2^t-line network, all three occurrences of r in the mask sequence should be t.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The three mask exponents in the shuffle answer on p. 667 use an undefined r where the defined word-size parameter is t. Replacing r by t makes the masks agree with their stated bit lengths and the construction.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

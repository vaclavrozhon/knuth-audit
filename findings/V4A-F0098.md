# V4A-F0098 — The gamma argument is shifted by one

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.5–42, parenthetical continuation, printed 772

Replace the gamma argument (x-1)/2 by (x+1)/2.

The parenthetical expression has Gamma((x-1)/2) instead of Gamma((x+1)/2).

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.5-42, p. 772: at x=5 the parenthetical expression gives 1/Gamma(2)=1, although the integer coefficient is 1/2. The gamma argument must be (x+1)/2, not (x-1)/2.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

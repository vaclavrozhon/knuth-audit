# C037 — Candidate: zero rows need a preprocessing step

**Candidate · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / exercises and answers 7.2.2.1–175–176, pp. 146, 467; MCC definition p. 93

A small scope clarification for the matrix reductions; accepted nonzero-row results remain valid.

Exercises 175–176 allow general matrices, while MCC requires every option to contain a primary item. A zero row becomes an invalid empty option. Tiny examples lose independent choices: 1 instead of 2, 2 instead of 3, and 1 instead of 2. Removing zero rows and restoring their choices, or adding a primary tag with lower bound zero, fixes the issue. This remains a candidate because the MCC domain restriction or preprocessing may be implicit.

This earlier candidate or dismissed entry was outside the 806 confirmed-card publication review.

# F160 — The predictable-width Azuma extension needs a fixed quadratic bound

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / Mathematical Preliminaries Redux, p. 9, sentence after (38)

The unrestricted extension is false. Keep predictable endpoints but require a deterministic upper bound on the sum of squared widths. The fixed-width bound and the hashing applications remain valid.

The sentence after (38) allows arbitrary history-dependent interval endpoints. A fair three-atom sequence has P(Y1+Y2≥1)=4/5, while its squared-width sum is 17/4 or 29. The printed exponential bound fails on a positive-mass branch. Even averaging the two exponentials gives a value strictly below 11106/14167<4/5. All intervals have positive width and all atoms have positive probability.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Page 9 permits arbitrary predictable interval widths in the displayed Azuma bound. The three-atom bounded fair-increment example has tail probability 4/5, exceeding even the average of the proposed random bound. A fixed upper bound on the sum of squared widths repairs the statement. The ordinary fixed-width applications remain valid.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# F165 — The profit sum counts one extra trial

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / Mathematical Preliminaries Redux, answer67(c), p.380

Sum from0 through n−1 and set Z₀=0. The martingale assertion remains true; the printed sequence is a one-step shift of the intended one.

Exercise67(c) defines the profit after n trials, but the answer sums increments from0 through n. After two initial losses, its n=1 expression is−3 although the profit after one trial is−1.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer MPR67(c) sums Y0 through Yn, counting n+1 bets for the profit after n bets. Two initial losses make the purported one-bet profit -3 instead of -1. Sum only through n-1. Both the intended process and its shifted version are martingales.

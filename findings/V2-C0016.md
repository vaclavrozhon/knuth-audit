# V2-C0016 — Pearson used published roulette records

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.3.1 · p. 55

The historical anecdote misstates the source of the observations.

Pearson’s 1897 account says he obtained published records from Le Monaco. Its table of 4,274 runs is the same table cited in his 1900 paper. The book describes the observations as his personal experience. This finding concerns the source of those data; it does not assert that he never visited Monte Carlo.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Pearson’s own account identifies published records, contrary to the book’s description of his personal experience of the observations.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# F030 — The table’s probabilities do not sum to 1

**Confirmed · high severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer SAT 321, p. 611

Two incorrect terms produce a total of about 1.796.

Two entries in one table are incorrect: the opposite pair needs 2θ′c(1−c)², and all four need 2θ²c². For p=3/10, the printed masses sum to 1.795530737…; the corrected masses sum to 1 and agree with the book’s numerical row. Checked by symbolic derivation and in the PDF and PostScript.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 321, p. 611: in the opposite-pair mass replace 2 theta' (1-c)^3 by 2 theta' c(1-c)^2; in the all-four mass restore the factor two before theta^2 c^2. With multiplicities 1,4,4,2,4,1 the printed masses sum to about 1.79553 at p=0.3. The corrected expressions match the book's numerical line and follow from independently turning on missing events in the boundary distribution.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

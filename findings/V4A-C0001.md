# V4A-C0001 — Possible missing n > 1 assumption in the roots-of-unity exercise

**Candidate · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / exercise 7–20, p.37; answer 7–20, p.518

If order-one orthogonal arrays are allowed, the two transformed rows need not be orthogonal.

For n=1, the two-row, one-column array [[0],[0]] satisfies the printed pairwise definition in exercise 7–18. Replacing 0 by exp(2πi·0/1) gives rows (1) and (1), whose scalar product is 1. The answer uses the vanishing sum of roots of unity, which requires n>1. Original typesetting and five relevant errata layers were checked. This remains a candidate because an implicit exclusion of order one may be intended; it is not a confirmed defect.

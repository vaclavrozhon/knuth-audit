# F008 — The identity is missing an assumption

**Confirmed · high severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / MPR121, p.24 and answer p.390; introduction to MPR124, p.25

Without absolute continuity, one expression is finite while the others are infinite.

The first divergence expectation loses target mass outside the source support. For x=(1,0),y=(1/2,1/2), it is−1/2 while the other expressions are+infinity, and E rho(X)=1/2 instead of1. The same issue recurs in the introduction to124: f=1 gives1 versus1/2. Original ID retained.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

MPR 121 and introduction to 124, pp. 24–25,390: the expectation identities require y(t)=0 whenever x(t)=0. With X=0 surely and Y uniform on {0,1}, E(rho(X)*log_2(rho(X)))=-1/2 while the divergence sum is infinite, and E(rho(X))=1/2 rather than one. For f=1 the introductory sampling identity also fails. The finite-divergence theorem in 124 already enforces the missing support condition.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

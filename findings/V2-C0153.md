# V2-C0153 — The added AKS theorem quantifies the wrong symbol

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · Section 4.5.4 · Printed page 396

The 2005 addition uses the polynomial indeterminate z where it needs the integer parameter a. This symbol substitution is confirmed; the separate question about omitted preconditions remains candidate C 0155.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The 2005 replacement's quantified integer is visibly z, although z is the polynomial indeterminate and the bound belongs to the integer a. Retain only this variable typo; this card does not certify all hypotheses of the abbreviated AKS theorem.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

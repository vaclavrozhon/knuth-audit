# V3-C0030 — The unsafe Quicksort variant overruns the lower sentinel in phase two

**Confirmed · low severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Volume3 / §5.2.2 answer18, p.631, final description of the second and third partition phases

The explanatory trace is wrong; the original algorithm and the warning against non-strict scan tests remain correct.

Answer5.2.2-18 says that a third partition moves the original sentinel to position2 after both scan conditions are changed to non-strict comparisons. With N2, M1, input(0,0) and strict sentinels(-1,1), the first phase swaps R0/R1 and leaves the active interval unchanged. In phase two the new pivot is-1, and Q4 passes indices2,1,0 before reading undefined K[-1]. No third phase is determined. The complete hand trace, original typesetting and all five errata layers were checked; the2016 correction changes punctuation only.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

In the deliberately modified Quicksort with non-strict scans, equal keys cause an out-of-range read already in the second partition phase, not the third as p. 631 says. For N=2 and cutoff M=1, two zero keys with sentinels -1 and 1 suffice: the second Q4 reaches index -1. This does not criticize the unmodified algorithm.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

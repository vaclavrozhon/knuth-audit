# V4A-F0017 — The outer permutation masks cannot be paired positionwise

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / answer7.1.3-51, p.591

The purported mask characterization admits networks that compute a different permutation, in both parts of the answer.

With d=2,c=2, the permitted masks0001,0011,0001 send input0001 to1000, whereas both requested permutations send it to0100. The outer masks must account for the intervening permutation; equal positionwise masks need not work.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 51, p. 591: for d=2,c=2, the allowed masks 0001,0011,0001 send 0001 to 1000 instead of 0100. Pairing outer masks by position ignores the intervening permutation. Both part (a) and the even-c branch of (b) admit this example.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

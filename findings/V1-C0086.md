# V1-C0086 — The published Chebyshev entry has Volume 4B page numbers

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 errata · all1 PDF p. 16; err1 PDF p. 14

This is a public-errata defect; it does not establish errors on original Volume 1 pp. 377 or 388.

The Volume 1 errata give Chebyshev’s targets as 377 and 388, which fit Volume 4B but have no matching Volume 1 content. The supported Volume 1 targets are 98, 104 and 472. This is one defect repeated in all1 and err1; the proposed repair preserves the expanded name. A complete later printed index has not been inspected.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The public index erratum repeats targets belonging to Volume 4B rather than the Volume 1 inequalities.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

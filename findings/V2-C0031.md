# V2-C0031 — Four auxiliary errors in the walk asymptotics

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.3.2 · Printed 571

The displayed derivation has incorrect intermediate expressions; an independent uniform proof confirms the final result.

The initial coefficient counts the complementary tail, the linear term of g has the wrong sign, the normalized Gaussian integral needs 1/sqrt(2pi), and the final remainder needs m+3n instead of m−3n. The first contour integral through +1 and the scaling of higher coefficients are correct. Rechecked on 6 November 2026; latest physical printing and priority remain unverified.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Four intermediate steps in the walk asymptotic are incorrect, although the main result survives; the first contour identity is not among the errors.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

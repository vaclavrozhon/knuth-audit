# V2-C0046 — The type-3 orbit does not have the claimed lattice

**Confirmed · high severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.3.4 · Answer 20 · p. 581

The proposed reduction can return an incorrect largest hyperplane spacing for an actual multiplicative-generator orbit.

For m=16, a=3 and seed 1, the four points are (1,3), (3,9), (9,11), (11,1), divided by 16. Their difference group already contains Z². Its dual requires u+3v=0 modulo 8 and has squared minimum 8, giving spacing 1/sqrt(8). The printed reduction modulo 4 gives squared minimum 2 and spacing 1/sqrt(2). The 2005 seed correction does not fix this. Integer normals for a type-3 orbit generally require modulus m/2. RANDU has witnesses showing that its displayed nu values in dimensions 2–9 remain unchanged; that fact does not establish the same density normalization for its merit scores. Original pages and all errata layers rechecked on 6 November 2026. Later physical printing and priority remain unverified.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

For `m=16,a=3`, the odd-seed orbit is `1,3,9,11`. Its difference lattice has dual condition `u+3v=0 mod 8`, with minimum squared norm 8. The proposed reduction modulo `m/4=4` instead gives 2. Reflecting alternate coordinates does not put all starting states in one class modulo 4. The 2005 seed-residue correction does not repair this distinction. The displayed RANDU minima happen to agree and are not themselves counterexamples.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

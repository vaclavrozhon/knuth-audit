# V3-C0015 — The median-of-three exchange formula fails at the allowed cutoff M=2

**Confirmed · low severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Volume 3 / answer 5.2.2-56, p. 639

The formula yields a negative expected number of exchanges for three records.

The corrected exercise assumption is M>1, so M=2 is allowed. For N=3, median selection already orders all three distinct keys; the partition has A_3=1, C_3=2 and B_3=0. The stated B_N=(C_N-3A_N)/5 gives -1/5. The partition toll (N-4)/5 needs an exception at N=3; this does not refute the recommended cutoff M=9. Original typesetting and all relevant archived errata layers checked; no matching correction found.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The median-of-three exchange formula on p. 639 fails for the allowed cutoff M=2. At N=3 the median step orders the three records, with A=1, C=2 and B=0, whereas (C-3A)/5=-1/5. The stated practical optimum M=9 is not contradicted.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V3-C0080 — Earlier retrieval methods are not all linear

**Confirmed · low severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Printed574: comparison with earlier secondary-key methods

Restrict the comparison to the relevant methods and state its storage and data assumptions.

The blanket order-N claim conflicts with the earlier k-d bounds and compound sorted lists.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The general comparison on p. 574 contradicts the earlier balanced k-d tree bound on p. 566. With two coordinates and one unspecified, that bound is O(sqrt(N)), the same exponent as Rivest's scheme, rather than order N. Compound sorted lists on p. 567 give another counterexample for fixed attributes.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

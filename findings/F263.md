# F263 — The pruned resolution tree can still be awkward

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / SAT answer 225, p.592: pruning does not immediately make the resolution tree non-awkward

The intermediate claim of non-awkwardness is false. The proof needs another pruning step or size induction; the regularization theorem remains valid.

The construction in answer 225 shortens the tree but does not necessarily eliminate every resolution variable from its conclusion.

## Publication review, 17 November 2026

Resolve D1=x OR y OR z with D2=x OR NOT y OR z on y, and D3=NOT x OR y OR z with D4=NOT x OR y OR NOT z on z; then resolve on x. The printed construction replaces the left subtree by D1 but retains the right subtree, so the result still resolves on z while concluding y OR z.

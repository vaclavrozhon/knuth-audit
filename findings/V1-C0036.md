# V1-C0036 — The 2018 replacement adds an extra exponential to a bound

**Confirmed · high severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · §2.2.3 · Answer 13 replacement dated March 15, 2018

The cited improved bound is transcribed incorrectly in the 2018 erratum. Remove the outer exp.

The replacement answer13 prints exp(B_n), while Brightwell–Tetali Theorem1.3 gives B_n=(2 e n 2^n/r)^r times the product of binom(n,k)!, with r=(2^(n+1)-2)/(n+1). The extra exponential preserves an upper bound but defeats the claimed improvement: exp(B_n)>U_n for every n>=1, whereas at n=16 the correct bound satisfies B_16<U_16. The original typeset replacement and primary theorem were compared. This exponential is absent from our 1997 book; that page has a separate, already published asymptotic correction. All four errata layers were checked again.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The added answer inserts an outer exponential absent from the primary theorem and loses the claimed improvement.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

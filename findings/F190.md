# F190 — Five-clue lower bound needs solvability

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / DLX exercise394, printed175/file195: Five-clue lower bound needs solvability

Restrict the assertion to solvable puzzles, or state zero or at least four solutions.

Five locally consistent strong clues can have no Latin-square completion, contradicting the unqualified lower bound of four solutions.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Exercise 394 needs the assumption that the puzzle is solvable. Four clues 1,2,3,4 in the first column and a 5 in row 5, column 2 are locally consistent but have no completion. The immediately preceding exercises explicitly include puzzles with zero solutions, so solvability is not implicit in this usage.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V2-C0169 — The norm-product identity has the wrong sign

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.5.4 · printed 417, 670

The central composition identity and its use in Fact2 are incorrect as printed.

The first coordinate must be x1*x2+a*y1*y2. With a=2 and both pairs(1,1), the printed minus gives norm-7 instead of1; the error also survives modulo5.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The norm-composition identity needs a plus sign in x1*x2+a*y1*y2 when the second coordinate is x1*y2+x2*y1. With a=2 and both input pairs (1,1), the printed output has norm -7 instead of 1; the corrected output (3,2) has norm 1. The error appears both in the hint and Fact 2.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# F7-C0005 — Answer 40 allows a visible junction with four branches

**Confirmed · medium severity.** Published fascicle · Digital release 1 · 2025.

Location: Fascicle 7 / 7.2.2.3, answer 40, p167

The conditions need to exclude coincident projected edge crossings. This affects the sufficiency of answer 40; the qualitative rotation-stability definition on p. 11 is not refuted.

Three disjoint tetrahedra satisfy both corrected quantitative conditions, but three projected edges meet at one nonvertex point. Occlusion leaves four visible branches, violating the required HC-picture degrees 2 or 3. All exact incidence tests, local visibility, original pages and relevant errata are documented; the main reviewer has independently accepted this finding.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Even after applying the September 2026 amendment, answer 40 permits three projected edges to cross at one nonvertex point. The explicit three-tetrahedron configuration meets both printed conditions yet has four visible branches there, contrary to the HC-picture definition. This is a defect in the sufficient conditions, not in the theory assuming genuine general position.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

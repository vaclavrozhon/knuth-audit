# F211 — Search-profile asymptotic omits the endpoint regime

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / SAT answer215, printed590/file610: Search-profile asymptotic omits the endpoint regime

Restrict the interior formula to alpha>(7/6)ln4 and handle boundary maxima separately.

The stationary-point formula is used for every fixed positive clause density, although its point may lie beyond depthn.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

SAT215's stationary-point formula requires alpha>(7/6)*ln(4). At alpha=1 the maximum is at the endpoint t=1/2, and the proposed stationary depth lies outside the tree. At equality the Gaussian is one-sided. The exact profile and alpha=4 example remain valid; the 2018 expansion correction does not supply the missing restriction.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V2-C0057 — Wrong left-hand index in the bit-generator cost formula

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.4.1 · Exercise 27 · p. 589

An index typo in the displayed cost formula; it does not change the algorithm or its mathematical analysis.

The final display starts with A_m, while every expression on its right uses n. The left side must be A_n. The values A_1=2 and A_2=8/3 rule out an independent index m. The recurrence, finite sum, periodic term and generator are correct. Original type and all errata layers were rechecked on 6 November 2026.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The final displayed mean has a free left index `m` where all right-hand quantities and the recurrence use `n`. Since `A_1=2` and `A_2=8/3`, the indices cannot be independent. Retain as a local subscript typo.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

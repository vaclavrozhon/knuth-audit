**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · §2.3.4.6 · Exercise 4 answer · Printed pages 600–601

Two auxiliary assertions fail for the explicitly included empty tree; the main bijection remains correct.

# F133 — P and Y pentomino frequencies are too small

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / exercise 7.2.2.1–134, p. 450

Two entries in the complete frequency table are incorrect: P should be 1685 and Y 243.

Answer 134 prints 1672 occurrences of P and 242 of Y. Two complete independent enumerations agree on all 13,328 symmetry classes, and two geometric component checks give 1685 P and 243 Y. A standalone certificate already exhibits these larger counts in 1734 legal, inequivalent tilings; no search completeness assumption is needed to refute the printed numbers. The other ten entries and the unique four-pentomino solution agree. The same text occurs in the 2019 author draft; no matching correction was found in six errata layers checked against the official files on November 2, 2026. Priority and physical-copy verification remain separate.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 134: the component multiplicities are P=1,685 and Y=243, not 1,672 and 242. The small explicit certificate alone exceeds both printed counts; the complete stored census agrees and uses the corrected symmetry convention.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

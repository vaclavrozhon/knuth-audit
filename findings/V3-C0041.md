## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The six-key decision tree on p. 196 has leaf levels 9 and 10, not 10 and 11, with the root at level zero. A full binary tree whose leaves all had depth at least 10 would need at least 1024 leaves, but this tree has 720. The corresponding main-text and answer counts are consistent with 9 and 10.

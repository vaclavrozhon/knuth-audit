# F086 — The parameter g is a lower bound on code size

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / Algorithm C, p. 45–46

The algorithm also outputs larger sets than its description promises.

The parameter g is a lower bound on size. With m=2 and g=2, the algorithm gives 15 two-word codes and 14 three-word codes, although the description says sets of g. Change this to at least g, or visit only when s=0. Checked by independent exhaustive enumeration and the original C program, and confirmed in the PDF and PostScript.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Algorithm C specifies sets of exactly g words but visits sets of at least g words. At m=2,g=2, its first output has three words; the completed small enumeration gives fifteen two-word and fourteen three-word outputs. The remaining slack is not tested at the visit.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

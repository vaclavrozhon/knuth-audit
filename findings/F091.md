# F091 — The probability belongs to a different threshold

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / main text p. 49, binomial tree

The printed number corresponds to l ≥ 21, not l ≥ 20.

For n=100, Pr(l≥20) is 1.951987e−8; the printed 2.7e−9 corresponds to l≥21. Checked by exact convolution and Stirling numbers. Confirmed in the PDF, PostScript, and April 2026 preview.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Page 49: for n=100 the stated 2.7·10^(−9) is the tail probability at termination level at least 21, not at least 20. The latter is 1.9519870726687698·10^(−8). The distinction follows from level=number of chosen entries+1 and the printed generating function in answer 52.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

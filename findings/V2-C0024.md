# V2-C0024 — The zero-spacing contribution needs a plus sign

**Confirmed · high severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.3.2 · Answer 28(c); printed 568

The printed exact probability is wrong; the numerical table on page 71 is already correct.

For R=0, cases with zero and one zero-length spacing are disjoint and must be added. Replace the minus before (1/2)p_(n−1)(m−binom(n,2)) by plus. With n=2,m=3 every one of the nine birthday pairs has R=0, so the probability is 1; the printed expression gives 1/3. Rechecked against the first printing and all four errata layers on 6 November 2026. A recent physical printing and priority remain unverified.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The zero-spacing cases are disjoint contributions and require a plus sign; the printed difference fails for two birthdays in three days.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

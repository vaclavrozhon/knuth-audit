# V4A-F0030 — The parity example reports zero instead of two basis vectors

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.1.3-194, printed page 618: r in the n=9 example

A numerical value in the worked example contradicts its displayed basis; the no-perfect-solution conclusion remains valid.

The n=9 example states r=0 while displaying two independent nullspace vectors. Its five displayed gamma vectors have rank three, so r=2.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 194, p. 618: the five displayed vectors have rank three and nullity two. The example's r=0 contradicts the two displayed basis vectors and must be r=2. The earlier initialization r=0 is correct.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

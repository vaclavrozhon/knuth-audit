# V2-C0114 — Extending division by zero requires changing the exception branch

**Candidate · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.5.1 · Exercise 8

The extended arithmetic is consistent, but the claim that the routine needs no change is ambiguous.

Answer4 explicitly rejects a zero divisor. To obtain the finite-nonzero rule x/0=sign(x)·infinity in answer8, that exception branch must change and the sign-at-zero case must be handled. This remains a candidate: the author may mean that the arithmetic core is unchanged once exception handling is extended.

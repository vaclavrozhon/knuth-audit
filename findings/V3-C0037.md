# V3-C0037 — The distribution formula can address a bin that was not allocated

**Confirmed · low severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Printed176: floor(N/8) bins are insufficient for the index floor(KN/8)

An implementation following the literal allocation can access one bin beyond its storage; the asymptotic method is unaffected.

The stated bin count rounds down, but the index formula can attain that count whenever N is not divisible by8.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

On p. 176, floor(N/8) allocated bins do not suffice for the assigned index floor(KN/8), for keys K in [0,1). For N=17 and K=33/34 the index is 2, outside the two bins 0 and 1. A ceiling in the allocation, or consistent rescaling of the index, repairs this.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

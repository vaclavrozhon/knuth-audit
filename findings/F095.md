# F095 — The supposedly impossible pattern has eight orientations

**Confirmed · high severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2–64, p. 409, pattern B/A/D

An explicit three-dimensional witness exists.

The pattern claimed to be impossible has eight valid orientations: hide A and its opposite face, and fill the remaining faces with O. Rotating D by 90° gives the intended impossible pattern. Checked by an explicit 3D witness and all 24 rotations, and confirmed in the PDF and PostScript. No matching erratum was found.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 64: hiding the rotated-A face and its opposite allows the illustrated B/A/D pattern, with O on the other faces, to contribute legitimate letters in eight orientations. Turning the displayed D by ninety degrees produces the intended impossible pattern. The actual puzzle's solution counts are unaffected.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

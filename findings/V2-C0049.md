# V2-C0049 — The cosecant sum has a missing constant term

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.3.4 · Answer 25 · p. 582

The integral replacement and the refined asymptotic constant are incorrect; the logarithmic upper bound remains valid.

Subtracting 1/(pi*x) from the cosecant removes its singularity. The harmonic part contributes Euler’s constant gamma, giving f(M)=(ln M+gamma+ln(2/pi))/pi+O(1/M). The printed integral lacks gamma/pi, so their difference does not tend to zero. The later full-sum formula needs e^gamma in place of e. The leading logarithm and the discrepancy estimates remain correct. Original pages and all errata layers rechecked on 6 November 2026. Later physical printing and priority remain unverified.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Subtracting `1/(pi x)` from the cosecant summand gives the asymptotic `(log M+gamma+log(2/pi))/pi+O(1/M)`. The integral estimate omits the nonvanishing `gamma/pi` term, so its claimed error is false. The leading logarithm is correct; the constant is standard and is not presented as newly discovered mathematics.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# F146 — Cell 22 cannot force cell 00 in the Y-pentomino example

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / main text 7.2.2.1, p. 111, Y-pentomino preprocessing

The second-round example names an impossible forcing cell; the observed cell is 11.

Every Y pentomino fits a 2×4 or 4×2 box. Cells 00 and 22 require a span of at least three in both directions, so no option can contain both. Algorithm P only deletes or shortens options. A nonempty column 22 therefore cannot force 00, regardless of processing order. Both the book and author draft print 22. Two short native runs with different option orders instead show 11 forcing 00 in round 2; an independent set model checks every reduction. No matching correction was found in six archived errata layers.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Page 111: no Y pentomino placement contains both 00 and 22; its bounding box is 2-by-4 or 4-by-2. Thus 22 cannot force 00 in the stated residual instance. The stored native traces support 11 as the intended item, but the geometrical contradiction establishes the error independently.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# F270 — Viennot publication year is 1986

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / main text p.267, Traces and pieces: Viennot publication year

A bibliographic date error. The theory and its mathematical applications are unchanged.

The citation on p.267 dates Lecture Notes in Mathematics 1234 to 1985, the conference year. The cited volume was published in 1986.

## Publication review, 17 November 2026

Compare the original p.267 citation with the copyright page of Combinatoire enumerative, LNM 1234. It prints 1986. Springer also gives a print-publication date of December 1,1986 and cites Viennot's pp.321–350 chapter as 1986.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

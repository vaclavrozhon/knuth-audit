# F092 — The return label overwrites the algorithm’s state

**Confirmed · critical severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2–58, p. 407

Repeated initialization leads to u = Λ on a four-vertex star.

The return label (*) must come after the initialization of u,T,S. Otherwise, a four-vertex star reaches u=Λ. The known 2025 correction does not move the label. The correction passed 162,366 BFS checks and was confirmed in the PDF and PostScript.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 58: the return label must follow the initialization of u,T,S. On the four-vertex star with edges 01,12,13, deleting vertex 1 causes the second layer to discard its next tarnished vertex and use a null vertex. The February 2025 erratum fixes other operations but leaves the label in the wrong place.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

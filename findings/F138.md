# F138 — Four incorrect symmetry histograms for 2 × 2 × 4 bricks

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–147(b), p. 457, 2 × 2 × 4 brick histograms

The book counts three classes with six distinct exterior colors, but five explicit inequivalent constructions exist.

Four rows of the 2 × 2 × 4 table misclassify solutions by exterior colors and symmetry. The total remains 244 normalized solutions and 48 classes, so a check of totals misses the errors. Two different complete solvers produced identical sets of all 244 models. A standalone Python certificate verifies five explicit constructions without searching. The same table appears in our PDF and the 2019 author draft; no matching correction was found in six public errata layers.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 147(b): four entries in the 2-by-2-by-4 brick symmetry histograms are wrong. The stored witnesses and automorphism checks give (0,3,4,1), (0,11,12,2), (0,2,2,4), (0,1,3,1) for the four affected color patterns. The total number of classes remains 48.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V1-C0065 — A harmonic sum ends at the wrong index

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · Exercise 43 · page 617

The displayed equality is false; the final allocation bound survives the repair.

The left sum ends at 2m+1, while the alternating sum on the right equals H(2m−1) − H(m−1). The left endpoint must therefore be 2m−1.

For m=2 the printed sides are 77/60 and 5/6. Correcting the endpoint preserves the inequality with ln 2 and the final first-fit bound.

# V2-C0117 — The achieved timing maximum needs a one-bit exception

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.5.2

The upper bound remains valid, but the claimed equality and smallest-input construction exclude the one-bit case.

For N=1 the only positive input pair is(1,1), taking19 cycles, while13N+8 is21. For N≥2 the stated maximum is attained. The answer37 construction also needs n≥2; for n=1 its pair(2,0) is outside the positive-input domain.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

At N=1 the only positive pair is (1,1), whose MIX time is nineteen rather than the claimed maximum twenty-one. The attaining pair in answer 37 also needs n>=2; at n=1 it gives the forbidden zero input. Retain as a minor one-bit exception, not a failure of the main asymptotic bound.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V2-C0126 — The residue derivatives must be evaluated at −1

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.5.2 · Exercise 30

The printed expansion uses the wrong derivative values; one printed derivative is not finite.

The double pole of the Mellin integrand is at s=−1. Its residue therefore contains a′(−1) or b′(−1), not the values at+1 printed in the answer. In particular b′(1) is not finite. The stored local residue calculation and the complete corrected expansions establish the correction.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The first two residue expansions require derivatives at -1, not at 1. The double pole is at s=-1, and the printed b(s) itself has a pole at 1. Re-reading the definitions and residue coefficient confirms this local substitution error.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# F172 — The cut argument takes the wrong order closure

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / Mathematical Preliminaries Redux, answer109(c), p.386

Use the lower closure A=(P\J)↓ and update the closure statement to A=A↓ or B=B↑. The complete corrected cut proof establishes the original theorem.

The proof chooses the upper closure of P\J but then applies a lower-set probability inequality. A two-state example with positive weights makes its displayed bound5/4≥1/2≥1.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The same answer needs the lower closure of the complement of J, not its upper closure. On the two-element chain with marginals (3/4,1/4) and (1/4,3/4), the printed inequality chain becomes 5/4 >= 1/2 >= 1. Reversing the closure and adjusting the subsequent closure identity repairs the cut proof.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

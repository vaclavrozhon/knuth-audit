# V2-C0006 — The 2016 exact equality needs a floor

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · Public errata all2-pre, p. 13 · Answer 3.2.1.3–8

The exact quantized-output formula in the public erratum fails for an actual generated triple.

The original answer uses an approximation. A 2016 public erratum changes it to exact equality without adding a floor. The generated triple (6,16,3) at indices 75–77 would require 6ε₁−9ε₂ ≡ 11 (mod 20), impossible for 0≤ε₁,ε₂<1. Adding the outer floor repairs the equation. Original p. 551 is related evidence only.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

# F012 — The hypergeometric parameter is −n, not −N

**Confirmed · high severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer MPR 132(a), p. 393

The printed expression gives the wrong probability polynomial.

The upper parameter −N should be −n. For N=4, K=2, n=1, the printed formula gives 1/2+2z+z² instead of (1+z)/2. The correction agrees with 1,767 regular polynomials. So far this is confirmed only in the PDF; the downloaded author’s draft does not include the exercise. No matching correction was found in the complete 4B and Fascicle 5 errata; the physical copy has not been checked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer MPR 132(a), p. 393: the second upper hypergeometric parameter is -n, not -N. At N=4,K=2,n=1 the printed expression is 1/2+2z+z^2, although the probability generating polynomial is (1+z)/2.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

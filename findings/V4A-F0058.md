# V4A-F0058 — A cycle splice uses the wrong endpoint

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer7.2.1.1-45(d), printed686: Case2 inserted edges

The corrected pair uses all four exposed endpoints once and consists of cube edges.

The first inserted edge joins u xor2 to v xor3, changing two bits, and both inserted edges use v xor3. Replace its endpoint by v xor2.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.1-45(d), p. 686: the inserted edge (u XOR 2)--(v XOR 3) changes two bits and duplicates the exposed v XOR 3 endpoint. Its second endpoint must be v XOR 2. The same error occurs in the 2009 replacement.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

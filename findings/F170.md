# F170 — The first coupling recurrence uses the wrong successor state

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / Mathematical Preliminaries Redux, answer103(b), p.385

Replace g112 by g122 in this first recurrence. The final generating function, uniform output law and both stopping-time moments are correct.

Answer103(b) uses g112 where the initial state012 moves to122. For output2, the printed recurrence gives coefficient[z²]=0, while the two-bit word11 has probability1/4.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer MPR103(b): the first successor of state 012 under bit 1 is 122, not 112. The marked output-2 coefficient at two steps is 1/4 on the left and zero in the printed recurrence. The final generating function already uses the correct transition.

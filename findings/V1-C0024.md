# V1-C0024 — The added mean-time formula has the wrong harmonic term and remainder

**Confirmed · high severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · §1.3.2 · Added answer 12 · Erratum of April 24, 1997

The added expected-time formula has the wrong harmonic coefficient and an insufficient error bound; the saddle-search algorithm itself is correct.

The first stated expected running time does not match Solution 1 on
page 513. In particular, its harmonic term is 5m H_n; the displayed
program requires 7m H_n. The claimed exponentially small remainder
also does not cover the saving from stopping at a saddle point.

Here m and n are the numbers of rows and columns, H_n=sum(1/k,k=1..n),
and all mn distinct entries are uniformly permuted. Times below are
in units u and exclude the final HLT.

A row-minimum scan costs 6n+4+7K, where K counts new strict minima after
the first entry. A nonrecord entry takes 6u, while a new minimum takes
13u: DEC1, DEC2, J2Z, CMPX, JL, JG, LDX, ENT3, INC3, ST2.
Since E K=H_n-1, this is 6n+7H_n-3 on average.

Here is a precise formula that also tracks early termination. Write
B=binom(m+n,m) and P=(m+n)/B. If all rows were processed even after a
saddle was found, the mean total number D of column comparisons would
be, for n>1,

D = n(m+1)/(n-1) - (m+n)^2/((n-1)B).

To derive this, number rows i=0,...,m-1 in processing order and let
U be their minimum after replacing random ranks by independent uniform
(0,1) entries. Then a_t=E U^t=1/binom(n+t,t), and

E D_i = sum(t=0..m-1) a_(t-[i<t]),
  D = sum(t=0..m-1) ((m-t)a_t+t a_(t-1)).

The second summand at t=0 is zero. The indicator accounts for the
comparison with the row minimum itself. Summing the beta integrals
gives the displayed value of D; no independence of the D_i is needed.

The cost W_i of a completed row, including its column check and its
transition or successful return, is 6n+8+7K_i+5D_i. Therefore the actual
expected time, without HLT, is exactly

A - 5(m+n)^2/((n-1)B) - Delta,
  A = 6mn+7m H_n+6m+6+5(m+1)/(n-1).

Delta is the mean cost of the rows omitted after a saddle is found.
There is at most one saddle; each row contains it with probability
P/m. Since 6n+13 <= W_i <= 13n+5m+1, one has

(6n+13)(m-1)P/2 <= Delta <= (13n+5m+1)(m-1)P/2.

In particular, on square matrices m=n=k, the discrepancy from the
published expression has leading size 2k(H_k-1), not an exponentially
small size. Even after replacing 5m H_n+8m by 7m H_n+6m, the stated
O((m+n)^2/B) remainder is too small: the lower bound on Delta is of
order k^3/B. Both corrections to A are negative, so cannot cancel.
The exact formula and the bounds above avoid making an unsupported
claim about a sharper remainder. A safe bound is O(m(m+n)^2/B).

The second expected-time formula in the same addition is consistent
with its program when HLT is excluded; its remainder has an independent
proof in notes/mixal-saddle.md.

The relevant Hofri-Jacquet preprint was read, but its generic instruction
multipliers were not substituted for an actual MIX instruction count.
Independent finite exact expectations, direct instruction execution,
and exhaustive small matrices agree (results/mixal-saddle.json).

This is a finding about the published added answer and the displayed
program, not a claim that the saddle search is incorrect. Priority and
inspection of a physical printed copy remain unverified.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Independent instruction counts confirm the wrong harmonic coefficient; skipped-row bounds refute the claimed absolute remainder.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

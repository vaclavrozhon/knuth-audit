# C029 — The triangle argument may need projection rather than containment

**Candidate · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–116(a), p. 444

A general sentence is too strong literally; the triangle-free induction remains valid.

For G=K3, two original vertices and the clone of the third form a triangle in the Mycielski graph. It is not contained in the original G. Replacing the clone by its original vertex gives a triangle of G, which is all the induction needs. The wording occurs in both the local PDF and author PostScript. This remains a candidate because it may be intended as shorthand within the induction.

# V4A-F0083 — The multiplicity threshold loses its dependence on k

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Note after answer 7.2.1.4–50, Fristedt statement, printed 755

Replace C*x*sqrt(n) by x*sqrt(n)/(C*k), for fixed positive k and x.

The claimed exponential tail at threshold C*x*sqrt(n) has the wrong normalization. The threshold should be x*sqrt(n)/(C*k).

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Note after answer 7.2.1.4-50, p. 755: removing r copies of k gives Pr(X_k>=r)=p(n-kr)/p(n). With C=pi/sqrt(6), the printed threshold C*x*sqrt(n) therefore gives exp(-C^2*k*x), not exp(-x). The intended threshold is x*sqrt(n)/(C*k).

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

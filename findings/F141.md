# F141 — The chess sudoku variants were published before December 2018

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–173, p. 466, historical paragraph below Fig. A–3

A chronology error in the historical note; the puzzle solutions remain correct.

Page 466 places the first publication of both variants in the December 2018/January 2019 MAA Focus. Nacin’s own posts present the same knight rules on May 16 and bishop rules on June 6, 2018. The September 2018 College Mathematics Journal also lists his four Knightdoku puzzles. His university CV and publisher metadata corroborate the journal citation. The PDF and author draft agree; no correction was found in the relevant errata.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 173: the claimed first publication in December 2018/January 2019 is too late. Nacin's own dated posts describe knight sudoku on May 16 and bishop sudoku on June 6, 2018; the knight puzzles also appear in the September 2018 College Mathematics Journal. The October 2023 gallery erratum leaves this historical paragraph intact.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V2-C0239 — D should be alpha_5, not alpha_6

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.6.4 · Exercise 35

In the definitions following the quartic expansion, D=alpha_6 should read D=alpha_5. The displayed chain on p. 703 has five parameters, and its last step is lambda_7=alpha_5+lambda_6. There is no alpha_6.

In the definitions following the quartic expansion, D=alpha_6 should
read D=alpha_5. The displayed chain on p. 703 has five parameters,
and its last step is lambda_7=alpha_5+lambda_6. There is no alpha_6.

For example, alpha_1=...=alpha_4=0 and alpha_5=1 give x^4+1,
so the additive constant D is 1. The lower-bound argument is unchanged.

A later physical printing and prior reporting have not been checked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The five-parameter formula on p. 704 needs alpha5 in its constant term, not alpha6. The preceding derivation uses alpha1 through alpha5; setting the first four to zero and alpha5=1 must produce x⁴+1.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

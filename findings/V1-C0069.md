# V1-C0069 — A buddy-program comment names the wrong block

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · Exercise 27 · page 612, line 20

The comment misdescribes the instruction’s destination.

The instruction stores through I5, which points to P, but its comment names L. The original comment should say LINKB(P), not LINKB(L). After the 2006 field change it should say LINKF(P).

The original instruction itself is correct. This comment defect is separate from the incomplete FIFO correction.

The original pages and all four current/historical errata layers were reviewed on November 5, 2026. This does not establish priority or inspection of a later physical printing.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

A store through P is incorrectly described as a store through L.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

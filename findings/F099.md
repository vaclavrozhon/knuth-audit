# F099 — Subtract 1 after the entire product

**Confirmed · high severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / 7.2.2–79(h), p. 415

The placement of the parenthesis excludes valid organ sounds.

In h(w,z), −1 belongs after the entire product. For K11, the 32 individual pedals give 32 new triples, while the printed formula gives only eight. The corrected complete polynomials agree with an independent model; the book’s P3/P10 and Q1 through Q10 are correct. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript; an older erratum corrects a different monomial.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 79(h): subtract one after the entire product defining h, rather than within its second factor. At switch setting 11, the thirty-two pedals give thirty-two distinct three-pipe sounds, while the printed expression counts only eight. The corrected polynomial agrees with the numerical examples already in the answer.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

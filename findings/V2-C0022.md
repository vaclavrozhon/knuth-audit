# V2-C0022 — An intermediate probability sum omits G0

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.3.2 · Answer 26(c); printed 566

One lower summation index is wrong; the following final formula and moments are correct.

The sum for 1−F_k must start at G_0, since at most k−1 includes zero. For n=2,k=2,s=1/4 the left side is 1, while the printed sum is 1/2. Adding G_0 supplies the missing 1/2. Rechecked against the first printing and all four errata layers on 6 November 2026. A recent physical printing and priority remain unverified.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The event of at most k-1 small gaps includes zero; the omitted G_0 term changes the probability.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

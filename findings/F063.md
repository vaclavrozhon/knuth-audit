# F063 — The second coordinate uses kx instead of ky

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–125, p. 447

The geometric transformation fails already at (0,1).

Both triangle-coordinate formulas repeat kx in the second coordinate; that factor must be ky. Scaling by one should preserve triangle (0,1), but the printed formula moves it to (0,0). The corrected formula now has a general proof for both orientations, supported by 882 additional exact geometric cases. The earlier finding and its original 400 checks are preserved.

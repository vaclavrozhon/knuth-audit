# V1-C0092 — Empty-input timing needs a domain qualification

**Candidate · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · §2.3.1 · Printed 325–326,567–568

The algorithms handle empty trees; only the stated domain of their timing formulas is in question.

For an empty tree, T and its linked-stack variant execute only LD5 (2 u) and J5Z (1 u): 3 u, while 15n+a+4 and 30n+a+4 give 4 u at n = a = 0. Program U also takes 3 u, while its polynomial gives −3 u. The book later says assuming n≠0 when discussing a; whether that also qualifies the entire timing analysis is ambiguous. The candidate asks for an explicit n>0 restriction and the separate empty case.

This earlier candidate or dismissed entry was outside the 806 confirmed-card publication review.

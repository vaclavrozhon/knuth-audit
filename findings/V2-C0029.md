# V2-C0029 — The third-moment formula is missing a denominator

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.3.2 · Printed 572

One answer line gives the wrong third moment; the exercise statement and main derivation are correct.

The first term must be m³/N, giving E S_m³=[m³−6B(N+1)]/N. With k=2, N=3 and m=1, the true expectation is 1/3 while the printed expression gives 1. The subsequent value near −144 is correct. Rechecked on 6 November 2026; latest physical printing and priority remain unverified.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The first term in the third-moment formula lacks division by the period; a one-sign window supplies a counterexample.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

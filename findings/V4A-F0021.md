# V4A-F0021 — The strong-chain construction omits complemented Boolean operations

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: Answer 7.1.3-113, printed page 600: unchanged Boolean operations in the strong-chain conversion

The printed construction fails, although the O(r) existence claim can be repaired with two bounded steps per omitted operator.

For n=2 and x_0=0, copying NOR(x_0,x_0) yields the 2-adic value -1 instead of the n-bit result 3. Complemented Boolean operations need their own bounded implementation.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 113, p. 600: the copied NOR operation gives -1 on zero inputs as a 2-adic operation, instead of the two-bit result 3. The allowed operations on p. 155 include NOR and other complemented Boolean operations. These, and the subtraction branch's intermediate XNOR, require a bounded implementation. The existence claim itself is not refuted.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

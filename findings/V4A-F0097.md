# V4A-F0097 — The Peirce estimate uses k instead of k-1

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.5–46, first formula, printed 772

Change the exponent in the first formula to k-1.

For fixed k the leading factor is (1-xi/n)^(k-1), not (1-xi/n)^k at the stated relative accuracy.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.5-46, p. 772: the factor should have exponent k-1. At k=1 the entry is exactly B_n, whereas the printed exponent k leaves relative discrepancy xi/n, larger than O(1/n). The weaker later k=n estimate is not refuted by this change.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

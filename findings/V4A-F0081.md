# V4A-F0081 — A power of v disappears from the Jacobi calculation

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.4–23, second equality, printed 750

Multiply the inner sum by v^choose(n,2).

The geometric sum must retain the factor v^choose(n,2). The final specialization v=1 is unaffected.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.4-23, p. 750: the finite geometric sum loses v^C(n,2). At n=2 the quotient is v+v^2+v^3+v^4+v^5, not 1+v+v^2+v^3+v^4. The final specialization v=1 is unaffected. The June 2010 Fascicle 3 replacement already contains this defect.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

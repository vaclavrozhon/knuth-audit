# V1-C0075 — The AMM entry assigns its publisher too early

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · Index p. 628, AMM

A historical publisher statement conflates two dates.

The original entry says the Mathematical Association of America published the journal since 1894. Contemporary evidence instead records its transfer to the newly formed association for the 1916 volume; 1894 is the journal’s founding year. No matching correction was found in the four public layers refreshed November 5, 2026. Persistence in later complete printings is unverified.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The journal began in 1894, but the contemporary report dates its transfer to the association to the 1916 volume.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

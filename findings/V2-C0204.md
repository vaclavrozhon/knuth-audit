The average number of irreducible quadratic factors, counted with multiplicity,
is p/(p+1) times the printed expression. There are A=p(p-1)/2 such quadratics;
the expected multiplicity of any one is sum_{j=1..floor(n/2)} p^(-2j). Hence
  E = p/(2(p+1)) * (1-p^(-2 floor(n/2))).

For p=n=2 the average is 1/4, since only x^2+x+1 contributes among the four
monic quadratics. The printed expression gives 3/8. This is independent of the
missing 1- in the preceding probability formula.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The mean multiplicity of irreducible quadratic factors in the same answer is p/(2(p+1)) times (1−p^(−2 floor(n/2))). The printed expression lacks p/(p+1); p=n=2 gives 1/4 rather than 3/8.

# V2-C0002 — The cycle detector can miss even a constant sequence

**Confirmed · high severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.1, Answer 7 · p. 540

Using the stated parameter range can cause nontermination and invalidate the claimed online guarantee.

The SSY paragraph permits m=2 and g=3, but then its table-search condition is never satisfied. The detector cannot terminate even for a constant sequence. It needs 1 ≤ g < m, initial spacing of at least 1, and a qualification of its short-sequence bound. The original page and all current errata layers were reviewed again on November 6, 2026.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Permitted cycle-detector parameters can prevent every search; the original paper supplies the missing restriction.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

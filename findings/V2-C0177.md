# V2-C0177 — The divisor algorithm has the wrong denominator for mu

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.5.4 · printed 667

This is a concrete incorrect output. The corrected elimination and complete enumeration are proved.

The quadratic elimination requires2*v1*s in the denominator for mu, while the book prints2*v3*s. A saved valid input makes the printed algorithm output4 as a divisor of14.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

In L2 the denominator for mu must contain v1, not v3. For N=14,r=1,s=3, the reachable pair (v1,v3)=(2,1), k=0 and sigma=-1 makes the printed formulas output 4 as a divisor of 14. The correct denominator instead gives mu=1/2 and rejects it. Direct quadratic elimination independently gives the correction.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

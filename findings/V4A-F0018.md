# V4A-F0018 — The distance-three prefix XOR loses an incoming carry

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / answer 7.1.3-66(d), p. 593

The algorithm gives an incorrect prefix XOR once a carry has to propagate across two word boundaries. Computing the carry from v_j repairs the recurrence.

For n=192 and u=1, the printed recurrence returns zero in the third 64-bit word, although bit 129 must be set. The outgoing carry uses the local prefix t instead of the completed word v_j, so it discards propagation from the preceding word.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 66(d), p. 593: at n=192,u=1 the outgoing carry computed from the local prefix t becomes zero after the second word, losing the required third-word bit 129. It must use the completed word v_j.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

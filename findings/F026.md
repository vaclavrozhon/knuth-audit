# F026 — Strong connectivity does not guarantee an autarky

**Confirmed · high severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / p. 579, answer 7.2.2.2–162(b)

The component must also have consistent literals.

A strongly connected component must be consistent to define an autarky. Two complementary ternary clauses yield a single blocking-graph component containing all six literals. This remains true after reversing every edge, so it is a separate missing assumption. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript. No matching correction was found in the complete 4B and Fascicle 6 errata; the physical copy has not been checked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 162(b), p. 579: a strong component must be consistent to define an autarky. The two clauses (a or -b or c), (-a or b or -c) give one component containing all six literals, in the actual printed orientation too. Reversing the graph in the old audit did not affect this witness.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

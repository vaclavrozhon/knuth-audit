# V4A-F0102 — The cumulant formula reverses the Stirling indices

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.5–52, cumulant formula and final note, printed 774

Use kappa_j=exp(xi)*sum(S(j,k)*xi^k,k=1,...,j), and change the specialization to xi=1.

The sum needs S(j,k), and the Bell-number specialization requires xi=1, not xi*exp(xi)=1.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.5-52, p. 774: extracting coefficients gives exp(xi)*sum(S(j,k)*xi^k), with j above k in the Stirling number, not reversed. At j=1,xi=1/2 the printed value is twice the mean. The specialization giving e*B_j is xi=1, not xi*exp(xi)=1. The case (b) notation also needs kappa_j in place of kappa=j.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# F265 — The product construction uses more clauses than stated

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / SAT answer 411, p.632: the relaxed-product clause count is short by 2n

A clause-count typo. The corrected count still fits the stated numerical upper bound. The separate unsound full-adder relaxation remains documented in F236.

The relaxed multiplication construction yields 9mn-5m-8n clauses; the printed expression subtracts another 2n.

## Publication review, 17 November 2026

Count one clause for each initial AND, eight for each full adder, and three for each half adder. With two-bit inputs there are four initial ANDs and two half adders: 10 clauses, versus the printed 6.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

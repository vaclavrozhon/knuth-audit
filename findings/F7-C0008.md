# F7-C0008 — The six boundary cases omit a realizable W ordering

**Confirmed · medium severity.** Published fascicle · Digital release 1 · 2025.

Location: Fascicle 7 / 7.2.2.3, exercise 50(a), p118 and answer50(a), p169

Include the other boundary placements of a W junction or add a hypothesis that excludes them. Under the stated realizability premise the six-case construction omits this legal input. The six printed matrices and the previously checked example counts are unaffected.

A simple eight-vertex polygon with one inner chord is biconnected, has noT junctions and admits a complete valid labeling. At one reflex boundary corner its actual order is W(h,b,c), but exercise 50(a) only permits W(b,c,h) for those boundary neighbors. Exact ray determinants disprove that order. Original pages and all relevant errata were checked; the main reviewer has independently accepted this finding.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The six cases in exercise 50(a) omit another boundary placement of a W junction. The simple, biconnected eight-vertex picture with chord ac realizes W(h,b,c), not the listed W(b,c,h). The new November 6 erratum restricts the algorithm in the last paragraph of answer 50(c); it does not amend the six-case assertion in part (a). The separate shared-inner-line defect is already known and is not counted here.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

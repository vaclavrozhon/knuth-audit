# V4A-F0052 — Wrong range in the sweetness recurrence

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.1.4-247, printed 674: conjunction upper bound

Repair the recurrence range; the numerical census is not independently refuted.

The bound is printed as 2^(k-1), with k undefined. The odd/even subsequences have 2^(n-1) entries, so the index bound must be 2^(n-1)-1.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 247, p. 674: k is undefined in the conjunction bound 2^(k-1). With 2^n inputs, the correct largest index j is 2^(n-1)-1; merely changing k to n still permits an out-of-range input. No additional defect in the base case or census is claimed.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# C006 — The modulus formula puts the minus sign in the wrong exponent

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / DLX answer 189(a), p.469: misplaced minus sign in the exponential modulus

A false intermediate identity; subsequent estimates use the intended negative exponent. The asymptotic conclusion is unaffected by this correction.

Answer 189(a) gives exp(exp(-x) cos y), but the modulus is exp(-exp(x) cos y). The same typo survives in the 2019 replacement answer.

## Publication review, 17 November 2026

Set z=x+iy=0. The modulus |exp(-exp(0))| is 1/e, while the printed right side is e. In general, use |exp(w)|=exp(Re(w)).

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# C006 — A published correction has an exponential sign error

**Candidate · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / p. 469, answer 189(a); errata 4f5.277

This is tracked as a supplement to an existing erratum.

The correction dated December 19, 2019 itself gives exp(exp(−x) cos y) instead of exp(−exp(x) cos y). At z=0 this gives e instead of 1/e. The mathematical issue is unambiguous, but it is tracked as a supplement to a known erratum and excluded from the count of new findings.

This earlier candidate or dismissed entry was outside the 806 confirmed-card publication review.

# V2-C0232 — The Datta–Singh reference names the wrong part

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.6.3

In the reference to Datta and Singh, History of Hindu Mathematics, the volume number should be 1, not 2. The year 1935 and page 76 should remain unchanged.

In the reference to Datta and Singh, History of Hindu Mathematics,
the volume number should be 1, not 2. The year 1935 and page 76
should remain unchanged.

Part I, page 76, gives Pingala's halving/subtracting and
squaring/doubling rule, with examples for 2^6 and 2^7. Part II,
page 76, discusses higher-degree equations. The 1962 combined
edition explicitly dates the first editions of Parts I and II
to 1935 and 1938, respectively.

Source scan:
https://www.dbraulibrary.org.in/RareBooks/History%20of%20Hindu%20Mathematics.pdf
PDF pages 3 (publication history), 97-98 (Part I, 76-77),
and 367 (Part II, 76), all visually checked.

The known 1990 place-of-publication correction and 2004 Pingala
corrections do not change the volume number. No correction was
found in the six archived errata layers. No later physical printing
or priority of this observation is claimed.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The Datta–Singh citation on p. 461 should point to volume I, p. 76, which discusses Pingala's halving procedure. Volume II, p. 76, concerns cubic equations. Both primary pages were inspected.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

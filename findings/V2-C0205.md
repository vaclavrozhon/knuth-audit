# V2-C0205 — The logarithm needs z^m, not z^(-m)

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.6.2 · Exercise 5

The exponent in log(1/(1-p z^(-m))) should be +m, not -m. For w=1, the Mobius formula gives a[1,1]=1 and a[m,1]=0 for m>1. The printed expression would then be 1/(1-p/z), whereas the generating function of all monic polynomials must be 1/(1-pz).

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The exponent of z in the logarithmic generating-function product on p. 680 is negative instead of positive. Setting w=1 leaves the m=1 term and would give 1/(1−p/z), rather than the required 1/(1−pz). The interchange of the counting parameters elsewhere is a valid Witt identity.

# V2-C0092 — Starting factorial iteration at 2 gives a constant sequence

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.2.4

The displayed sequence starts with 2!, (2!)!, ((2!)!)!, ... . Every term is 2, since 2! = 2, so this sequence does not have logarithmically distributed significands. For example, its proportion of leading digits equal to 1 is zero rather than log_10(2).

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The original sequence really starts with 2!, then iterates factorial. Every term equals two, so it cannot have the proposed logarithmic distribution. Retain as a starting-value typo, not a resolution of the nonconstant iteration problem.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

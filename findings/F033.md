# F033 — The cograph criterion is insufficient

**Confirmed · critical severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / SAT 343, pp.347,615

Two disjoint edges disprove the stated implication.

The claim M_G(p)>0⇒p∈R(G) for cographs is false. Two disjoint edges with p_i=3/4 give M=1/4; setting the probabilities on one edge to zero gives −1/2. There is also a connected five-vertex counterexample and an explicit construction of unavoidable events. The corrected test needs every node of the decomposition tree. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

SAT 343: a cograph consisting of two disjoint edges has Mobius polynomial (1-p1-p2)(1-p3-p4). At all p_i=3/4 it is positive, but setting p3=p4=0 makes it negative, contradicting the claimed region-membership criterion. Joining a universal vertex of probability 1/10 gives a connected counterexample too. Positivity at every node of the decomposition tree repairs the criterion.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

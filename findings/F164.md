# F164 — The tail asymptotic is missing a factor of √2

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / Mathematical Preliminaries Redux, answer68(d), p.380

Replace the equivalent by √(2/(πn)). The infinite expected stopping time and all exact probability formulas remain correct.

Answer68(d) gives P(N≥n) ~ 1/√(πn). Its exact tail formula, independently proved, instead has ratio √2 to that expression. A complete integral squeeze establishes the discrepancy without numerical approximation.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer MPR68(d): the exact preceding tail formula is binom(2k,k)/4^k with k=floor(n/2), hence its asymptotic is sqrt(2/(pi*n)), not 1/sqrt(pi*n). The integral bounds in the stored proof establish the missing factor without numerical estimation.

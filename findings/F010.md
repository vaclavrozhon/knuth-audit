# F010 — The divisor p applies to the whole difference

**Confirmed · high severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer MPR 130(e), p. 393

The joint density already fails at p = q = 1/2.

In the joint density, p must divide the entire difference z−qy. Exact counterexample: p=q=1/2, z=1, y=0. Confirmed in the PDF and the author’s PostScript. No matching correction was found in the complete 4B and Fascicle 5 errata; the physical copy has not been checked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer MPR 130(e), p. 393: the joint-density factor needs ((z-q*y)/p)^2, not (z-q*y/p)^2. This follows directly from the preceding transformed interval. At p=q=1/2,z=1,y=0 the factors disagree.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

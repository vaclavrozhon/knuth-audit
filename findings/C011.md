# C011 — The reduction factor may be stated per term

**Candidate · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.2–58, p. 558

Applying it to all parameters gives a different exponent.

The factor (3/4)^N is correct for one term; for all 2MN parameters it should be (3/4)^(MN). At M=2, N=1, there are 16→9 choices. A per-term reading may be implicit, so this remains a wording candidate. Confirmed on PDF p.558 and PostScript p.194.

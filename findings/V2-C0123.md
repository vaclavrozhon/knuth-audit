# V2-C0123 — The exact mean shift count omits initial normalization

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.5.2 · Exercise 22

The exact averaging identity is wrong, although its leading asymptotic coefficient is unchanged.

Writing inputs as2^a·u and2^b·v with odd u,v gives D(2^a·u,2^b·v)=D(u,v)+|a−b|. The answer omits the second term. For inputs1–3 the correct mean is8/9, while weighting only the odd classes gives4/9. The general missing mean tends to4/3.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The asserted reuse of the odd-input averaging formula for the shift count misses the initial difference of the two powers of two. For inputs 1,...,3, the actual average is 8/9 and the weighted formula gives 4/9. The omitted term tends to 4/3; the leading logarithmic coefficient is unaffected.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

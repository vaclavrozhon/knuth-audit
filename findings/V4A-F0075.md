# V4A-F0075 — The probability coefficients count zeros, not the stated ones

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.3–101(a), definition of N_t, printed 744

Change weight t to weight n-t, retaining the convention used by part (b).

The displayed probability formula and its definition of weight t have reversed indices.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.3-101(a), p. 744: the coefficient of p^(n-t)(1-p)^t must count satisfying inputs of weight n-t, not t. The four-variable AND otherwise gives (1-p)^4 instead of p^4. The zero-set convention in part (b) agrees with the correction.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

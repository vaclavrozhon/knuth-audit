# V4A-F0128 — Cycle eigenvectors need a corrected sine argument

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.6–106(b,c), printed 807

The spectral proof needs its sine vectors corrected; the final spectra and spanning-tree products remain valid.

The printed sine vectors sin(k theta) do not solve the cycle Laplacian equations. With the printed index ranges, sin((2k-1) theta) supplies the missing independent eigenvectors.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.6-106(b), p. 807: on the four-cycle at j=3, the proposed sin(k*theta) vector fails the Laplacian eigenvector equation. Keeping the printed index ranges requires sin((2k-1)*theta); simply doubling k leaves a zero vector at j=n/2 for even n. The eigenvalues and final tree-count products are correct.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

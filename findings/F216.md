# F216 — Watch-list cursor is not advanced on two branches

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / SAT answer261, printed598/file618: Watch-list cursor is not advanced on two branches

Advance c to the saved successor at the end of every nonconflicting iteration.

The only printed c←c-prime is in the forced-unit branch; satisfied and moved-watch branches can repeat the same clause.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

SAT261 advances its watch-list cursor only in the forced-unit case. A reachable satisfied-clause iteration instead processes the same clause repeatedly and can link it to itself. Advance after every nonconflicting iteration, including a moved watch. SAT13's loop does so.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

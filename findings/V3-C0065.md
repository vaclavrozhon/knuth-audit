# V3-C0065 — Digital-search timing charges three extra units

**Confirmed · low severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Answer 6.3-9, printed 722–723: successful digital-search timing

Use -6S and change the successful asymptotic constant from -6.17 to -9.17.

A successful root search takes 8 MIX units; 10C-3S+4 gives 11. The instruction sum is 10C-6S+4.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The instruction frequencies in answer 6.3-6 give 10C−6S+4, where C counts comparisons and S is 1 on success. A root success costs 8 units, whereas the printed formula gives 11. The successful-search constant consequently decreases by 3; unsuccessful searches are unaffected.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

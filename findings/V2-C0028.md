# V2-C0028 — The stated shift of c1 has the wrong powers

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.3.2 · Answer 30; printed 569

An auxiliary saddle-point coefficient changes by the wrong amount; the constant factor and final asymptotics are correct.

Set N=n(n+1)/2. Replacing f(z) by f(z)+N log z on z=exp(−rho+i delta t) increases c1 by N delta=alpha(n^−1/2+n^−3/2)/2. The printed expression is alpha(n^1/2−n^−1/2)/2. For n=2,m=8 these are 3sqrt(2)/8 and sqrt(2)/4. Rechecked against the first printing and all four errata layers on 6 November 2026. A recent physical printing and priority remain unverified.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The exact integrand substitution gives a linear increment N*delta, not the printed expression of a different asymptotic order.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

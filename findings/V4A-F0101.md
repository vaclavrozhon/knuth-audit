# V4A-F0101 — The involution saddle prefactor is missing sqrt(e)

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.5–51, intermediate asymptotic expansion, printed 774

Insert +1 in the numerator exponent.

The numerator needs exp((n+1+sigma)/2), not exp((n+sigma)/2). The final expanded formula is correct.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.5-51, p. 774: sigma^2+sigma=n+1 implies exp(sigma+sigma^2/2)=exp((n+1+sigma)/2). The intermediate prefactor omits sqrt(e). Its correction tends to one and cannot absorb the loss. The final expanded formula is correct.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

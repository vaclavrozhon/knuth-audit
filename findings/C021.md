# C021 — The candidate count in the minimal-clue search needs clarification

**Candidate · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–54, p. 426; candidate count for 26 clues

The reported 4,249 candidates do not match 4,244 distinct uniquely solvable subsets.

A complete certificate covers all 2^32 subsets of the 32 original clues. It gives exactly 4,244 uniquely solvable subsets with 26 clues; all other round sizes, 777 minimal subsets, and 15,441 oracle calls agree with the book. Both the PDF and author PS print 4,249. However, the text counts candidate pairs (W, X), and the historical caching policy is unspecified. Deduplicating subsets gives 4,244, while deduplicating literal pairs gives 23,588. Five repeated candidates could explain the published number, so this remains a candidate of low severity. No corresponding correction was found in the checked errata.

This earlier candidate or dismissed entry was outside the 806 confirmed-card publication review.

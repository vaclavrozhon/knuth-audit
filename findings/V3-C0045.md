# V3-C0045 — Blocks below the transition contain ones, not zeros

**Confirmed · low severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Answer 5.3.4-54, printed673: saturated lower blocks are incorrectly assigned zero

Change c_j=0 to c_j=m^2 in the stated lower-region conclusion.

The sorted 27-bit input 0 followed by 26 ones gives third-stage block counts (8,9,9), contradicting c_j=0 below the partial block.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

# V1-C0043 — The exponent-two test can overflow

**Confirmed · critical severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · §2.3.2 · exercise 16 · printed 574

A valid exponent produces the wrong power in the derivative.

For B=b^5 and v=−(B−2), both v and v−1 fit in a MIX word. Differentiating x^v on x>0 should yield v*x^(v−1). The temporary DECA 2 test overflows to negative zero, so JAZ takes the exponent-two branch and returns v*x. This example is independent of the MULT defect. CMPA CON2+1 / JE 3F / DECA 1 compares without overflow and directly forms v−1. All four refreshed errata layers leave this test unchanged. Accepted program runs cover every MIX radix 64–100; they were not repeated. Physical printing and priority remain unverified.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

A representable negative exponent overflows only in the auxiliary equality test, selecting the wrong derivative branch.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# F120 — The last cell of the 20×3 board should be j2

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / p. 82, second paragraph; final coordinate of the 20×3 board

The endpoint of the row-by-row coordinate list has its two characters reversed. The algorithm and the reported 71,191 nodes remain correct.

The paragraph defines rows 0 through 19 and columns 0 through 2, using j as the digit for 19. Its final cell is printed as 2j, which lies outside that board. The correct endpoint is j2, or (19, 2). Our PDF and the author’s older PostScript agree on the typo. All 60 coordinates and the complete eight-solution search were independently checked. No matching correction was found in the checked errata; a physical copy remains unchecked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Page 82: with row index 0 through 19 and column index 0 through 2, the last cell is j2, where j denotes 19. The printed 2j names a column outside the rectangle.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

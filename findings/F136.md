# F136 — The cylindrical octagon count is missing a digit

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / answer 7.2.2.1–142(ii), p. 454, left/right cylindrical arrangements

Case (ii) has 111,960 arrangements up to color renaming, not 11,960. Replace 2 × 5980 with 2 × 55980.

Two completed independent censuses produce exactly the same 111,960 valid arrangements with matching left and right boundaries. Every model was checked directly for all 24 distinct tile types and all shared edges; reflection pairs them into 55,980 classes. A separate 44 kB certificate and standard-library Python verifier establish a lower bound of 11,962 without any search, already exceeding the printed 11,960. The exact correction is supported by the complete censuses. The same incorrect number appears in our second-printing PDF and the author’s 2019 PostScript. No matching correction was found in six current and historical errata layers, last checked against the official files on November 2, 2026. The complete latest digital edition, a physical copy and priority have not been checked.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 142(ii): the cylinder count is 2*55,980, not 2*5,980. The stored explicit 5,981 representatives and their reflected partners already exceed the printed total, without rerunning the full census. The cut is distinguished and color renaming is factored out consistently.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V4A-F0130 — The odd-product spectrum is missing a minus sign

**Confirmed · low severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / Answer 7.2.1.6–105(h), printed 806

Insert the missing minus in the odd-product formula. The same error is present in the 5 April 2007 Fascicle 4 addendum.

The first factor d prime minus alpha prime_j times n prime [j=0] needs a minus sign before n prime [j=0]. As printed, the formula can give a negative Laplacian eigenvalue.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Answer 7.2.1.6-105(h), p. 806: insert a minus before n'*[j=0] in the first factor. For K3 and K2 the printed odd-product spectrum contains -1, impossible for a Laplacian; the repaired expression gives the triangular prism's spectrum 0,2,3,3,5,5. The April 2007 Fascicle 4 addendum introduces the same defective formula and is not a correction of it.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

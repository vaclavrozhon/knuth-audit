# V2-C0122 — The probability density is the negative derivative of the tail

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.5.2 · Exercise 34

A sign in the source translation is wrong; the main result is unchanged.

Morris uses a positive normalized density xi. The book uses its upper-tail function G(x)=integral_x^1 xi(t)dt, hence xi(x)=−G′(x). The2016 replacement identifies xi with G′ instead. Positivity and total mass rule out that sign. The full spectral proof is an external input, while this normalization identity is direct.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The 2016 erratum identifies Morris's positive normalized density xi with G'. The book defines G as a decreasing tail with G(0)=1,G(1)=0, so xi=-G'. Morris's preprint Theorem 2 and the original erratum were visually checked. The 2022 page-range correction does not fix this sign.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

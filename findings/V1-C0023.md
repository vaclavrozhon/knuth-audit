**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · §1.3.2 · Exercise 10 · Printed page 514

The displayed pair of times for the all-zero 9-by-8 matrix is 137u for Solution 1 and 524u for Solution 2. These use different conventions for the final HLT.

The displayed pair of times for the all-zero 9-by-8 matrix is 137u
for Solution 1 and 524u for Solution 2. These use different conventions
for the final HLT.

For Solution 1 the instruction costs, before HLT, are:
  initial ENT1:                          1
  row-minimum/list construction:        10 + 7*11 = 87
  LD2 and INC2:                          3
  nine column-loop iterations:         9*5 = 45
  final INC1:                            1
  total:                              137.

For Solution 2 they are:
  Phase 1:                            432
  Phase 2 initialization:                3
  eight row-loop iterations:           8*11 = 88
  total:                              523.

Thus the pair is (137,523) before HLT, or (138,524) including the
one-unit HLT overhead specified on page 138. The difference is 386u
under either common convention, not 387u as in the printed pair.
The 1999 and 2013 program corrections do not change this input's costs.

# F079 — The ZDD has one more node

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / p. 121 and answer 7.2.2.1–255, p. 481

For positive even N, the count should be V_N + 3.

The ZDD node count for even K_N is missing one: V_N+3 should replace V_N+2. The completed-solution term in the sum is −1. K2 has three nodes, K8 has 99, and K16 has 10,228. Checked by a general proof, an independent model, and DLX6 for N=2 through 20. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

DLX 255 and p.121: the sum for even N counts the completed state with contribution −1 although it produces no branch node. Its node count is V_N+3, not V_N+2. K2 has one branch and two terminals; the corrected formula also agrees with the printed 99 and 10228 examples.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# V3-C0078 — The conversion between the R functions has the wrong sign

**Confirmed · low severity.** 2nd edition · 1st printing · 1998.

Location: Printed 544, following equation (60): R(n)=R(1,n)-1

Replace the minus sign by plus; equation (60) and its expansion then agree.

Volume 1 defines R(n) as 1 plus the series R(1,n); the printed minus also contradicts equation (58).

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

Comparing the defining series gives R(n)=1+R(1,n), rather than R(1,n)−1 as printed on p. 544. Equations (58) and (60) require the plus sign; the b=1 specialization also detects the discrepancy.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

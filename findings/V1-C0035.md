# V1-C0035 — Initialization term in the amortized bound

**Candidate · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 1 · §2.2.2 · Exercise 18 · pages 248, 254 and 544

The bound needs a specified initial allocation or an initial O(n) term if all space may start in the last stack.

Candidate: with initialization (11), n≥2, M=n² and only one insertion into stack 1, Algorithm G immediately scans all n stacks. Its cost is Ω(n), but alpha_1=0 makes the printed bound O(1). If initialization (13) is required, the first overflow needs at least floor(M/n)+1≥n+1 insertions, which can pay for the first scan. The phrase “as before” on page 248 does not explicitly settle which initializations are allowed. The amortized proof after the first repacking is correct. This interpretation issue remains open after direct re-reading and checking all four errata layers on November 5, 2026.

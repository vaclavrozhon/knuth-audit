# V4A-C0004 — The runtime bound may omit repeated bit scans in P2

**Candidate · medium severity.** 1st edition · 1st printing · 2011.

Location: 4A / answer 7.1.1–30, p. 539, step P2 and the runtime paragraph

A literal execution takes quadratic time on a two-point input where the corrected bound is linear. The cube enumeration remains correct; the intended bit-scan model needs clarification.

For n≥2, take V={0,2^(n−1)}. There are two input points and exactly three contained subcubes. Every attempted mask A=2^h leaves an empty retained list, including the last mask where the sole maximal edge is emitted. P2 resets j to zero and increments it h times, for a total of n(n−1)/2 increments, whereas the known corrected bound O(mn+nC) is O(n). This remains a candidate because the prose might intend an unstated constant-time bit-scan operation. Retaining j from P3 on an empty list, with an explicit A=0 exit, preserves all outputs and admits a general proof of the advertised bound.

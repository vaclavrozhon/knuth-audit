# V2-C0097 — The multiplication routine has an unsafe memory overlap

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §4.3.1 · Exercise 30

In the sentence about overlapping storage in Algorithm M, w_(j+n) appears to need changing to w_(j+m).

In the sentence about overlapping storage in Algorithm M, w_(j+n)
appears to need changing to w_(j+m).

Algorithm M multiplies an m-place u by an n-place v, without assuming
m<=n. Take m=2, n=1, u=(10)_b, and v=1. If v_0 occupies w_1 as printed,
step M1 clears it, and M2 returns product zero instead of b.

Placing v_j at w_(j+m) avoids this: M1 clears only positions below m;
iteration j overwrites v_j only when its last use has finished, and
never overwrites a future multiplier digit. The printed index is safe
under an additional m<=n restriction, but that restriction is not given.

The original pages and all four available errata layers were checked.
The latest physical printing and priority of this correction have not
been verified.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

With m=2,n=1 and operands (10)_b and 1, the printed alias v_j at w_(j+n) puts the multiplier at w_1. M1 erases it before use, yielding zero. The original algorithm has no m<=n assumption. Placing v_j at w_(j+m) is safe because the last carry overwrites it only after its final use.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

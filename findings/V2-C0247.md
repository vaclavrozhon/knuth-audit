# V2-C0247 — The worst-case MIX time uses the wrong leading coefficient

**Confirmed · low severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · Section 4.5.2 · Printed page 340

Program A costs 19 T+6 cycles.

Program A costs 19 T+6 cycles. Fibonacci worst inputs give leading coefficient 19 ln 2/ln(phi)=27.36798…, not 26.8. With N=28, inputs 165580141 and 267914296 require 41 divisions and 785 cycles; the printed 26.8 N+19 gives 769.4. The gcd program and the Fibonacci theorem remain correct. The exact maximum is recorded in the proof; 27.4 N+19 is not proposed as an exact formula.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The 26.8N coefficient on p. 340 disagrees with the program's 19T+6 cost and its Fibonacci worst case. For N=28, inputs 165580141 and 267914296 require 41 divisions and 785 cycles, above 769.4. The asymptotic leading coefficient is 19 ln(2)/ln(phi)=27.36798…, not 26.8; the gcd program remains correct.

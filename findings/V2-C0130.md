## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The answer explicitly states a general strict bound for finite continued fractions with positive integer entries, but the one-entry list (1) has value one. Retain only as a minor overstatement of this auxiliary fact. The exercise already requires its finite last entry to exceed one, so every actual application in the uniqueness proof is valid. This is not a counterexample to uniqueness and does not require replacing that proof.

# V2-C0038 — Translation can change the number of covering planes

**Confirmed · medium severity.** 3rd edition · 1st printing · 1997.

Location: Volume 2 · §3.3.4 · Answer 15 · p. 580

The bracketed claim about the number of planes is false; their spacing and the spectral accuracy stay unchanged.

For m=4, a=1, U=(1, -1), the unshifted lattice has only x-y=0 intersecting the unit square. With c=1, two levels -1/4 and 3/4 intersect it and contain generator points. In general the number of intersecting planes is sum|u_j| minus 1 exactly when U has mixed signs and U.V0 is an integer. Occupied planes must be counted separately: m=256, a=137, U=(18, -2) gives 19 for c=1 and 20 for c=187, both full-period increments. Original answer 15 and all errata layers rechecked on 6 November 2026. No applicable correction found; priority unverified.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

The number of parallel hyperplanes meeting the unit cube can change when the increment changes, although their spacing does not. For `m=4,a=1` and normal `(1,-1)`, the unshifted lattice has one such plane and the increment-one translate has two, both occupied. The full-period example `m=256,a=137,c=1` versus `c=187` also has 19 versus 20 occupied planes for normal `(18,-2)`.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

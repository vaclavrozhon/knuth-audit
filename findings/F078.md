# F078 — The weight transformation does not preserve solution costs

**Confirmed · critical severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / 7.2.2.1–243, p. 153 and 478

Optional secondary items invalidate the constant-weight-sum assumption.

Claim 243 fails for two primary items a,b, a secondary item s, and unit weights: solutions with costs 2 and 5 have shifts 2 and 1 when r=2. Weighting only primary items fixes the claim. Confirmed in the PDF and PostScript; no matching erratum was found.

## Publication review, 12 November 2026

DLX 243: secondary items need not be covered. With primary a,b, secondary s, unit weights and options a, as, b, the two solutions have squared-weight totals 2 and 5, but squared deviations from r=2 of 2 and 1. The argument becomes correct if only primary items receive nonzero weights.

Decision: `retained`. No matching prior report was found in the checked sources; absolute priority is not established.

# C010 — “Any literal within F” may need both polarities

**Candidate · high severity.** 1st edition · 2nd printing · 2022.

Location: 4B / SAT 444 E2 and part(c), p. 360

The theorem is valid under the original paper’s explicit convention.

The words “any literal within F” may mean only literals that occur. Under that reading, a pure guard a before four binary clauses refutes SLUR=UC1. The primary paper explicitly includes both polarities; with that convention, the theorem is correct. This is a wording clarification, not a confirmed error.
